Overview

Electricity generation in Myanmar is principally governed by the Electricity Law, Pyidaungsu Hluttaw Law No. 44/2014 of 27 October 2014.

The law regulates electricity activities including construction, generation, transmission, distribution, utilization, trading, exchange and control of the electricity system.

Section 2 establishes three generation-scale categories:

  • Small-scale electrical business: up to 10 MW.
  • Medium-scale electrical business: more than 10 MW up to 30 MW.
  • Large-scale electrical business: more than 30 MW.

The scale classifications determine the applicable governmental licensing framework; they are not statutory maximum-generation limits.

Administrative Requirements

Section 11 requires a person wishing to invest in and operate an electricity activity to apply for the applicable licence.

  • Electricity generation licence.
  • Applicable construction authorization.
  • Approval by the competent licensing authority.
  • Electrical hazards safety certificate.
  • Compliance with electrical standards and inspection requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Compliance with applicable Ministry rules, regulations and directives.

For large-scale projects managed at Union level, licensing is subject to the Ministry and the applicable approval of the Union Government.

Public Information Notice

Myanmar regulates electricity generation under the Electricity Law No. 44/2014. The law classifies electricity projects as small-scale up to 10 MW, medium-scale above 10 MW to 30 MW and large-scale above 30 MW. These are regulatory classifications rather than maximum generation limits. Electricity activities require the applicable licence and electrical safety certification.
