Overview

Electricity generation in the Republic of Korea is principally regulated by the Electricity Business Act.

Article 7 establishes an authorization requirement for persons intending to operate an electricity business.

The competent permitting authority depends on the type and scale of the electricity business.

The Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy is the principal national permitting authority, while certain powers are delegated to local authorities.

Under the Enforcement Decree, authority for generation businesses with generation facilities of 3,000 kW or less is delegated to the competent metropolitan mayor or provincial governor.

The 3 MW figure is therefore primarily an allocation of permitting authority and should not be interpreted as a general exemption from electricity-business authorization.

Administrative Requirements
  • Obtain the applicable electricity-business authorization.
  • Identify the competent permitting authority according to installed generation capacity and business type.
  • Comply with financial and technical capability requirements.
  • Demonstrate that the business can be carried out according to the proposed plan.
  • Comply with environmental and land-use requirements where applicable.
  • Comply with electrical safety and technical standards.
Maximum Electrical Power
South Korea — important capacity threshold:

3 MW

Generation facilities of 3 MW or less fall within the authority delegated to the competent metropolitan mayor or provincial governor.

The 3 MW threshold concerns the allocation of administrative authority. It is not a general authorization exemption.

A larger facility is subject to the applicable national permitting framework.
