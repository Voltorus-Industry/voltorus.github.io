Overview

Electricity generation in Jordan is regulated principally under the General Electricity Law No. 10 of 2025, together with the regulations, licensing rules and decisions of the Energy and Minerals Regulatory Commission (EMRC).

The General Electricity Law was published as Law No. 10 of 2025 and forms the current legislative framework for the Jordanian electricity sector.

Electricity generation is a regulated activity and generation licences are issued by the competent regulatory authority.

Jordan's regulatory framework also contains specific licensing arrangements for renewable-energy generation facilities.

A private or self-consumption generation installation therefore requires a project-specific analysis of the applicable licence, exemption, technology, capacity and grid configuration.

Maximum Electrical Power
General statutory MW ceiling:

No single national MW ceiling applicable to every generation project has been identified in the General Electricity Law.

Jordan regulates electricity generation through licensing, technical standards and regulatory conditions rather than through a single general MW ceiling applicable to every generating facility.

The EMRC actively issues generation licences for electricity generating facilities.

Renewable-energy generation is subject to additional legislation and regulatory instruments.

The permissible project configuration and applicable licensing requirements should therefore be determined from the project's technology, capacity, location and connection configuration.
