Overview

Electricity generation in Thailand is principally regulated under the Energy Industry Act B.E. 2550 (2007).

The Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) is the principal regulatory authority for the energy industry.

The regulatory framework includes an Electricity Generation Licence.

The ERC's official licensing documentation distinguishes generation licences according to installed capacity.

The official schedule identifies the following capacity categories: not exceeding 10 MW, more than 10 MW and up to 150 MW, and more than 150 MW.

These categories are licensing classifications and fee categories; they should not be interpreted as statutory maximum generation limits.

Administrative Requirements
  • Determine whether an Electricity Generation Licence is required.
  • Determine the applicable generation-capacity category.
  • Determine whether a statutory exemption applies.
  • Apply to the Energy Regulatory Commission where required.
  • Obtain construction and site approvals where applicable.
  • Comply with electrical technical standards.
  • Comply with environmental requirements.
  • Obtain grid-connection approval where applicable.
