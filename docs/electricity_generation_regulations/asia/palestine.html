Overview

Electricity generation in Palestine is principally governed by Decree-Law No. 13 of 2009 concerning the General Electricity Law.

The law establishes the institutional and regulatory structure of the electricity sector and provides for generation companies, transmission, distribution, licensing and concessions.

The Palestinian Energy and Natural Resources Authority (PENRA) is responsible for electricity-sector policy, while the Palestinian Electricity Regulatory Council (PERC) performs regulatory and monitoring functions.

Electricity generation therefore operates within a licensing and concession framework rather than under a general private-generation exemption based solely on the absence of grid export.

Maximum Electrical Power
Palestine — no general MW generation ceiling identified in Decree-Law No. 13 of 2009

The General Electricity Law establishes the institutional, licensing and concession framework for the electricity sector but does not establish one universal maximum MW capacity applicable to all generation facilities.

The regulatory treatment depends on the nature of the generation project and the applicable licence or concession.
