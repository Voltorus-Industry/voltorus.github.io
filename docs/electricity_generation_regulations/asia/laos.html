Overview

Electricity generation in the Lao People's Democratic Republic is principally governed by the Law on Electricity and its implementing regulatory framework.

The electricity law regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution, import, export and related electricity activities.

Electricity business may be organized as general electricity business or as electricity business conducted under a concession framework.

Electricity-generation and electricity-development projects may therefore require governmental authorization, licensing and, for applicable projects, concession arrangements.

Environmental and technical requirements must also be considered during the development of electricity projects.

Administrative Requirements

The electricity framework requires electricity operators and projects to obtain the applicable government approvals.

  • Authorization for the relevant electricity activity.
  • Approval from the competent energy authorities.
  • Investment authorization where applicable.
  • Concession arrangements for projects falling within the concession framework.
  • Compliance with Lao electric-power technical standards.
  • Environmental assessment where applicable.
  • Construction and installation approvals.
Maximum Electrical Power
Laos — project-specific authorization framework

The electricity legislation does not establish a single general maximum MW capacity applicable to all electricity-generation installations.

Certain small-scale hydropower projects benefit from specific regulatory treatment. The electricity policy framework identifies an exemption from licensing approval for small-scale hydropower dams below 2 MW where the applicable conditions are satisfied.

This specific threshold should not be generalized to all electricity-generation technologies.

Public Information Notice

Electricity generation in Laos is regulated by the national electricity framework. Electricity projects may require governmental authorization, licensing, investment approval and, depending on the project, a concession. Specific treatment exists for certain small-scale hydropower projects. The exact requirements must be determined according to the technology, capacity, location and structure of the proposed project.
