Overview

Electricity generation in Lebanon is principally regulated by Law No. 462 dated 2 September 2002 concerning the regulation of the electricity sector.

The law establishes the legal framework for the regulation of electricity activities, including generation, transmission and distribution.

The law contains a dedicated chapter concerning licensing and authorization.

Electricity-generation projects must therefore be examined under the licensing and authorization provisions of Law No. 462/2002 and the applicable implementing framework.

Administrative Requirements

Law No. 462/2002 establishes a dedicated legal framework for electricity-sector licensing and authorization.

  • Authorization/licensing under the electricity legislation where applicable.
  • Compliance with electricity-sector technical requirements.
  • Construction authorization.
  • Environmental requirements where applicable.
  • Safety and technical requirements.
  • Compliance with applicable regulations and implementing decisions.
Maximum Electrical Power
Lebanon — no general electricity-generation MW ceiling identified in Law No. 462/2002

Law No. 462/2002 establishes the regulatory and licensing framework for electricity activities but does not provide a universal maximum generating capacity applicable to every generation project.

The regulatory treatment therefore depends on the project and applicable authorization framework.
