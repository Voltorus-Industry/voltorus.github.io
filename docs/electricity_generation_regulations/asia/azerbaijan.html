Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Azerbaijan and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate as an isolated electrical system without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity network.

Electricity-sector activities in Azerbaijan are regulated principally by the Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan on Electric Power.

Article 8 establishes the authorization framework for electricity-sector activities.

Article 8.1.1 specifically establishes capacity thresholds for electricity production requiring an authorization.

For hydroelectric stations and stations producing electricity from biogas or biomass gas or other fuels, electricity production above 500 kW is subject to the authorization framework.

For other electricity-generation cases, Article 8.1.1 establishes a threshold of more than 150 kW.

Because gravity-powered generation is not expressly identified as a separate generation category in the cited threshold provision, a gravity-powered facility that does not fall within the specifically listed categories should be assessed under the "other cases" threshold, namely 150 kW.

Administrative Requirements

Article 8.1 of the Electric Power Law provides that certain electricity-sector activities are carried out on the basis of authorizations issued under the law and the Law on Licences and Permits.

Electricity Generation Threshold

Article 8.1.1 establishes the following electricity-production thresholds:

  • More than 500 kW for hydroelectric power stations and electricity-generation stations using biogas or biomass gas or other fuel categories specified by the law.
  • More than 150 kW for other electricity production cases.

The competent regulator issues the relevant authorizations under Article 8.2, while the electricity-production authorization may be issued to a legal or natural person under Article 8.3.

Application to Gravity Technology

The statutory definition of renewable energy resources includes hydro, wind, solar, geothermal, biomass and marine resources among other listed sources.

Gravity-powered generation is not expressly identified as a separate category in the cited electricity-production threshold.

Unless the competent authority classifies the particular gravity technology under one of the specifically listed categories, the conservative legal classification is the "other cases" threshold of 150 kW.

Measures to Reduce Regulatory Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the external grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the external grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not participate in electricity trading.
  • Document the installation as a private self-consumption system.
  • Determine the installed electrical capacity precisely.
  • If the facility is above 150 kW, obtain a written regulatory determination and the applicable electricity-production authorization before operation.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the regulator concerning the legal classification of the gravity-powered technology.
  • Verify construction, environmental, technical and electrical-safety requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Azerbaijan — threshold applicable to other electricity-generation cases:

150 kW

Article 8.1.1 provides that electricity production above 150 kW in cases other than the specifically identified 500 kW categories is subject to authorization.

This threshold is not a general prohibition on electricity generation above 150 kW.

Rather, it is the statutory threshold at which the electricity-production authorization requirement applies to the relevant category.

A gravity-powered installation should therefore be treated conservatively as falling within the "other cases" category unless the competent Azerbaijani regulator determines that the technology belongs to a different statutory category.

For a completely isolated installation, grid connection, electricity import and electricity export requirements are not applicable where no connection or market activity exists, but this does not eliminate a production authorization where Article 8 requires one.

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. Azerbaijan's Electric Power Law establishes specific authorization thresholds for electricity production. Article 8.1.1 provides a threshold of more than 500 kW for specified hydro, biogas, biomass-gas and fuel categories and more than 150 kW for other cases. Gravity-powered generation is not expressly identified as a separate category in the cited provision. Accordingly, unless the competent regulator determines otherwise, a gravity-powered installation should be assessed under the 150 kW threshold applicable to other cases.
