Overview

Electricity generation in Kazakhstan is principally governed by the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Electric Power Industry" dated 9 July 2004 No. 588 and by the legislation governing permits and notifications.

The Electricity Law regulates public relations arising in the production, transmission and consumption of electric power.

Certain activities in the electric power industry are subject to licensing in accordance with Kazakhstan's legislation on permits and notifications.

The law also provides a regulatory framework for generating installations and certification of their electrical capacity.

A private electricity-generation project must therefore be assessed according to the type of generation, installed capacity, connection configuration, intended use of electricity and the applicable permitting and technical requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Article 7-1 of the Electricity Law provides that certain activities in the electric power industry are subject to licensing under Kazakhstan's legislation on permits and notifications.

The licensing requirement should therefore be determined according to the exact activity performed by the project operator.

Article 15-2 establishes certification of the electrical capacity of generating installations.

During certification, the system operator determines the electrical capacity that the generating organization technically possesses and is capable of generating.

  • Verification of the generation activity.
  • Determination of the installed and certified electrical capacity.
  • Compliance with applicable technical requirements.
  • Compliance with electricity-sector licensing requirements where applicable.
  • Compliance with construction and safety rules.
  • Compliance with grid-connection requirements where a connection exists.
  • Environmental approvals where required by the nature and location of the project.
Maximum Electrical Power
Kazakhstan — no single general MW ceiling identified in the Electricity Law

The Electricity Law does not establish one general maximum MW capacity applicable to every private electricity-generation installation.

Instead, Kazakhstan regulates electricity generation through the electricity-sector framework, permits and notifications, technical requirements and, where applicable, certification of generating capacity.

The exact regulatory treatment therefore depends on the characteristics of the generating installation and the activity carried out by its operator.

Project-specific capacity and authorization requirements

The applicable regulatory requirements must be determined from the generation activity, generating installation, electrical capacity and operating configuration.

The absence of a single general MW ceiling should not be interpreted as an exemption from licensing, certification, construction, environmental, technical or safety requirements.

Public Information Notice

Electricity generation in Kazakhstan is regulated by the Law "On Electric Power Industry" and related legislation. Certain activities are subject to licensing, while generating installations are subject to the applicable technical and capacity-certification framework. The permissible capacity and authorization requirements must therefore be established from the characteristics and operating configuration of the proposed generating installation.
