Overview

Electricity generation in Malaysia is regulated by the Electricity Supply Act 1990 (Act 447), together with regulations, licences and requirements administered by the Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga).

The Act regulates the electricity supply industry, including licensing of electrical installations and electricity-related activities.

The Energy Commission expressly provides licensing procedures for public electricity-generation installations and private installations.

The current licensing framework distinguishes, among other categories, private installations with capacity of 5 MW and above and private installations below 5 MW.

Consequently, the 5 MW figure is an important administrative licensing threshold for private installations, rather than a general maximum generating capacity.

Maximum Electrical Power
Malaysia — 5 MW is a licensing category threshold, not a maximum generation limit

The Energy Commission's licensing framework explicitly provides separate application categories for private installations with capacity of 5 MW and above and private installations below 5 MW.

Therefore, 5 MW should not be presented as the maximum permitted generation capacity.

Public Information Notice

Electricity generation and electrical installations in Malaysia are regulated principally by the Electricity Supply Act 1990 and the Energy Commission. The current licensing framework distinguishes private installations below 5 MW from private installations of 5 MW and above. The 5 MW figure is a licensing classification threshold and should not be interpreted as a maximum generating capacity.
