Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Bangladesh and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate as an isolated electrical system without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to an external public electricity network.

Electricity generation and electricity-sector activities in Bangladesh are regulated principally through the Electricity Act, 2018 and the Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission Act, 2003.

The Electricity Act, 2018 defines a licensee as a person licensed under the Commission law for electricity generation, transmission, distribution or supply.

The Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission Act, 2003 expressly provides for licences for power generation.

The Commission may also establish exemptions from licensing subject to specified conditions under the licensing framework.

No specific statutory MW threshold applicable specifically to gravity-powered electricity generation has been identified in the cited Electricity Act and BERC licensing provisions. The regulatory treatment of a completely isolated self-consumption facility therefore depends on the applicable licence category, any Commission exemption, the installed capacity and the technical characteristics of the installation.

Administrative Requirements

The Electricity Act, 2018 recognizes electricity generation as a regulated electricity-sector activity and defines a licensee by reference to the Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission licensing framework.

Section 28 of the Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission Act, 2003 provides for licences for power generation, transmission, distribution and marketing, supply and storage activities.

Generation Licence

Power generation is expressly included among the activities for which the Commission may issue a licence.

Section 29 provides that the Commission may make regulations allowing exemptions from the requirement of a licence subject to specified conditions.

Therefore, a completely isolated self-consumption project should not be assumed automatically to require or not require a generation licence solely because it is off-grid. The relevant BERC regulations and the project's characteristics must be checked.

  • Compliance with Electricity Act, 2018.

  • Construction and installation approvals.
  • Environmental requirements where applicable.
  • Fire and occupational safety requirements.
  • Any technical requirements imposed by the competent authority.
Measures to Reduce Regulatory Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not distribute electricity outside the controlled premises.
  • Document the installation as an internal self-consumption generating facility.
  • Determine the installed electrical capacity precisely.
  • Obtain a written determination from BERC concerning whether the installation requires a generation licence or qualifies for an applicable exemption.
  • Verify the applicable Electricity Rules and BERC regulations.
  • Obtain all applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety and inspection approvals.

No specific MW threshold identified in the cited generation licensing provisions

The Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission Act, 2003 provides for power-generation licences and permits the Commission to establish licensing exemptions subject to specified conditions.

The Electricity Act, 2018 does not establish a gravity-specific electricity-generation capacity threshold in the provisions reviewed.

The applicable regulatory treatment therefore depends upon the generation licence framework, BERC regulations, any applicable exemption and the characteristics of the generating installation.

A completely isolated self-consumption facility should nevertheless be documented separately from a facility supplying electricity to customers or participating in the electricity market.

The appropriate licensing or exemption status should be confirmed with BERC before construction or operation, particularly for a large generating installation.
