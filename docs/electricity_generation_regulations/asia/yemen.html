Overview

Electricity generation in Yemen is principally governed by Electricity Law No. 1 of 2009.

The Law applies to electricity generation, transmission, distribution and wholesale supply.

The Ministry of Electricity and Energy is responsible for issuing licences for generation and other electricity activities.

The Law contains a specific provision for private generation facilities intended to satisfy the electricity requirements of the owner's own needs, including a specific licensing exemption up to 3 MW.

Licensing Requirements

Article 15 provides that no person may establish or operate generation, transmission, distribution or wholesale-supply activities without obtaining the required licence.

Article 19(a) permits private generation stations to satisfy the electricity needs of a person, project or facility, subject to the safety rules and conditions established by the regulations.

Article 19(b):

A person generating electricity with a capacity not exceeding 3 MW for use in that person's own facilities and buildings is exempt from obtaining a generation licence, provided that the applicable safety rules, principles and conditions are respected.
Administrative Requirements
  • Compliance with Electricity Law No. 1 of 2009.
  • Compliance with electrical safety requirements.
  • Environmental protection requirements.
  • Construction and infrastructure approvals.
  • Additional requirements established by the implementing regulations.
Maximum Electrical Power
Yemen — private self-consumption licensing exemption:

Up to 3 MW

Article 19(b) exempts electricity generation of capacity not exceeding 3 MW when the electricity is used in the generator's own facilities and buildings, subject to applicable safety requirements.

The 3 MW figure is specifically a licensing exemption under Article 19(b), not a general authorization to construct any generation project without other approvals.

Projects above this threshold should be assessed under the ordinary generation licensing provisions.
