Overview

Electricity generation in Japan is principally governed by the Electricity Business Act and its implementing regulations.

The regulatory framework is administered principally by the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the Agency for Natural Resources and Energy.

Under the current Electricity Business Act, a person intending to conduct an electricity generation business falling within the statutory notification regime must notify the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry.

The notification includes information concerning the generation facilities, including their site, motive power, frequency and output capacity.

A purely private installation used exclusively behind the meter must nevertheless be assessed against the applicable Electricity Business Act classification, technical safety rules and electrical-facility requirements.

Administrative Requirements
Generation Notification

Article 27-27 of the Electricity Business Act provides for notification to the Minister of Economy, Trade and Industry for electricity generation business meeting the statutory criteria.

The notification includes the generation site, motive power, frequency, output capacity and scheduled commencement date.

Other Requirements
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Technical standards for electrical facilities.
  • Construction and planning requirements.
  • Environmental requirements where applicable.
  • Grid connection requirements where applicable.
  • Fire and occupational safety requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
General national generation ceiling:

No single MW ceiling applicable to every generation installation has been identified.

Japanese regulation does not operate on the basis of one general national MW ceiling for all electricity-generation installations.

Instead, the applicable legal obligations depend on the classification of the generation business, the electrical facilities, output capacity and relationship with the electricity system.

Article 27-27 specifically requires the output capacity of the generation facilities to be included in the relevant notification.

The project capacity should therefore be stated precisely and assessed against the current METI electricity-generation notification and technical-safety requirements.
