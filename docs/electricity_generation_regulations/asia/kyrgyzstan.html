Overview

Electricity generation in Kyrgyzstan is regulated by the legislation governing the electricity sector and by the Law of the Kyrgyz Republic No. 195 of 19 October 2013 on the Licensing and Permitting System.

Production, transmission, distribution, sale, export and import of electricity are generally activities requiring a licence.

However, the licensing legislation expressly excludes electricity production from any energy source for own use where the capacity does not exceed 1,000 kW.

Electricity generated from renewable energy sources is also expressly excluded from the licensing requirement stated in the relevant provision.

Consequently, the 1,000 kW threshold is particularly relevant to private electricity generation intended exclusively for own use.

Administrative Requirements

The official Kyrgyz licensing portal identifies a licence for electricity production as a regulated authorization.

The licensing procedure is administered by the authority responsible for regulation of the fuel-and-energy complex.

  • Electricity generation generally requires licensing unless an applicable statutory exemption applies.
  • Own-use generation up to 1,000 kW is excluded from the licensing requirement.
  • Renewable electricity generation is excluded from the licensing requirement identified in Article 15.
  • Construction and technical requirements remain applicable.
  • Environmental requirements may apply according to the project.
Maximum Electrical Power
Kyrgyzstan — own-consumption licensing threshold: 1,000 kW

The licensing legislation expressly excludes electricity production from any energy source for own use at a capacity of up to 1,000 kW.

This threshold concerns the licensing requirement. It should not automatically be interpreted as a universal construction or technical maximum for electricity generation.

Public Information Notice

Kyrgyzstan's licensing legislation expressly provides an exemption from licensing for electricity production from any energy source for own use at capacity up to 1,000 kW. Renewable electricity production is also expressly excluded from the licensing requirement stated in the relevant provision. Other legal, technical, construction and environmental requirements may nevertheless apply.
