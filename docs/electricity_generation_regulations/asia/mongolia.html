Overview

Electricity generation in Mongolia is principally governed by the Law of Mongolia on Energy.

Article 12.1 of the Energy Law establishes electricity generation as an activity normally conducted on the basis of a licence issued by the competent authority.

The Energy Law contains a specific statutory exemption for certain small energy sources intended solely for own use.

Under Article 12.5, a licence is not required for the construction and operation of energy sources with a capacity of up to 1.5 MW, together with their transmission and distribution lines, where the installation is designated solely for own use and does not adversely affect the environment or normal living conditions of people.

Article 13.2.1 also expressly excludes electricity generated solely for own use from the requirement for regulatory approval of electricity and heat selling prices and conditions.

Administrative Requirements

Article 21 establishes the application framework for energy licences.

  • Feasibility study where a licence is required.
  • Survey of energy resources.
  • Type, quantity and quality of energy to be generated.
  • Main technical specifications of the equipment.
  • Energy generation, supply and consumption balance.
  • Environmental impact assessment.
  • Environmental protection action plan.
  • Financial capability and financing information.
  • Technical personnel qualifications and experience.

For installations falling within Article 12.5, the statutory generation licence exemption does not remove other applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety or technical requirements.

Maximum Electrical Power
Mongolia — own-use licence exemption: 1.5 MW

Article 12.5 provides that a licence is not required for construction and operation of energy sources with capacity up to 1.5 MW when they are designated solely for own use and satisfy the environmental and living-condition requirements stated in the law.

The 1.5 MW threshold is therefore a statutory licensing exemption for the specified own-use configuration.

It should not be interpreted as a universal technical maximum for every electricity-generation project in Mongolia.

Public Information Notice

Mongolia's Energy Law generally regulates electricity generation through a licensing framework. Article 12.5 creates a specific exemption from licensing for energy sources with capacity up to 1.5 MW that are designated solely for own use and do not adversely affect the environment or normal living conditions. Other construction, environmental, technical and safety requirements may remain applicable.
