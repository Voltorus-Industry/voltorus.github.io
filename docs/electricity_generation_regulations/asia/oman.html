Overview

Electricity generation in Oman is principally governed by the Electricity Sector Law promulgated by Royal Decree 78/2004 and by regulations and policies issued under that framework.

The electricity sector is regulated by the Authority for Public Services Regulation.

Oman has established a specific regulatory framework for electricity self-generation, including generation from renewable energy sources for self-supply.

Under the Electricity Self-Generation, Direct Sales and Wheeling Policy, a self-generator must obtain a generation licence or an exemption from licensing from the Authority.

The policy further provides that where the self- generation capacity does not exceed 25 MW, the Authority is not required to coordinate with the Ministry of Energy and Minerals before issuing the licence or exemption.

Maximum Electrical Power
Oman — 25 MW is an administrative coordination threshold, not a generation-capacity ceiling

The self-generation policy states that the Authority must coordinate with the Ministry of Energy and Minerals when issuing a generation licence or exemption, except where the generation capacity does not exceed 25 MW.

Therefore, 25 MW should not be interpreted as the maximum permitted self-generation capacity.

The project must obtain the applicable generation licence or exemption and comply with the electricity sector's technical, environmental and operational requirements.

Public Information Notice

Oman regulates electricity generation under the Electricity Sector Law and the regulations and policies issued under it. Renewable self-generation for self-supply is permitted subject to obtaining a generation licence or applicable exemption. The 25 MW threshold in the self-generation policy concerns Ministry coordination and should not be interpreted as a general maximum generation capacity.
