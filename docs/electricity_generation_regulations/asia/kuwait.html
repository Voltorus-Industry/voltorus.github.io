Overview

Electricity generation projects in Kuwait are governed principally by the legal framework applicable to electrical power projects, including Law No. 39 of 2010 concerning the incorporation of Kuwaiti joint-stock companies to undertake the construction and implementation of electrical power and water desalination plants.

Law No. 39 of 2010 establishes a specific framework for electricity-power and water-desalination projects, particularly projects implemented through the Independent Water and Power Producer framework.

Large electricity-generation projects are therefore subject to a project-specific governmental and institutional authorization framework.

A private electricity-generation project cannot be assumed to be outside the regulatory framework merely because the electricity is intended for internal use. The precise legal treatment depends on the project structure, technology, capacity, ownership and intended electricity use.

Administrative Requirements
  • Identification of the legal structure of the electricity-generation project.
  • Determination of whether the project falls within the scope of Law No. 39 of 2010.
  • Governmental authorization applicable to the project.
  • Technical and electrical approvals.
  • Environmental requirements where applicable.
  • Construction and land-use approvals.
  • Applicable requirements imposed by the competent governmental authorities.
Maximum Electrical Power
Kuwait — no general private-generation MW ceiling identified from Law No. 39 of 2010

Law No. 39 of 2010 regulates electricity-power and desalination projects through a specific project and institutional framework rather than establishing a universal maximum MW figure for every electricity installation.

The applicable authorization requirements therefore depend on the project structure and applicable legislation.
