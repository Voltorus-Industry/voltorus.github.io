Overview

Electricity generation in Indonesia is principally governed by Law No. 30 of 2009 concerning Electricity, together with implementing government regulations and regulations issued by the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources.

Indonesian law distinguishes electricity supply for public interest from electricity supply for own interest.

An installation operated exclusively for the electricity requirements of its owner is treated differently from an electricity business supplying electricity to the public.

The Indonesian regulatory system provides an IUPTL for Kepentingan Sendiri for electricity supply for own interest when the applicable capacity and regulatory conditions are met.

Current administrative guidance identifies a threshold of more than 500 kW for an IUPTL for own-interest installation within one electrical installation system, while installations at or below 500 kW are subject to the applicable reporting regime.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity supply for own interest is subject to the Indonesian electricity licensing framework.

IUPTL for Own Interest

The Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources describes the IUPTL for own interest as the authorization for electricity supply for the applicant's own interest.

Technical documentation includes information such as the installation location, single-line diagram, type and capacity of the electricity installation, construction schedule and operating schedule.

Maximum Electrical Power
Own-interest generation licensing threshold:

More than 500 kW in one electrical installation system

Under the current Indonesian licensing framework, electricity supply for own interest exceeding 500 kW in one electrical installation system requires the applicable IUPTL for own interest.

Installations at or below 500 kW may be subject to reporting rather than the same IUPTL licensing procedure, depending on the applicable current regulations.

This threshold concerns the licensing treatment of electricity supply for own interest and should not be interpreted as a general technical limit on the physical size of all electricity-generation installations in Indonesia.
