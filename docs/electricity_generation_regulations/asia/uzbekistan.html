Overview

Electricity generation in Uzbekistan is principally governed by the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. ZRU-939 "On Electric Power Industry" dated 7 August 2024.

Article 24 establishes that electricity generation is generally carried out on the basis of a licence issued by the Energy Market Regulator.

The Law expressly provides an exemption where electricity is generated for own needs at a power plant with an installed capacity below 5 MW.

This 5 MW threshold is therefore directly relevant to a private self-consumption generation project.

Licensing Requirements

Article 48 identifies electricity generation as a licensed activity in the electric-power sector.

Article 24 provides the specific own-use exemption for a power plant with installed capacity below 5 MW.

The exemption applies to electricity production for the producer's own needs.

  • Below 5 MW: own-use generation is exempt from the electricity-generation licence under Article 24.
  • 5 MW and above: the general licensing regime applies unless another statutory exemption is applicable.
  • Separate requirements may apply to construction, environmental protection, land and electrical safety.
Maximum Electrical Power
Uzbekistan — own-use licensing threshold:

Below 5 MW installed capacity

Electricity generated for own needs at a power plant below 5 MW does not require an electricity-generation licence under Article 24.

This is a licensing exemption, not a general exemption from construction, environmental, safety or other applicable legislation.

A project at or above 5 MW should be assessed under the ordinary electricity-generation licensing regime.
