Overview

Electricity generation in Turkmenistan is governed by the national legislation regulating the electric power sector.

The Law "On Electric Energy" establishes the legal, economic and organizational basis for activities in the electric-energy sector.

The legislation identifies state regulation, reliability, technological unity and environmental safety as fundamental principles of the sector.

Licensing requirements are additionally governed by Law No. 205-VI of 2019 on Licensing of Certain Types of Activities.

Administrative Requirements

The electric-power sector is subject to state regulation and supervision.

Activities in the electric-power sector are identified as licensable activities under the licensing legislation.

  • Electricity-sector authorization.
  • Technical and safety requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Construction requirements.
  • Requirements imposed by the competent electricity authorities.

No general MW threshold specifically applicable to private isolated electricity generation was identified in the legislation reviewed.

The existence of licensing requirements means that the absence of a specific MW threshold should not be interpreted as an exemption.

The applicable authorization must be determined from the technology, ownership, operating mode and purpose of the proposed generation facility.
