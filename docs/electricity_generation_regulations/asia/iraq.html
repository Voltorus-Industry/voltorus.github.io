Overview

Electricity generation in Iraq operates within a government-controlled electricity-sector framework administered principally through the Ministry of Electricity and the applicable electricity-sector regulatory and technical instruments.

Ministry of Electricity Law No. 53 of 2017 establishes the institutional legal framework for the Ministry of Electricity.

Iraq's electricity-sector framework also recognizes independent power producers, self-generation connected to the transmission system and embedded generation connected to distribution networks.

A private electricity-generation project should therefore be assessed according to whether it is connected to the national electricity system, whether electricity is sold, and whether it qualifies as self-generation.

A genuinely isolated installation should nevertheless be checked against applicable electricity, construction, environmental, safety and investment requirements before construction or operation.

Administrative Requirements
  • Ministry of Electricity requirements.
  • Applicable electricity-sector licence requirements.
  • Grid Code requirements where connected to the system.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Environmental approvals where applicable.
  • Construction and land approvals.
  • Investment and company-law requirements for private projects.
  • Additional requirements imposed by the competent authority for the particular project.
Maximum Electrical Power
General national MW threshold:

No single general MW threshold applicable to every electricity-generation installation has been identified.

The applicable regulatory requirements depend on the generation category, connection to the electricity system, purpose of the generation and applicable licence.

The Iraq Grid Code distinguishes, among others, between Ministry-owned generating stations, licensed independent power producers, principal consumers with self-generation connected to the transmission network and embedded generation connected to distribution networks.

Project capacity should therefore be established from the engineering design and assessed under the applicable Iraqi regulatory and technical framework.
