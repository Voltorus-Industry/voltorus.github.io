Overview

Electricity generation in Türkiye is principally governed by Electricity Market Law No. 6446 and the secondary legislation issued by the Energy Market Regulatory Authority (EPDK).

As a general rule, electricity-market generation activities require the applicable authorization, including the pre-license and production-license process.

Turkish legislation also establishes a separate framework for electricity generation activities that are exempt from the ordinary licensing requirement.

A genuinely isolated self-consumption installation must therefore be classified under the applicable provisions of Law No. 6446 and the Electricity Market Unlicensed Electricity Generation Regulation.

Licensing Requirements

EPDK states that, for electricity-market generation, a legal entity generally obtains a pre-license and subsequently a production license after completing the required obligations.

Law No. 6446 also provides exemptions from the ordinary licensing and company-establishment requirements for certain generation activities.

The unlicensed-generation regime is governed by the Electricity Market Unlicensed Electricity Generation Regulation.

  • Determine whether the project qualifies for unlicensed generation.
  • Determine the applicable connection regime.
  • Obtain required technical approvals.
  • Comply with distribution or transmission requirements where applicable.
  • Obtain environmental and construction approvals where applicable.

The licensing regime depends on the applicable generation category and exemption.

The historical 1 MW threshold commonly associated with certain unlicensed renewable generation rules should not automatically be treated as a universal ceiling for every generation technology or project configuration.

The current unlicensed-generation legislation must be examined for the specific technology, connection arrangement and project date.

For a private isolated facility, the project should be submitted for a determination of the applicable licensing and authorization regime before construction.
