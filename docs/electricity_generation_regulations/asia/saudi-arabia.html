Overview

Electricity activities in Saudi Arabia are regulated principally under the Electricity Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/44 dated 16/05/1442 AH.

The Saudi Electricity Regulatory Authority (SERA) regulates electricity activities and administers the applicable licensing, authorization and exemption framework.

Electricity generation is an activity subject to regulatory control.

Importantly, the regulatory framework provides for an exemption mechanism for certain generation stations that are not connected to a transmission or distribution network and whose entire output is consumed exclusively by the station owner.

This exemption is highly relevant to a genuinely isolated self-consumption project.

Administrative Requirements
  • Determine whether the project requires a generation licence, authorization or exemption.
  • Apply to SERA where the applicable regulatory framework requires an authorization or exemption.
  • Maintain the technical and legal conditions of the authorization or exemption.
  • Comply with electrical safety requirements.
  • Comply with environmental requirements.
  • Comply with construction and civil-defense requirements where applicable.
  • Obtain any additional permits required for the project site and technology.

Saudi Arabia — isolated self-consumption:

The Electricity Law framework provides an exemption mechanism for a generation station that is not connected to a transmission or distribution network and whose products are exclusively consumed by the station owner.

The exemption is based on the characteristics of the installation rather than a simple universal MW figure.

The project capacity must nevertheless be declared and technically assessed, and SERA confirmation should be obtained before construction.
