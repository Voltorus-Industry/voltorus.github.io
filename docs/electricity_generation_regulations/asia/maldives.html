Overview

Electricity generation in the Maldives is regulated through the national electricity-sector framework administered by the Maldives Energy Authority.

The Generation, Distribution and Supply Licensing Regulation (2015/R-143) establishes a licensing framework for electricity-sector operators.

The regulation applies to parties involved in producing, transmitting, distributing and supplying electricity.

The regulatory framework provides different licence categories, including Service Provider Licences, Independent Power Producer Licences and Transmission Licences.

The precise licence requirement therefore depends on whether the operator is generating electricity for supply to a service provider, operating a service provider network or carrying out another regulated electricity activity.

Administrative Requirements
  • Registration/licensing of regulated electricity service operators.
  • Service Provider Licence where the operator provides electricity services.
  • Independent Power Producer Licence where the project qualifies as an IPP.
  • Transmission Licence where applicable.
  • Compliance with technical and safety requirements.
  • Installation and engineering requirements.
  • Environmental requirements where applicable.
Maximum Electrical Power
Maldives — no single general MW maximum identified in the Generation, Distribution and Supply Licensing Regulation

The regulation establishes licence categories and regulatory requirements rather than a universal electricity-generation MW ceiling applicable to every installation.

The Independent Power Producer framework is principally designed for generators supplying electricity to service providers under the applicable contractual framework.

A purely private installation intended exclusively for internal consumption must therefore be assessed against the specific licensing provisions and any applicable exemptions.

Public Information Notice

Electricity generation and electricity services in the Maldives are regulated by the Maldives Energy Authority. The Generation, Distribution and Supply Licensing Regulation (2015/R-143) establishes licensing categories for electricity service providers, independent power producers and transmission operators. The applicable legal requirements depend on the nature, purpose and configuration of the proposed installation.
