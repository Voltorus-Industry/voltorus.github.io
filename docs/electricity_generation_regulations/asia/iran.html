Overview

Electricity generation in Iran is principally regulated through the national electricity legislation and the regulatory framework administered by the Ministry of Energy and its electricity sector entities.

Iranian legislation provides for governmental authorization of electricity generation, transmission and distribution installations in the circumstances covered by the applicable electricity laws.

The Electricity Organization Law contains provisions requiring authorization for the development, construction and operation of certain electricity generation, transmission and distribution facilities for agricultural, industrial and commercial uses.

Private electricity production is therefore not treated as completely outside the electricity regulatory framework merely because the electricity is intended for the operator's own use.

Additional rules apply to renewable generation, including specific governmental policies and support mechanisms.

Administrative Requirements

The applicable authorization depends on the nature of the electricity facility, its use, technology, location and relationship with the electricity system.

  • Authorization from the competent electricity authority where required.
  • Technical approval for electricity facilities.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Construction and land-use approvals.
  • Additional requirements applicable to renewable electricity facilities.
  • Grid-connection requirements where applicable.
Maximum Electrical Power
General national MW ceiling for private generation:

No single general generation-capacity ceiling has been identified in the principal electricity legislation that can be applied to every private generation project.

Iranian electricity regulation is based principally on authorization, technical requirements, the nature of the installation and the applicable electricity-sector rules.

A project-specific assessment is therefore required before determining the applicable authorization pathway.

Renewable generation may also be subject to specific procurement and support regulations.
