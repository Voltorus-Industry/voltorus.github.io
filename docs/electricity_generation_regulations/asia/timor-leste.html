Overview

Electricity generation in Timor-Leste is governed by the national electricity-sector legislation, principally Decree-Law No. 13/2003 establishing the bases of the National Electricity System, together with subsequent institutional legislation.

Decree-Law No. 13/2003 establishes the legal framework applicable to electricity production, transmission and distribution activities.

The electricity sector is subject to public regulation and the competent national authorities responsible for electricity-sector policy and regulation.

The legal treatment of an isolated private generation facility must therefore be determined from the applicable electricity legislation, the project's purpose, its connection status and the competent authority's authorization requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Decree-Law No. 13/2003 provides the basic legal framework for electricity production, transmission and distribution.

The legislation also establishes the framework for public electricity services, infrastructure and concessions.

A private generation project must therefore be examined against the applicable authorization, concession, construction, safety and environmental requirements.

  • Electricity-sector authorization requirements.
  • Construction and infrastructure requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Land and construction approvals.
  • Requirements imposed by the competent electricity authority.
Self-Consumption and Off-Grid Operation

The absence of a public-grid connection does not by itself establish a general statutory exemption from electricity-sector regulation.

The project's legal status must be determined from the applicable electricity legislation and the characteristics of the installation.

  • No intended connection to a public electricity network.
  • No intended electricity export.
  • Electricity consumed exclusively by the operator.
  • Verify authorization requirements before construction.
  • Verify environmental and construction requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Timor-Leste — statutory capacity threshold:

No specific national MW threshold identified in the principal electricity legislation reviewed for a private isolated generation facility.

The principal legislation reviewed does not establish a general MW ceiling specifically applicable to private off-grid electricity generation.

Project capacity should therefore be established from the engineering design and submitted to the competent authorities for determination of the applicable authorization requirements.
