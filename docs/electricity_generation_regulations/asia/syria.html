Overview

Electricity generation in the Syrian Arab Republic is subject to Syrian electricity, energy, infrastructure, construction and environmental legislation.

Electricity generation is principally associated with the national electricity and energy authorities.

Private electricity-generation projects must be assessed according to their legal form, purpose, installed capacity, location and relationship with the public electricity system.

A distinction must be made between generation for internal private consumption and generation intended for public supply or electricity-market activities.

No sufficiently reliable current official source was identified establishing a single nationwide MW threshold that can safely be presented as the general maximum generation capacity for a private isolated installation.

  • Verify construction authorization.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify electrical and fire-safety requirements.
  • Verify land-use and site requirements.
