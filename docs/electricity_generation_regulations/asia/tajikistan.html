Overview

Energy activities in Tajikistan are principally regulated by the Law of the Republic of Tajikistan "On Energy".

The law was adopted on 29 November 2000 as Law No. 33 and has subsequently been amended.

The law establishes the organizational and legal principles for economic activity in the energy sector.

Electricity-generation projects are subject to the applicable energy, licensing, construction, environmental and technical requirements.

No single nationwide MW threshold applicable to every private isolated electricity-generation installation has been identified in the principal Energy Law provisions reviewed.

Administrative Requirements
  • Verify the applicable energy-sector authorization.
  • Verify licensing requirements under Tajik licensing legislation.
  • Obtain applicable construction approvals.
  • Comply with electrical safety requirements.
  • Comply with environmental requirements.
  • Obtain grid approvals if the facility is connected to the electricity system.
  • Verify additional governmental approvals according to project capacity and location.
