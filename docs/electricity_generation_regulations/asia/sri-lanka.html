Overview

Electricity generation in Sri Lanka is regulated principally by the Sri Lanka Electricity Act, No. 20 of 2009.

The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) is the principal regulatory authority for the electricity industry.

The Act establishes a licensing regime covering generation, transmission, distribution and supply of electricity.

A person may not generate electricity unless authorized by a generation licence or covered by an applicable statutory exemption.

The Act specifically provides for a generation licence authorizing a person to generate electricity from an identified generation plant.

Administrative Requirements
  • Determine whether the generation facility requires a generation licence.
  • Determine whether a statutory exemption applies.
  • Apply to PUCSL where a licence is required.
  • Identify the generation plant in the licence application.
  • Comply with licence conditions.
  • Comply with electrical and technical standards.
  • Comply with environmental and construction requirements.
