Overview

Electricity generation in Vietnam is governed by the Electricity Law No. 61/2024/QH15 and implementing regulations.

The Electricity Law entered into force on 1 February 2025.

Article 33 establishes exemptions from electricity activity licences for specified categories of electricity generation.

Decree No. 61/2025/ND-CP specifies the capacity thresholds applicable to electricity-generation licensing exemptions.

Electricity Generation Licensing

Article 33 of the Electricity Law establishes electricity-activity licensing exemptions.

Decree No. 61/2025/ND-CP specifies the capacity exemption applicable to self-use electricity generation.

Self-use and not connected to the national power system:

No capacity limit applies to the electricity-generation licensing exemption under Article 21 of Decree No. 61/2025/ND-CP.
Self-use and connected to the national power system:

Installed capacity below 30 MW is exempt from the electricity-operation licence for generation.
Administrative Requirements
  • Compliance with the Electricity Law.
  • Compliance with investment legislation.
  • Construction requirements.
  • Land-use requirements.
  • Environmental-protection requirements.
  • Electrical and fire-safety requirements.
  • Grid-connection requirements where applicable.
Maximum Electrical Power
Vietnam — isolated self-consumption:

No capacity limit for the electricity-generation licensing exemption where the self-use generation facility is not connected to the national power system.

For a self-production/self-consumption installation connected to the national power system, the electricity-generation licensing exemption applies below 30 MW installed capacity.

These are licensing rules and do not eliminate other requirements relating to investment, construction, land, environment, safety or grid operation.
