Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Mozambique and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The facility is intended to operate independently from the national electricity network.

Mozambique's current electricity framework is based on Law No. 12/2022 of 11 July 2022 on Electricity, which repealed Law No. 21/97.

The law applies to electricity production, storage, transport, distribution, commercialization and consumption.

The regulatory framework is supplemented by concession regulations and rules administered by the relevant electricity authorities.

Administrative Requirements

Law No. 12/2022 establishes the legal framework for electricity supply activities, including production, storage and consumption.

The law provides a concession-based framework for electricity activities subject to State authorization.

Article 5 assigns the Council of Ministers authority concerning electricity-supply projects with an installed capacity of 100 MW or more and establishes the framework for determining competences for lower capacities.

Important: The 100 MW figure is a governmental competence threshold for electricity-supply projects. It is not a general licence-free threshold for an isolated private self-consumption installation.
  • Electricity-generation authorization requirements must be checked for the specific project.
  • Construction permits may apply.
  • Environmental authorization may apply.
  • Electrical and occupational-safety requirements apply.
  • Land-use and site-specific requirements may apply.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the national electricity network.
  • Use electricity solely for the owner's internal consumption.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not export electricity to the public network.
  • Obtain a written determination from the competent Mozambican electricity authority before construction.
  • Verify environmental, construction and safety requirements independently.
Maximum Electrical Power
Mozambique regulatory maximum: No verified numerical licence-free maximum for a purely isolated private self-consumption gravity generation installation was identified in the official sources reviewed.
