Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Gabon and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is completely isolated from the public electricity network, without electricity imports or exports.

Gabon's electricity legislation expressly regulates self-generation (autoproduction) installations.

Law No. 2016-012 of 2023 establishes different regulatory regimes according to the installed power of self-generation installations.

The legislation provides a declaration regime for self-generation installations above 100 kW and below 1 MW and an authorization regime for installations above 1 MW.

Administrative Requirements

Gabon's electricity legislation provides a specific regulatory structure for self-generation facilities.

Self-generation above 100 kW and below 1 MW

Article 71 provides that where the power of a self-generation installation is greater than 100 kW and less than 1 MW, the owner must make a prior declaration before commissioning to the Ministry responsible for Energy.

Self-generation above 1 MW

Article 69 establishes an authorization regime for self-generation installations with a power greater than 1 MW.

Article 70 further specifies the conditions under which this authorization may be granted.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation physically isolated from the public electricity network.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Do not import electricity.
  • Determine the exact installed generation power before selecting the legal procedure.
  • Submit the required declaration when the facility is above 100 kW and below 1 MW.
  • Obtain the applicable authorization when the facility exceeds 1 MW.
  • Prevent accidental connection to the public grid.
Maximum Electrical Power
Gabon self-generation threshold: 100 kW is the upper boundary below which the specific Article 71 declaration regime is not triggered by the stated >100 kW threshold.

Above 100 kW and below 1 MW, a prior declaration is required.

Above 1 MW, the installation falls under the authorization regime established by Article 69.
