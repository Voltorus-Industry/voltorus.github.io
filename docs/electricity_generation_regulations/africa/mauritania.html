Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Mauritania and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the national electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public grid.

Electricity would be consumed exclusively by the owner or operator for household, industrial or corporate requirements.

Mauritania's electricity sector is governed by Loi n° 2022-027 portant Code de l'Electricité, promulgated in December 2022.

The Code expressly recognizes electricity self-production ("autoproduction") and establishes different regulatory regimes according to installed power.

Administrative Requirements

The Mauritanian Electricity Code expressly allows persons to satisfy their own electricity requirements through self-production within their private property or authorized premises.

Article 14 establishes three regulatory levels for electricity self-production:

  • Free regime: self-production below a power threshold fixed by ministerial order on the proposal of the electricity regulator.
  • Prior declaration: self-production above the free-regime threshold and below the second threshold fixed by ministerial order.
  • Prior authorization: self-production above the maximum threshold applicable to the declaration regime.
Important: The numerical thresholds are not specified directly in Article 14 of the 2022 Code. They are delegated to ministerial regulatory instruments.

Therefore, a national numerical licence-free capacity should not be invented without identifying the applicable ministerial order.

  • Environmental feasibility requirements may apply.
  • Electrical safety requirements apply.
  • Construction and land-use requirements may apply.
  • Environmental authorization may be required depending on the project.
  • Any sale of surplus electricity requires the applicable electricity-market authorization.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain a completely isolated electrical system.
  • Do not connect the installation to the national electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not export electricity to the public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Verify the current ministerial power thresholds before construction.
  • Obtain confirmation from the Mauritanian electricity regulator concerning the applicable self-production regime.
  • Complete the required environmental and construction assessments.
Maximum Electrical Power
Mauritania regulatory maximum: No verified numerical licence-free threshold was identified in the text of Article 14 of the 2022 Electricity Code itself.

Article 14 delegates the numerical thresholds for the free, declaration and authorization regimes to ministerial orders proposed by the electricity regulator.

Consequently, the website should not state a fabricated kW or MW figure as the Mauritanian licence-free maximum.
