Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Ethiopia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed as a completely isolated electrical system with no import from or export to the public electricity grid.

Ethiopia regulates electricity generation through its national energy legislation and regulatory framework.

The principal framework includes Energy Proclamation No. 810/2013, as amended, Energy Regulation No. 447/2019, and the Ethiopian Energy Authority's regulations and directives governing electricity generation and off-grid systems.

Ethiopia also has a specific Mini-Grid Directive governing off-grid electricity generation and distribution systems up to 10 MW. This should not, however, be confused with a nationwide exemption from generation licensing for every isolated self-consumption installation.

Administrative Requirements

Ethiopia's electricity framework requires regulatory consideration of electricity generation even where the facility is off-grid.

Energy Regulation No. 447/2019 provides the regulatory framework for generation, transmission, distribution and electricity-sales licences.

The Ethiopian Energy Authority also issued a Mini-Grid Directive governing off-grid generation and distribution systems up to 10 MW.

Off-Grid / Mini-Grid Framework

The 10 MW figure is the regulatory scope of the mini-grid directive identified in the sources reviewed.

It should not automatically be interpreted as a licence-free maximum for a private, self-consumption-only generation facility. The exact licence or consent requirement must be determined from the applicable category of the proposed project.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Generation authorization or licence, where applicable.
  • Off-grid / mini-grid authorization where applicable.
  • Land and regional-authority approvals.
  • Environmental authorization.
  • Construction approval.
  • Electrical and technical standards.
  • Structural and fire-safety requirements.
  • Water permits where applicable.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the facility physically isolated from the national electricity grid.
  • Do not import electricity.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Restrict electricity use to internal consumption.
  • Avoid third-party electricity supply.
  • Determine in advance whether the project falls within the applicable off-grid or generation licensing category.
  • Obtain the necessary Ethiopian Energy Authority approvals before construction.
  • Install protection preventing accidental grid energisation.
