Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Tunisia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Tunisian electricity generation from renewable energy sources is principally regulated by Law No. 2015-12 of 11 May 2015 relating to the production of electricity from renewable energies , together with its implementing legislation.

The Tunisian renewable-electricity framework expressly covers electricity production for self-consumption.

The current Tunisian framework provides a specific threshold of 1 MW for renewable-energy self-production projects below which the corresponding authorization requirement was removed in 2022.

The Tunisian Ministry of Industry, Mines and Energy expressly announced the removal of authorization for renewable-energy self-production projects with a capacity of less than 1 MW.

A completely isolated installation must nevertheless be assessed according to its nominal generating capacity, electrical configuration, technology, location and applicable construction, environmental, safety and planning requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Tunisian legislation establishes different administrative regimes for electricity production from renewable energy sources.

Law No. 2015-12 expressly covers renewable-energy electricity production for self-consumption.

In November 2022, the Tunisian Ministry of Industry, Mines and Energy announced the removal of the authorization requirement for renewable-energy self-production projects with a capacity below 1 MW .

Consequently, for the specific renewable self-consumption framework, the relevant regulatory threshold is 1 MW .

Application to an Isolated Gravity Installation

Tunisian legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The regulatory treatment therefore depends on the legal classification of the installation and whether it falls within the renewable electricity self-production framework.

For a genuinely isolated gravity-powered installation intended exclusively for self-consumption, the 1 MW threshold is the relevant threshold to use for the renewable self-production authorization assessment.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Tunisian public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity is supplied to third parties.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-consumption facility.
  • The nominal electrical capacity is documented precisely.
  • The applicable Tunisian authorization requirements are verified before construction.
