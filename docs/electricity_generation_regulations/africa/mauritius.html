Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Mauritius and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to remain physically disconnected from every licensee's electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively by the owner, its employees or tenants located on the premises, subject to the conditions established by the Electricity Act.

The principal federal framework is the Electricity Act 2005, as amended.

Section 3 contains a specific exemption for qualifying off-grid generating stations with an installed capacity not exceeding 400 kW.

Administrative Requirements

Section 3 of the Electricity Act establishes electricity-generation licensing requirements and specific exemptions.

An off-grid generating station can qualify for the exemption where:

  • the generating station is not physically connected at any time to a licensee's network;
  • the electricity is used to meet the electricity requirements of the person, employees or tenants on the premises;
  • the premises are not physically connected to and are not supplied by a licensee's network; and
  • the installed capacity does not exceed 400 kW.

The exemption does not eliminate other applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety, fire-safety or land-use requirements.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation physically disconnected from the licensee's network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Keep installed generating capacity at or below 400 kW if relying on the statutory off-grid exemption.
  • Use electricity exclusively for qualifying on-premises consumption.
  • Install appropriate electrical isolation and protection equipment.
  • Verify construction, fire, environmental and occupational-safety requirements independently.
Maximum Electrical Power
Mauritius licence-free off-grid maximum: 400 kW for a qualifying off-grid generating station under the conditions of the Electricity Act.

This is the relevant legal threshold for the specific off-grid self-consumption exemption.
