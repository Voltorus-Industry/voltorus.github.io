Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Algeria and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, agricultural or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Algerian electricity generation is regulated under Law No. 02-01 of 5 February 2002 relating to electricity and public gas distribution, together with its implementing regulations.

The Commission de Régulation de l'Électricité et du Gaz (CREG) distinguishes electricity-generation installations according to their purpose, installed capacity and connection to the electricity network.

For self-consumption installations, the principal threshold identified by the CREG is 25 MW under ISO conditions. Installations below this threshold are subject to a declaration regime, while installations at or above 25 MW require an operating authorization.

Administrative Requirements

An isolated self-consumption installation is not automatically free from all administrative requirements.

Under the Algerian electricity framework, an electricity-generation installation intended for self-consumption with installed capacity below 25 MW under ISO conditions is subject to a prior declaration rather than the operating-authorization regime.

Self-consumption below 25 MW

The CREG identifies installations intended for self-consumption with installed capacity below 25 MW under ISO conditions as being subject to the declaration regime.

The declaration is made to the electricity and gas regulatory authority and must comply with the applicable technical and legal requirements.

Therefore, the absence of a grid connection does not eliminate the requirement to declare an installation falling within this regime.

Installations at or above 25 MW

Self-consumption installations with installed capacity of 25 MW or more are subject to the operating-authorization regime.

The CREG evaluates authorization applications according to criteria including safety, energy efficiency, primary energy source, site selection, land use, environmental protection and the technical and financial capabilities of the applicant.

Less than 25 MW

Self-consumption installations below 25 MW under ISO conditions fall under the declaration regime. At 25 MW or above, an operating authorization is required.

The relevant legal threshold for an isolated self-consumption installation is therefore 25 MW .

The legally precise formulation is "less than 25 MW" for the declaration regime.

A 25 MW installation should not be classified as being below the authorization threshold.

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. The 25 MW figure is the regulatory threshold identified by the Algerian electricity regulator for distinguishing the declaration regime from the operating-authorization regime for self-consumption electricity-generation installations. It is not a technology-specific mechanical or engineering limit for gravity-powered generation. The proposed installation must also comply with applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety, land-use and technical requirements.
