Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Rwanda and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Rwanda is principally regulated by Law No. 21/2011 of 23 June 2011 governing electricity in Rwanda , as amended and supplemented, including by Law No. 52/2018 of 13 August 2018 , together with electricity licensing regulations administered by the Rwanda Utilities Regulatory Authority (RURA) .

The Rwandan electricity licensing framework establishes licensing requirements for electricity production and provides specific treatment for electricity auto-production intended for the producer's own consumption.

Electricity production, transmission, distribution and trading are generally subject to licensing by the competent regulatory authority.

The electricity licensing regulations expressly provide that operation of a project intended for electricity auto-production with a capacity of less than 50 kW does not require a licence issued by the Authority and is not subject to prior notification.

Consequently, for a completely isolated, self-consumption electricity-generation facility, the maximum capacity below this specific no-licence threshold is 49.99 kW .

An installation at or above the applicable threshold must be assessed under the Rwandan electricity authorization and licensing framework according to its capacity, operating configuration, technology and intended use.

Administrative Requirements

The Rwandan electricity licensing regulations establish a general requirement for a licence to conduct electricity production, transmission, distribution and trading activities.

The framework also specifically recognizes electricity production by a company or household for its own consumption.

The regulations provide a specific no-licence treatment for electricity auto-production projects with a capacity of less than 50 kW .

Self-Production Capacity Regimes
Installed Capacity Regulatory Treatment Principal Requirement
Less than 50 kW Auto-production without electricity licence No electricity-generation licence and no prior notification under Article 6 of the Electricity Licensing Regulations.
50 kW or more Electricity regulatory framework applies The applicable licence, authorization or regulatory procedure must be determined according to the project configuration and activity.
Electricity Auto-Production

The electricity licensing regulations recognize electricity operations undertaken by a company or household for its own consumption.

Where a private power plant is established within private property, the installation must not violate the public or State private domain.

Electricity operations must also comply with applicable electrical-safety requirements.

The absence of a generation licence below 50 kW does not remove other construction, environmental, safety, land-use or technical requirements that may apply to the specific installation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Rwandan public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity is supplied to third-party consumers.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private auto-production facility.
  • The nominal installed electrical capacity is documented precisely.
  • Where the project is intended to remain below the no-licence threshold, its installed capacity is maintained below 50 kW.
  • Any project at or above 50 kW is reviewed with RURA before construction and operation.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.

Rwanda — maximum capacity below the auto-production no-licence threshold:

49.99 kW

The Rwanda Electricity Licensing Regulations expressly provide that an electricity auto-production project with a capacity of less than 50 kW does not require a licence and is not subject to prior notification to the Authority.

Article 6 of the Electricity Licensing Regulations provides that operation of a project intended for electricity auto-production with a capacity of less than 50 kW does not require a licence issued by RURA and is not subject to prior notification.

Accordingly, for the website's regulatory classification of a purely off-grid self-consumption facility operating below the no-licence threshold, the maximum practical value to display is 49.99 kW .

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

Rwandan electricity legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The applicable regulatory treatment is therefore determined according to the installation's electrical capacity, operating configuration and intended use.

The proposed installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island exclusively for internal consumption.

For this configuration, the appropriate website value for operation below the electricity auto-production no-licence threshold is 49.99 kW .

Website classification for Rwanda:

49.99 kW

Use 49.99 kW as the maximum rated electrical capacity for a purely off-grid auto-production facility intended to remain below the 50 kW no-licence threshold.

At 50 kW or above , the applicable Rwandan electricity regulatory requirements must be assessed with RURA.

The 49.99 kW figure is a regulatory threshold for the website's classification.

It should not be interpreted as a technology-specific mechanical or electrical performance limitation for gravity-powered electricity generation.
