Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Angola and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, agricultural or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Angolan electricity generation is governed by the Lei Geral de Electricidade and its implementing regulations.

Presidential Decree No. 76/21 of 25 March 2021 regulates electricity production, transmission, distribution and commercialization.

The regulation expressly distinguishes self-production from public electricity production. Self-production for own use is a free activity subject to the applicable regulatory and technical rules.

Administrative Requirements

Angola expressly recognizes electricity production for own use as auto-production.

Presidential Decree No. 76/21 establishes a specific treatment for self-production installations with installed capacity up to 100 kVA.

Self-production up to 100 kVA

Self-production systems with installed capacity up to 100 kVA do not require an establishment or exploitation licence under Article 45(2) of Presidential Decree No. 76/21.

Instead, registration of a term of responsibility is sufficient, subject to the applicable regulatory requirements.

This is the principal capacity threshold relevant to a small isolated self-consumption installation.

Self-production above 100 kVA

Self-producers with installed capacity above 100 kVA must license their generating centres under the applicable electricity-generation licensing regulations.

The absence of a public-grid connection does not remove this capacity-based licensing requirement.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Registration of the term of responsibility for systems up to 100 kVA.
  • Establishment and exploitation licensing above 100 kVA.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Construction requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Structural and mechanical safety requirements.
  • Technical requirements specific to the generation technology.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • No physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity import.
  • No electricity export.
  • No electricity sale to third parties.
  • Electricity used exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Maintain the installation at or below 100 kVA if the project is intended to remain within the non-licensed self-production category.
  • Register the required term of responsibility.
  • Install appropriate electrical protection.
  • Comply with applicable construction, environmental and safety requirements.
