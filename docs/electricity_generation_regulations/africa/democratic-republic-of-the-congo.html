Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in the Democratic Republic of the Congo is principally regulated by Law No. 14/011 of 17 June 2014 relating to the electricity sector , as amended and supplemented, including by Ordinance-Law No. 25/025 of 5 February 2025 .

The electricity-sector framework establishes different legal regimes according to the nature and installed capacity of the electricity installation.

The legislation expressly defines an "auto-producteur" as a natural or legal person producing electricity for its own consumption.

For installations located outside the public domain, the regulatory framework distinguishes between freedom, declaration, authorization and licence regimes according to installed capacity and the nature of the electricity activity.

For self-production facilities, installations with an installed capacity between 100 kW and 999.99 kW fall under the authorization regime.

Self-production installations between 51 kW and 99 kW are subject to a declaration, while installations with a capacity of 50 kW or less may fall under the freedom regime when the conditions established by law are satisfied.

Independent electricity production with a power equal to or greater than 1 MW falls under the electricity-production licence regime.

A completely isolated installation must therefore be assessed according to its nominal generating capacity, legal classification, electrical configuration, technology, location and applicable construction, environmental, safety and planning requirements.

The electricity-sector legislation of the Democratic Republic of the Congo establishes different legal regimes for electricity installations according to their activity, location and installed capacity.

For an installation designed exclusively for self-consumption outside the public domain, the following capacity thresholds are particularly relevant.

Self-Producer Definition

The DRC electricity legislation defines an "auto-producteur" as a natural or legal person producing electricity for its own consumption.

The proposed gravity-powered installation is intended to operate exclusively for internal consumption and therefore should be assessed under the self-production framework where the applicable legal conditions are satisfied.

The fact that the installation is completely isolated from the public grid does not by itself remove the applicable construction, safety, environmental or electricity-sector requirements.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Congolese public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity is supplied to third-party consumers.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-production facility.
  • The nominal installed electrical capacity is documented precisely.
  • Where the installation is between 51 kW and 99.99 kW, the applicable written declaration is completed.
  • Where the installation is between 100 kW and 999.99 kW, the applicable self-production authorization is obtained before operation.
  • If the proposed installation reaches 1 MW or more, the applicable electricity-production licence regime is verified with the ARE and competent authorities before construction.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

DRC electricity legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The applicable regulatory threshold is therefore determined by the legal classification of the installation and its installed electrical capacity rather than by the word "gravity" itself.

The proposed installation is designed as an independent electrical island for internal self-consumption, without electricity import, export or sale.

For this configuration, the relevant self-production authorization range extends up to 999.99 kW .

Consequently, the exact maximum capacity to use on the website for this category is 999.99 kW .

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. The 999.99 kW figure represents the upper limit of the self-production authorization range expressly identified by the Democratic Republic of the Congo electricity-sector framework. Self-production installations from 100 kW to 999.99 kW are subject to authorization, while installations from 51 kW to 99.99 kW are subject to declaration and installations of 50 kW or less may fall under the freedom regime where the statutory conditions are satisfied. Independent electricity production at 1 MW or more falls under the electricity-production licence regime. The legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically for gravity-powered electricity generation.
