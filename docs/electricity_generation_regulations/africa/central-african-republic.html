Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Central African Republic and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

Electricity production is regulated as part of the national electricity sector and remains subject to public-law requirements.

The available regulatory sources reviewed confirm that electricity production and related activities are regulated.

No reliable nationwide numerical exemption specific to a completely isolated gravity-powered self-consumption facility was identified.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity production, transmission and distribution are regulated activities in the Central African Republic.

A project-specific determination should therefore be obtained from the competent electricity authority before construction.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain physical isolation from the public grid.
  • Restrict electricity use to the owner's site.
  • Do not sell or supply electricity to third parties.
  • Document installed capacity and technical characteristics.
  • Obtain written regulatory confirmation before construction.
  • Verify construction, environmental and safety requirements.

Chad has adopted an electricity-sector legal framework establishing licensing regimes and an electricity regulatory authority.

The Electricity Sector law regulates electricity production and other electricity-sector activities.

Administrative Requirements

Chad's electricity-sector legislation establishes licensing regimes and a regulatory authority for electricity activities.

The available sources reviewed do not provide a sufficiently clear numerical exemption threshold specifically for an isolated self-consumption installation using gravity-powered generation.

The actual project capacity, technology and electricity-use configuration should therefore be submitted to the competent authority.

Construction, environmental, land-use and electrical safety requirements should be assessed separately.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the facility physically disconnected from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import or export electricity.
  • Do not supply third parties.
  • Keep electricity consumption on the owner's premises.
  • Document the installed electrical capacity.
  • Obtain written regulatory classification before construction.
  • Verify environmental and construction requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Exact numerical threshold: Not reliably identified for a completely off-grid gravity-powered self-consumption installation.

The current sources confirm the existence of an electricity licensing framework but do not establish a reliable single kW/MW threshold that can safely be presented as an exemption for this particular configuration.

The project capacity should therefore be validated directly with the competent Chadian electricity authority before development.
