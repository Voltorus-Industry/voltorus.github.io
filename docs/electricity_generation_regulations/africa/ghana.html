Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Ghana and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Ghana regulates electricity generation through the Energy Commission and the national electricity regulatory framework.

The Renewable Energy Act 2011 and the Energy Commission's regulatory framework are particularly relevant where the technology is classified as a renewable-energy electricity-generation technology.

A genuinely isolated self-consumption installation must be distinguished from a mini-grid supplying third parties and from a renewable-energy facility connected to the public distribution network.

Administrative Requirements

Ghana's electricity regulatory framework distinguishes electricity generation, mini-grid operation and grid-connected renewable self-generation.

Important distinction concerning the 100 kW threshold

Ghanaian mini-grid rules use a 100 kW threshold in the regulatory treatment of mini-grids.

Mini-grids exceeding 100 kW and up to 1 MW have been subject to licensing requirements, while smaller mini-grids have been subject to registration requirements.

These mini-grid thresholds must not be treated as a general nationwide exemption for a completely isolated private self-consumption electricity-generation facility.

Grid-connected Renewable Generation

Ghana also operates a net-metering framework for renewable-energy generating systems connected to the distribution network.

That framework is not the appropriate classification for a facility that has no physical connection to the public electricity network and does not export electricity.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The facility has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is consumed exclusively by the operator or owner.
  • The facility is not operated as a mini-grid supplying external consumers.
  • No electricity trading is undertaken.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to the public network.
  • The facility is documented as an isolated self-consumption installation.
  • The Energy Commission classification is confirmed before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Verified maximum specifically applicable to private isolated self-consumption:

No separate numerical nationwide capacity ceiling for a completely isolated private self-consumption facility has been verified from the sources reviewed.

Ghanaian regulations contain numerical thresholds for particular electricity-sector configurations, including mini-grids.

The 100 kW and 1 MW thresholds associated with mini-grid regulation should not be presented as a general maximum for every off-grid electricity generator.

Mini-grid thresholds — not applicable automatically to private self-consumption

Mini-grid regulatory material identifies installations up to 100 kW for registration and larger installations up to 1 MW for licensing.

Those values concern mini-grid operation and should not be entered as the maximum capacity of the proposed isolated gravity-powered self-consumption facility.

Website classification for Ghana:

No verified national numerical maximum identified specifically for a completely isolated private self-consumption facility.

The 100 kW / 1 MW mini-grid thresholds must not be substituted for an off-grid self-consumption limit.

The exact permissible capacity should therefore be confirmed with the Energy Commission of Ghana based on the technology, installed capacity, location, ownership and actual operating configuration.
