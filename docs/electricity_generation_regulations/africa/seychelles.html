Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Seychelles and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Seychelles is principally regulated by Electricity Act, 2023 (Act 13 of 2023) , together with the Electricity (Licensing) Regulations, 2024 .

Under the Seychelles electricity framework, electricity generation is a licensable electricity activity.

The Electricity Act also provides specific circumstances in which a person is not obliged to obtain a generation licence.

One of the statutory provisions specifically addresses generation for own consumption and provides a capacity ceiling of 500 kW for the applicable licence-exemption category.

A person benefiting from this licence exemption must nevertheless obtain an authorisation in accordance with the prescribed procedure.

The Seychelles regulatory framework therefore distinguishes between a generation licence and a generation authorisation.

For the proposed completely isolated self-consumption installation, the relevant website capacity value is 500 kW , subject to confirmation that the specific project qualifies for the statutory authorisation regime.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity generation is a licensable electricity activity under the Seychelles Electricity Act.

The Act provides specific circumstances in which a person is not required to obtain a generation licence.

The relevant statutory provision allows a qualifying person to generate electricity for own consumption within the applicable capacity limit of 500 kW .

The licence exemption does not mean that the installation is exempt from regulatory control. The Act requires the person benefiting from the exemption to obtain an authorisation .

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • The installation has no physical connection to the Seychelles public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as an autogeneration facility.
  • The installed electrical capacity is precisely documented.
  • The project remains within the statutory 500 kW licence-exemption ceiling where the exemption is relied upon.
  • The required generation authorisation is obtained before construction or operation.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.

Seychelles — maximum capacity within the statutory licence-exemption category:

500 kW

The Electricity Act, 2023 provides a specific capacity ceiling of 500 kW for the relevant own-consumption generation exemption from the obligation to obtain a generation licence.

A qualifying person within this statutory category must nevertheless obtain the applicable generation authorisation.

The 500 kW value should therefore be understood as the maximum capacity associated with the licence-exemption category, not as an exemption from all regulatory requirements.

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

Seychelles electricity legislation does not establish a separate capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The relevant legal distinction is based on the electricity activity, the own-consumption purpose and the applicable authorisation or licence regime.

The proposed installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island exclusively for internal consumption.

For the website classification based on the statutory licence-exemption ceiling, the applicable maximum value is 500 kW .

Website classification for Seychelles:

500 kW

Use 500 kW as the maximum generating capacity associated with the statutory licence-exemption category.

A generation authorisation remains applicable to qualifying installations relying on this exemption.

The 500 kW figure is a regulatory capacity ceiling and is not a technology-specific mechanical performance limit for gravity-powered electricity generation.
