Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Djibouti and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Djibouti's electricity production framework is principally established by Law No. 88/AN/15/7ème L of 1 July 2015 regulating independent electricity producers and its implementing regulations.

Djibouti's legislation expressly recognizes the auto-producer producing electricity for exclusive personal use from renewable energy without injection into the national grid.

The 2019 implementing order establishes an explicit capacity category for small renewable-energy auto-producers operating for personal use only.

Administrative Requirements

Djibouti expressly distinguishes between self-consumption installations and electricity production intended for injection into the national network.

Small Renewable Auto-producer

The 2019 order identifies the following category:

Petit Auto-Producteur (P1)

  • Renewable energy.
  • Personal use only.
  • Installed capacity: 1 kW to 20 kW .
  • No production licence, certificate or authorization required.
Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Electricity-sector licensing where the installation exceeds the applicable small-producer category.
  • Construction requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Structural engineering requirements.
  • Site-specific authorization requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for personal consumption.
  • The installation operates in island mode.
  • The installed capacity is kept within the applicable regulatory category where appropriate.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental grid connection.
Maximum Electrical Power
Maximum capacity in the identified no-licence renewable auto-producer category:

20 kW

This applies to the "Petit Auto-Producteur (P1)" category for renewable energy used for personal consumption only.

The order defines installed power as the sum of the maximum unit capacities of generating machines capable of operating simultaneously within the same establishment.

Demonstrated Gravity-Technology Benchmark

The Rudong EVx gravity-energy-storage system in China has a rated electrical output of 25 MW and an energy capacity of 100 MWh.

This 25 MW value demonstrates the scale of commercially commissioned gravity technology and is not the Djibouti regulatory threshold.
