Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Senegal and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Senegalese electricity generation is principally regulated by Law No. 2021-31 of 9 July 2021 establishing the Electricity Code , together with its implementing regulations, including the regulatory framework applicable to electricity self-production.

The Electricity Code establishes a declaration regime for self-production undertaken for the producer's own needs and a licensing regime for self-production above the applicable capacity threshold.

The implementing decree on electricity self-production establishes different declaration thresholds according to the technology used.

The decree expressly establishes a maximum installed capacity of 2 MW for a self-production technology that is not specifically listed in the technology categories provided by the decree.

Gravity-powered electricity generation is not identified as a separate technology category in the decree.

Consequently, the proposed gravity-powered installation falls within the other technology category, for which the applicable declaration threshold is 2 MW .

A purely off-grid installation intended exclusively for internal self-consumption should therefore be assessed against the 2 MW threshold, together with all applicable construction, environmental, safety, land-use and technical requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Article 29 of the Electricity Code establishes a declaration regime for persons wishing to operate electricity installations they own for their own needs, without supplying the public or third parties.

Article 30 establishes a licensing regime for electricity production and also applies the licence regime to self-production whose installed capacity exceeds the threshold established by decree.

Decree No. 2025-286 establishes the capacity thresholds applicable to self-production according to the technology.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • The installation has no physical connection to the Senegalese public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as an auto-production facility.
  • The installed electrical capacity is precisely documented.
  • The gravity technology is classified as a technology not specifically listed in the self-production decree.
  • The installed capacity does not exceed 2 MW where the project is intended to operate under the applicable declaration threshold.
  • Any project exceeding 2 MW is submitted for review under the electricity-generation licence regime.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.

Decree No. 2025-286 provides that for any self-production technology not specifically listed, the maximum installed capacity for the declaration regime is 2 MW .

Gravity-powered electricity generation is not separately identified among the technology categories listed in the decree.

The proposed technology therefore falls within the category of technologies not specifically listed.

The corresponding maximum installed capacity is 2 MW .

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

The Senegalese self-production decree does not identify gravity-powered generation as a separate technology.

It establishes a residual category for technologies not otherwise enumerated.

Gravity-powered electricity generation should therefore be classified under this residual category, giving an applicable threshold of 2 MW .

Website classification for Senegal:

2 MW

Use 2 MW as the maximum installed capacity for the gravity-powered self-production category under the technology-not-specifically-listed threshold.

Above this threshold, the applicable electricity-generation licence regime must be assessed.

The 2 MW figure is a Senegalese regulatory capacity threshold and is not a technology-specific mechanical performance limitation.
