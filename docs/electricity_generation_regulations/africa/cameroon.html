Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Cameroon and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Cameroon is principally regulated by Law No. 2011/022 of 14 December 2011 governing the electricity sector in Cameroon .

The implementing framework includes Decree No. 2012/2806/PM of 24 September 2012 applying certain provisions of Law No. 2011/022.

The Cameroonian electricity framework establishes different regulatory regimes for electricity self-production according to the installed capacity of the facility.

For self-production facilities, the regulatory framework distinguishes between installations having a capacity of up to 100 kW , installations above 100 kW and below 1 MW , and installations above 1 MW .

For a genuinely isolated installation with no connection to the public electricity network, the project must nevertheless be assessed according to its nominal generating capacity, technology, electrical configuration, location and applicable construction, environmental, safety and planning requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Cameroonian electricity legislation establishes different administrative regimes for self-production facilities according to their installed electrical capacity.

The current framework identifies three relevant capacity ranges for self-production facilities:

  • 100 kW or less — no legal or administrative formality for self-production installations other than hydroelectric plants, subject to compliance with approved standards and safety requirements.
  • More than 100 kW and less than 1 MW — prior declaration to the Electricity Sector Regulatory Agency (ARSEL) before commissioning.
  • More than 1 MW — authorization for the establishment and operation of the self-production installation.

The declaration regime requires the owner to provide, among other information, the primary energy source, the capacity of the installation and evidence of conformity with approved standards.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Cameroonian public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No participation in electricity balancing or aggregation markets is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-production facility.
  • The nominal electrical capacity is documented precisely.
  • If the facility exceeds 100 kW but remains below 1 MW, the required prior declaration is submitted to ARSEL before commissioning.
  • The installation does not exceed 1 MW if the project is intended to remain within the declaration regime rather than the authorization regime.
  • If the facility is designed above 1 MW, the applicable authorization for self-production is obtained before establishment and operation.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.
Maximum Electrical Power
Cameroon — principal self-production capacity threshold:

1 MW (1,000 kW)

Under the Cameroonian electricity regulatory framework, self-production facilities with a capacity greater than 100 kW and less than 1 MW fall under the prior declaration regime.

Self-production facilities with a capacity greater than 1 MW fall under the authorization regime.

The official ARSEL framework identifies installations of self-production with a power greater than 1 MW as subject to authorization.

For installations with a capacity greater than 100 kW and less than 1 MW , the owner must make a prior declaration to ARSEL before commissioning.

Installations of self-production with a capacity of 100 kW or less are not subject to a legal or administrative formality under the stated regime for self-production installations other than hydroelectric plants, provided that the installations comply with approved standards and safety norms.

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

Cameroonian electricity legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The applicable capacity regime is therefore determined according to the legal classification of the electricity-generation installation and its installed capacity, rather than by the word "gravity" itself.

A gravity-powered installation used solely for internal self-consumption should therefore be assessed under the Cameroonian self-production framework.

For a project intended to remain below the authorization threshold, the relevant maximum capacity to use for the website is 1 MW (1,000 kW) .

Website classification for Cameroon:

1 MW (1,000 kW)

Use 1 MW as the Cameroonian self-production capacity threshold for a gravity-powered electricity-generation facility intended for internal self-consumption.

A facility with a nominal capacity greater than 1 MW falls within the authorization regime for self-production.

A facility with a nominal capacity greater than 100 kW and less than 1 MW requires a prior declaration to ARSEL.

The 1 MW (1,000 kW) figure is a regulatory capacity threshold for determining the applicable self-production regime. It is not a technology-specific mechanical or electrical performance limit for gravity generation.

Consequently, for the purpose of classifying the proposed facility on this website, the appropriate Cameroon value is 1 MW / 1,000 kW as the upper capacity threshold before the self-production authorization regime applies.

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. The 1 MW (1,000 kW) figure represents the principal capacity threshold separating the declaration regime from the authorization regime for self-production facilities under the Cameroonian electricity regulatory framework. Facilities above 100 kW and below 1 MW require a prior declaration to ARSEL, while self-production facilities above 1 MW are subject to an authorization regime. The legislation does not establish a separate capacity threshold specifically for gravity-powered electricity generation. The precise legal treatment of a completely isolated gravity-powered self-consumption installation should therefore be confirmed with ARSEL before construction or operation. Applicable construction, environmental, land-use, structural, electrical-safety and other regulatory requirements must also be verified for the specific installation and site.
