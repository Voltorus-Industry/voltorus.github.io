Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Morocco and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The principal framework is Law No. 82-21 relating to the self-production of electrical energy , promulgated on 10 February 2023.

The law expressly covers self-production installations whether or not they are connected to the national electricity network.

An installation connected to an internal self-consumption installation but not connected to the national network falls under the declaration regime established by Article 3.

Therefore, the Moroccan regime is materially different from a simple nationwide licence-free kW exemption.

Administrative Requirements

Article 3 provides that the realization or operation of an electricity self-production installation connected to a self-consumption installation and not connected to the national electricity network is subject to a declaration to the administration.

For installations connected to the national network, Law No. 82-21 establishes different regimes according to power:

  • Below the regulatory threshold of Article 4: declaration to the distribution-network manager.
  • From that threshold up to less than 5 MW: connection agreement regime.
  • 5 MW and above: administrative authorization under Article 6.

The 5 MW figure should therefore not be interpreted as a general off-grid exemption threshold.

  • Electrical safety requirements apply.
  • Construction and planning requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Technical standards must be respected.
  • Multiple installations on the same site are aggregated for power calculation under Article 11.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the generation installation physically disconnected from the national electricity network.
  • Maintain a dedicated internal electrical island.
  • Do not import electricity from the national grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the national grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • File the applicable declaration under Article 3.
  • Verify construction, environmental and safety approvals independently.
Maximum Electrical Power
Morocco off-grid classification: Article 3 requires a declaration for an self-production installation connected to a self-consumption installation and not connected to the national electricity network.

The law therefore does not establish a simple nationwide off-grid threshold such as 400 kW below which no administrative procedure exists.
