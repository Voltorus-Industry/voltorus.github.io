Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of the Congo and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements under the control of the producer.

The principal electricity-sector framework is the Law No. 14-2003 of 10 April 2003 establishing the Electricity Code .

The Electricity Code expressly defines an auto-producer as a person or entity producing electricity for personal purposes.

Article 40 establishes that the right of an auto-producer to operate depends on installed capacity and may be subject to a freedom, declaration or authorization regime.

The exact thresholds for these regimes are to be specified by implementing regulation. A specific nationwide kW exemption threshold should therefore not be invented where the applicable official threshold has not been identified.

Administrative Requirements

The Electricity Code does not make self-production automatically exempt from administrative regulation.

Auto-production Regime

Article 40 provides that the right of an auto-producer to operate is determined according to installed capacity.

Depending on capacity, the activity may be subject to:

  • a freedom regime;
  • a prior administrative declaration; or
  • an administrative authorization.

Article 41 provides that the applicable regimes, conditions and thresholds are to be specified by regulatory provisions.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Electricity-sector declaration or authorization where applicable.
  • Certificate of conformity.

  • Additional requirements arising from the particular technology and site.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain genuinely isolated and dedicated to self-consumption, we recommend that:

  • No physical connection is made to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported.
  • No electricity is exported.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is consumed exclusively by the producer.
  • The installation is operated in electrical island mode.
  • Appropriate electrical protection prevents accidental grid energisation.
  • The installed capacity is documented precisely.
  • The applicable implementing regulations are checked before construction.
  • A certificate of conformity is obtained where required.
Maximum Electrical Power
Republic of the Congo legal threshold:

The Electricity Code establishes capacity-based freedom, declaration and authorization regimes for auto-producers, but Article 41 delegates the precise thresholds to implementing regulations.

No reliable official nationwide kW exemption threshold has been identified in the sources reviewed.

It would therefore be incorrect to insert a fabricated kW figure as the maximum legally exempt capacity.

Demonstrated Gravity-Technology Benchmark

The Rudong EVx gravity-energy-storage facility in China has a rated electrical output of 25 MW and an energy capacity of 100 MWh.

It represents a demonstrated commercial-scale gravity technology benchmark and does not constitute a Republic of the Congo legal threshold.
