Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Botswana and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, agricultural or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Botswana is principally regulated under the Electricity Supply Act and the regulatory framework administered by the Botswana Energy Regulatory Authority (BERA).

The Electricity Supply Act establishes a general licensing requirement for generation while providing a specific exemption for qualifying small self-generation installations located entirely on the owner's premises where no public electricity supply is available.

Administrative Requirements

Botswana generally requires a licence for electricity generation, supply, transmission, distribution, export or import.

A specific exemption applies to qualifying self-generation installations located entirely on the owner's property and used solely by the owner where no public electricity supply is available.

Owner-premises self-generation

The current framework, following the 2007 and 2016 amendments, maintains a capacity threshold of 100 kW for the qualifying self-generation exemption.

The installation must satisfy the conditions attached to the exemption, including its location on the owner's property and its use solely by the owner.

The absence of public electricity supply is an important condition of the statutory exemption.

Maximum Electrical Power
Maximum qualifying self-generation capacity:

100 kW

The 100 kW threshold applies to the statutory self-generation exemption where the other conditions of the Electricity Supply Act are satisfied.

The relevant capacity value for the comparative database is therefore 100 kW.

This value is a statutory exemption threshold and not a technology-specific engineering limit for gravity-powered generation.

Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Burkina Faso and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, agricultural, commercial or institutional electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

The electricity sector of Burkina Faso is regulated under Law No. 053-2012/AN of 17 December 2012 on the general regulation of the electricity subsector.

The law establishes different regimes for electricity production, including specific rules for installations in the second segment of the electricity subsector.

The law expressly excludes electricity-generation installations with capacity of 10 kW or less from the prior authorization regime identified for production in the second segment.

Administrative Requirements

Burkina Faso's electricity legislation distinguishes between the first and second segments of the electricity subsector.

Installations up to 10 kW

Article 49 of Law No. 053-2012/AN provides that electricity-generation installations with capacity less than or equal to 10 kW are excluded from the prior authorization regime applicable to the second segment.

This is the principal statutory threshold relevant to a small isolated self-consumption installation.

Installations above 10 kW

Electricity-generation installations above 10 kW and below 25 kW are subject to the authorization regime identified in Article 49.

The legislation provides for government authorization, with the regulatory authority involved according to the applicable procedures.

The regulatory treatment therefore changes once the installation exceeds 10 kW.

Maximum Electrical Power
Maximum generation capacity excluded from the prior authorization regime:

10 kW

Article 49 of Law No. 053-2012/AN excludes installations with generation capacity less than or equal to 10 kW from the prior authorization regime specified for the second segment.

The relevant statutory threshold for the comparative database is therefore 10 kW.

Above 10 kW, the regulatory regime changes and authorization requirements may apply.
