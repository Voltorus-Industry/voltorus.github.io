Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Guinea and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Guinea is subject to the national electricity regulatory framework and the competent electricity authorities.

The legal treatment of an isolated electricity generator depends on the nature of the generation, its capacity, its location and whether electricity is supplied to third parties.

No verified numerical capacity threshold specific to a completely isolated gravity-powered self-consumption facility has been identified in the sources reviewed.
