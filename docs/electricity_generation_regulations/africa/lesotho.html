Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Lesotho and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity-sector activities in Lesotho are regulated by the Lesotho Electricity and Water Authority (LEWA).

The licence application rules provide specific exemptions from licensing, including an exemption for a person generating electricity which does not exceed 2 MW.

The exemption may be granted with terms and conditions and should therefore be confirmed with LEWA for the specific project.

Administrative Requirements

LEWA regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution and supply in Lesotho.

Generation Licence Exemption

LEWA states that a person who generates electricity which does not exceed 2 MW may be exempted from the licensing requirement.

The applicable licence-exemption rules also identify a person generating electricity which does not exceed 2 MW as a category that may be exempted.

The exemption may be subject to terms and conditions imposed by the Authority.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • LEWA exemption or licensing determination.
  • Construction and building approvals.
  • Land-use and planning requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Water authorization where applicable.
  • Structural engineering requirements.
  • Occupational health and safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep installed generation capacity at or below 2 MW if relying on the identified generation exemption.
  • Obtain confirmation of the exemption from LEWA before construction.
  • Maintain completely isolated operation where intended.
  • No electricity import from the public grid.
  • No electricity export to the public grid.
  • No electricity sale to third parties unless separately authorized.
  • Electrical protection should prevent accidental grid connection.
  • Comply with all conditions attached to any LEWA exemption.
