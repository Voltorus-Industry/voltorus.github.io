Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Malawi and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements.

Malawi's principal electricity legislation includes the Electricity Act and its subsequent amendments.

The licensing provisions specifically identify electricity generation for sale as an activity requiring a generation licence.

The legislation reviewed does not establish a specific nationwide kW exemption threshold for a completely isolated self-consumption installation that generates electricity solely for its owner's own use.

Administrative Requirements

The Electricity Act requires licences for specified electricity supply activities, including generation of electricity for sale.

A purely isolated self-consumption installation does not fit the statutory description of generation for sale.

However, this does not eliminate other regulatory obligations that may apply to the physical facility.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Electricity-sector authorization where applicable.
  • Construction approval.
  • Land-use requirements.

  • Environmental requirements.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Install electrical protection preventing accidental connection to the public network.
  • Obtain confirmation from the competent electricity authority concerning the project's classification.
  • Verify construction and environmental requirements before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Malawi-wide self-consumption threshold:

No specific kW/MW threshold for a completely isolated self-consumption generation facility was identified in the electricity licensing provisions reviewed.

The licensing provisions expressly require a generation licence for generation of electricity for sale.

Therefore, a numerical generation-licence threshold should not be invented for a facility that produces electricity exclusively for its own consumption.
