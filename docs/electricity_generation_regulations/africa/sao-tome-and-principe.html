Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in São Tomé and Príncipe and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

The electricity sector of São Tomé and Príncipe is principally governed by the Regime Jurídico do Sector Eléctrico (RJSE) , established by Decreto-Lei n.º 26/2014 .

The RJSE establishes a general licensing framework for electricity production and distinguishes production for own consumption from independent electricity production.

Article 52 of Decreto-Lei n.º 26/2014 specifically regulates produção para consumo próprio (production for own consumption).

The legal framework identifies the following own-consumption configurations:

  • Production units with a capacity below 7.5 kVA .
  • Production units with a capacity above 7.5 kVA that are not connected to the public grid.
  • Grid-connected production units where more than 60% of installed capacity is intended for the producer's own consumption.

Production for own consumption is subject to an authorization regime rather than the ordinary independent-production licensing regime.

Production units below 7.5 kVA fall below the authorization threshold applicable to the RJSE's own-consumption production regime.

For a completely isolated gravity-powered installation, the applicable legal treatment must therefore be assessed according to its installed capacity, grid status, intended use, technology, location and the authorization requirements of the competent authorities.

Administrative Requirements

The RJSE establishes a general licensing framework for electricity production activities in São Tomé and Príncipe.

Production for own consumption is treated separately from independent electricity production.

Article 52 provides that production units intended for own consumption are subject to an authorization regime and are exempt from the ordinary obligation to obtain an electricity-production licence before commencing operations.

Own-Consumption Authorization

Article 52 of the RJSE establishes authorization for production units intended for own consumption.

The competent government authority is responsible for issuing the authorization, with the regulatory authority responsible for defining and supervising the applicable authorization procedures.

For a completely isolated installation, electricity is consumed at the producer's premises and is not supplied through the public electricity network.

The absence of a public-grid connection does not remove applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety, land-use and technical requirements.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the São Tomé and Príncipe public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity is supplied to third-party consumers.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-consumption facility.
  • The nominal apparent power is documented precisely in kVA.
  • Where the project is intended to remain below the Article 52 threshold, its rated apparent power is maintained below 7.5 kVA.
  • Any project exceeding 7.5 kVA is reviewed with the competent authorities before construction and operation.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, structural and safety requirements are independently verified.

Article 52 of Decreto-Lei n.º 26/2014 identifies 7.5 kVA as the relevant capacity threshold for production for own consumption.

The RJSE establishes a specific legal regime for electricity production for own consumption.

Article 52 identifies production units with a capacity below 7.5 kVA and production units above 7.5 kVA that are not connected to the public grid within the own-consumption framework.

For the specific website category representing a completely isolated facility operating below the authorization threshold, the maximum practical value to display is 7.49 kVA .

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

São Tomé and Príncipe electricity legislation does not establish a separate statutory capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The applicable regulatory treatment is determined according to the installation's capacity, grid connection status, intended use and legal classification.

The proposed installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island exclusively for internal consumption.

The legal threshold is expressed in kVA rather than kW.

Therefore, without making an unsupported power-factor conversion, the appropriate website value below the 7.5 kVA threshold is 7.49 kVA .

Website classification for São Tomé and Príncipe:

7.49 kVA

Use 7.49 kVA as the maximum apparent generating capacity for the website category representing a purely self-consumption installation below the 7.5 kVA threshold.

An isolated installation above 7.5 kVA falls within the Article 52 own-consumption authorization framework.

The 7.49 kVA figure is a regulatory threshold classification for the website.

It should not be interpreted as a technology-specific mechanical or electrical performance limitation for gravity-powered electricity generation.
