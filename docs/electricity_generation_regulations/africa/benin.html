Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Benin and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, agricultural, commercial or service requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation and self-production are regulated under the Electricity Code of the Republic of Benin and its implementing regulations.

Decree No. 2021-596 of 12 November 2021 establishes the declaration and authorization procedures applicable to electricity self-production installations.

The current regulatory framework distinguishes self-production installations according to installed capacity, with declaration and authorization regimes applying above defined thresholds.

Administrative Requirements

Benin has established a specific regulatory framework for electricity self-production.

Self-production up to 50 kVA

The current capacity-based framework begins the declaration regime above 50 kVA.

Accordingly, installations with capacity up to and including 50 kVA fall below the declaration threshold established for self-production.

Self-production from above 50 to below 500 kVA

Self-production installations with capacity above 50 kVA and below 500 kVA are subject to the declaration regime.

The declaration must be made to the Autorité de Régulation de l'Électricité before the installation is placed into service.

Self-production above 500 kVA

Self-production installations with capacity above 500 kVA require an authorization.

The authorization procedure includes technical and safety information concerning the proposed installation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • No physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity import.
  • No electricity export.
  • No electricity sale to third parties.
  • Electricity used exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Keep the installation at or below 50 kVA if the objective is to remain below the declaration threshold.
  • Install appropriate electrical protection.
  • Comply with applicable construction, environmental and safety requirements.

For a completely isolated self-consumption facility, the relevant capacity threshold below which the declaration regime does not apply is 50 kVA.

A facility above 50 kVA enters the declaration regime, while a facility above 500 kVA enters the authorization regime.
