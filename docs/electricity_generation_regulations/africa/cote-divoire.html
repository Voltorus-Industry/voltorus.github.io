Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Côte d'Ivoire and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Côte d'Ivoire's electricity sector is principally governed by Law No. 2014-132 of 24 March 2014 establishing the Electricity Code.

The Electricity Code expressly recognizes "autoproduction" as electricity production principally intended for the producer's own consumption.

Article 8 establishes freedom, declaration and authorization regimes according to installed capacity.

The Directorate-General for Energy specifies the applicable self-production thresholds, including different thresholds for conventional energy and new and renewable energy.

Administrative Requirements

Côte d'Ivoire expressly regulates electricity self-production according to installed capacity.

Self-production Capacity Thresholds

According to the Directorate-General for Energy, prior authorization is required for cumulative installed self-production capacity above:

  • 20 kW for conventional-energy self-production.
  • 400 kW for new and renewable-energy self-production.

The classification of a particular gravity system should therefore be confirmed before relying on the 400 kW renewable-energy threshold.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is consumed exclusively by the producer.
  • The system operates in island mode.
  • Appropriate electrical protection prevents accidental grid connection.
  • The installed capacity is documented precisely.
  • The technology is legally classified before selecting the applicable capacity threshold.
Maximum Electrical Power
Relevant renewable self-production threshold:

400 kW is the threshold identified by the Directorate-General for Energy above which prior authorization applies to self-production based on new and renewable energy.

This threshold should not be treated as a universal technical limit for gravity technology.

The Electricity Code defines new and renewable energy and specifically addresses hydraulic energy with a distinction at 10 MW.

The legal classification of a gravity-powered system should therefore be confirmed with the competent Ivorian energy authority before applying the 400 kW threshold.

Demonstrated Gravity-Technology Benchmark

The Rudong EVx gravity-energy-storage facility in China has demonstrated a peak electrical output of 25 MW and an energy capacity of 100 MWh.

This 25 MW value is a technology benchmark, not an Ivorian legal exemption threshold.
