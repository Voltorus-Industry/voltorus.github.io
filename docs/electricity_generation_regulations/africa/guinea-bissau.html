Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Guinea-Bissau and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation and supply activities in Guinea-Bissau are subject to the national electricity regulatory framework.

No reliable numerical capacity threshold specific to a completely isolated gravity-powered self-consumption facility has been identified in the sources reviewed.
