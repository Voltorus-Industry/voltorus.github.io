Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Burkina Faso and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively by the owner for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements.

Burkina Faso's electricity sector is principally governed by Law No. 014-2017/AN of 20 April 2017 on the general regulation of the energy sector.

The electricity framework regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution, exploitation, import, export, purchase and sale, as well as the promotion of renewable energy and energy efficiency.

Burkina Faso has established different regulatory regimes for electricity-generation and self-production installations according to their installed capacity and energy source.

The current regulatory framework also includes Decree No. 2024-0977/PRES/PM/MEMC/MEFP/MDICAPME concerning renewable-energy self-production, access of renewable-energy self-producers to the electricity network and the purchase of surplus electricity.

A completely isolated self-consumption installation must therefore be assessed according to its actual technology, legal classification, installed electrical capacity and operating configuration, together with applicable environmental, construction, land-use and safety requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity generation in Burkina Faso is subject to a regulatory regime based on the nature of the installation and its installed capacity.

The regulatory framework distinguishes between declaration, authorisation and licensing regimes for electricity-production installations.

For self-production installations using renewable energy sources, the regulatory framework identifies a threshold of 500 kW.

  • Self-production installations using renewable energy with an installed maximum capacity up to 500 kW fall within the declaration regime under the applicable electricity framework.
  • Renewable-energy self-production installations above 500 kW fall within the authorisation regime.
  • Renewable-energy installations strictly for personal use with an installed capacity up to 5 kW were identified in the regulatory framework as not being subject to declaration.
  • The 2024 renewable self-production framework specifically regulates renewable-energy self-producers and their relationship with the electricity network.

The 2024 framework also provides specific rules for renewable-energy self-production installations according to their electrical capacity and connection configuration.

In particular, renewable-energy self-production installations for domestic or professional use with a power of up to 500 kW may be connected in low voltage under the conditions established by the applicable regulation.

Because gravity-powered electricity generation is not expressly identified as a separate energy-source category in the provisions reviewed, the competent Burkinabè authority should confirm that the proposed gravity technology qualifies for the renewable-energy self-production regime before the 500 kW threshold is applied to the project.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical separation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for the owner's internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.
  • Document the installed electrical capacity of the generation installation.
  • Determine whether the gravity-generation system qualifies legally as a renewable-energy self-production installation.
  • If classified as renewable-energy self-production, determine whether the installation is at or below the 500 kW regulatory threshold.
  • Verify whether the installation requires a declaration or prior authorisation before construction or operation.
  • Verify applicable environmental requirements before construction.
  • Verify construction, land-use and structural requirements applicable to the site.
  • Verify electrical safety and technical requirements applicable to the installation.
Maximum Electrical Power
Applicable regulatory threshold:

500 kW

Under the Burkinabè electricity framework, 500 kW is the principal threshold identified for renewable-energy self-production installations.

The electricity-sector regulatory framework provides that self-production installations using renewable energy with an installed maximum capacity of up to 500 kW fall under the declaration regime.

Renewable-energy self-production installations above 500 kW fall under the authorisation regime.

The framework also identifies a specific personal-use threshold of 5 kW for renewable-energy installations that are strictly for personal use.

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

The legislation reviewed does not expressly identify "gravity-powered electricity generation" as a separate regulatory technology category.

Consequently, the 500 kW figure should be treated as the applicable regulatory threshold for the project only if the competent Burkinabè authority classifies the proposed gravity system as a renewable-energy self-production installation.

The 500 kW figure is therefore a regulatory capacity threshold for determining the applicable administrative regime, rather than a technical performance specification of the gravity-generation technology.

Website classification for Burkina Faso:

500 kW

Use 500 kW as the principal regulatory threshold for a gravity-powered self-consumption facility where the installation is legally classified as renewable-energy self-production.

The project's technology classification should be confirmed with the competent Burkinabè energy authority before relying on this threshold.

The separate 5 kW threshold concerns renewable-energy installations strictly for personal use and should not be confused with the principal 500 kW self-production regulatory threshold.

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. The 500 kW and 5 kW figures identify regulatory capacity thresholds applicable to renewable-energy self-production categories identified in the Burkinabè electricity framework. The legislation reviewed does not create a separate capacity category specifically named "gravity-powered electricity generation".
