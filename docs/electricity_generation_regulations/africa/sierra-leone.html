Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Sierra Leone and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity generation in Sierra Leone is regulated by the national electricity legislation and the regulatory framework administered by the Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission (SLEWRC) .

The current regulatory framework includes the Sierra Leone Electricity and Water Regulatory Commission Act, 2025 , together with applicable electricity regulations, licensing rules and regulatory instruments.

Sierra Leone maintains a specific regulatory regime for captive electricity generation where electricity is generated for the generator's own use and is not sold to third parties.

The Captive Electricity Generation Permit Rules establish a threshold of 10 kVA .

A person may not install or operate an electricity generation unit with a rated output of 10 kVA or above without the required captive-generation permit.

The Sierra Leone regulator confirmed in June 2026 that captive-generation licences are mandatory for operators using generators of 10 kVA and above .

For a completely isolated gravity-powered self-consumption installation intended to remain below this threshold, the maximum practical capacity to display is 9.99 kVA .

Administrative Requirements

Sierra Leone regulates captive electricity generation separately from ordinary electricity generation activities.

Captive power generation means generation for consumption by the permit holder itself and not for sale to a third party.

The Captive Electricity Generation Permit Rules establish that the rules apply to captive generators with a rated output of 10 kVA and above .

A person must not install or operate a generation unit with a rated output of 10 kVA or above without the required captive-generation permit.

Sierra Leone's Captive Electricity Generation Permit Rules establish a threshold of 10 kVA . A generation unit rated at 10 kVA or above requires the applicable captive-generation permit.

The rules define captive power generation as electricity generation exceeding 10 kVA for consumption by the permit holder and not for sale to a third party.

The rules further state that a person must not install or operate a generation unit with a rated output of 10 kVA and above unless that person holds a captive-generation permit.

The Sierra Leone regulator confirmed this regulatory threshold in its June 2026 compliance enforcement, stating that captive-generation licences are mandatory for generators of 10 kVA and above .

Specific Application to Gravity Technology

Sierra Leone's captive-generation framework does not establish a separate capacity threshold specifically named for "gravity-powered electricity generation".

The relevant classification is based on the installation's rated electrical output and whether the electricity is generated for the producer's own consumption.

The proposed installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island exclusively for internal consumption.

For the website category representing operation below the captive-generation permit threshold, the maximum practical value is 9.99 kVA .
