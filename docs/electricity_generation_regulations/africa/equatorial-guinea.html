Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Equatorial Guinea and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Electricity-sector activities in Equatorial Guinea are subject to State regulation. Electricity supply services are identified as public-sector reserved services, while the State may delegate, grant concessions or associate with private initiatives under the conditions established by law.

Publicly available official material confirms that Equatorial Guinea has been developing and reforming its electricity-sector legislation, including a proposed regulatory framework for the electricity sector and renewable-energy implementing regulations.

Because a current, authoritative capacity exemption specifically applicable to a completely isolated self-consumption generation installation could not be verified, the project should not be assumed to be exempt solely because it is off-grid.

Administrative Requirements

An off-grid installation should not be considered automatically exempt from administrative requirements in Equatorial Guinea.

Electricity supply services are treated as public services reserved to the State, subject to delegation, concessions or private participation under applicable legislation.

Official government material also confirms that the electricity sector has been subject to legislative reform and development of a specific regulatory framework.

Electricity-Sector Regulation

The Government has worked on a specific law regulating the electricity sector and on implementing regulations for renewable energy.

Consequently, the precise authorization, declaration or licensing regime applicable to a private off-grid generation facility should be confirmed with the competent electricity authority before construction.

No reliable nationwide kW/MW exemption threshold specifically applicable to an isolated self-consumption gravity-generation facility has been identified in the authoritative material reviewed.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal consumption.
  • The system operates in electrically isolated island mode.
  • Protection prevents accidental energisation of the public grid.
  • The facility is documented as a private self-consumption installation.
  • The competent electricity authority is consulted before construction.
  • Construction, environmental, land-use and safety requirements are independently verified.
Maximum Electrical Power
Equatorial Guinea legal maximum identified: No authoritative nationwide kW/MW exemption threshold specifically applicable to a completely isolated, self-consumption-only electricity-generation facility has been verified.

The absence of a verified exemption threshold does not mean that a facility may automatically operate at any particular capacity without authorization.

The applicable electricity-sector authorization must therefore be determined with the competent Equatorial Guinean authority for the exact project and technology.

Technology Benchmark

The largest commercial-scale non-pumped-hydro gravity energy storage installation verified in operation/commissioning is the Rudong EVx system in China.

Its published peak power output is 25 MW, with 100 MWh of storage capacity.

This is a technology benchmark and is not an Equatorial Guinean legal authorization threshold.
