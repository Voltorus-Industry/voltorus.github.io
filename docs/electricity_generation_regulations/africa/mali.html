Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Mali and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.

The Malian electricity sector is regulated through national electricity legislation and institutions including the Commission de Régulation de l'Électricité et de l'Eau (CREE).

The regulatory framework distinguishes electricity production activities, authorization regimes and self-producers.

No reliable current national kW exemption threshold specifically applicable to a completely isolated mechanical gravity self-consumption installation has been identified in the sources reviewed.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity production in Mali is subject to the national electricity regulatory framework.

Project documentation for electricity-generation facilities identifies production licensing and other administrative approvals as part of the authorization process.

Self-Consumption / Self-Production

The Malian electricity-sector framework recognizes self-producers and private electricity-sector operators.

However, no sufficiently reliable current provision was identified establishing a specific exemption in kW for a solid-mass gravity self-consumption installation operating entirely off-grid.

Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Electricity-sector authorization where applicable.
  • Construction permit.
  • Environmental authorization.
  • Land-use authorization.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Structural engineering requirements.
  • Water authorization if water is used.
  • Fire and occupational safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation completely physically isolated from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Install electrical protection preventing accidental connection to the public network.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the competent Malian electricity authority concerning the legal classification of the technology.
  • Verify construction and environmental requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power

No reliable current national kW/MW threshold specifically applicable to a completely isolated mechanical gravity self-consumption installation was identified.
