Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation and energy-storage facility located in Madagascar and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.

Madagascar's electricity framework specifically recognizes electricity self-production ("autoproduction") and establishes capacity thresholds for certain categories of generating installations.

The legal framework distinguishes thermal and hydroelectric self-production installations.

A mechanical gravity installation using elevated solid masses does not fall naturally within either category. Its classification must therefore be confirmed with the competent Malagasy electricity authority before construction.

Administrative Requirements

Malagasy electricity legislation establishes specific regimes for self-production installations.

Autoproduction Thresholds
  • Thermal self-production installations with installed capacity up to 1 MW are subject to declaration.
  • Hydroelectric self-production installations with installed capacity up to 500 kW are subject to declaration.
  • Thermal installations strictly for personal use with installed capacity up to 10 kW are exempt from the declaration requirement specified in Article 27.
  • Larger installations are subject to the authorization regime provided by the electricity legislation.
Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Electricity-sector declaration or authorization.
  • Construction authorization.
  • Environmental authorization.
  • Water authorization if water resources are used.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Structural engineering requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Land-use requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Obtain a written classification of the technology as a mechanical gravity installation before construction.
  • Confirm whether the competent authority applies the thermal, hydroelectric or another category.
  • Verify environmental and construction requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Gravity-specific regulatory maximum:

No separate kW/MW threshold specifically applicable to a solid-mass mechanical gravity electricity installation was identified.

Madagascar establishes a 500 kW declaration threshold for hydroelectric self-production and a 1 MW declaration threshold for thermal self-production.

These figures cannot automatically be applied to a mechanical gravity installation using solid masses.

Important Technology Classification

If the proposed gravity system is actually a pumped-hydroelectric system, the hydroelectric threshold of 500 kW may become relevant.

If the system uses solid masses, cranes, cables and a generator, the legislation reviewed does not provide a specific gravity-storage threshold.

The competent authority should therefore provide a project-specific classification.
