Overview

Electricity activities in Suriname are regulated under the Elektriciteitswet 2016.

The law forms part of the modern legal framework for electricity generation, supply and supervision in Suriname.

The sector is supervised through the Energie Autoriteit Suriname (EAS).

A private isolated installation designed exclusively for internal consumption must be distinguished from the commercial supply of electricity to third parties.

The applicable authorization, technical and safety requirements must nevertheless be confirmed with the competent Surinamese electricity authority before construction.

Administrative Requirements

The Elektriciteitswet 2016 establishes the statutory framework for electricity-sector activities and supervision.

The EAS is the designated electricity-sector authority for supervision and regulatory functions.

  • Confirm whether the proposed private generation requires registration or authorization.
  • Verify the applicable technical requirements.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify construction requirements.
  • Verify electrical and occupational safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Document the facility as private self-consumption.
  • Obtain written confirmation of the applicable authorization requirements from EAS.
Maximum Electrical Power
No general gravity-specific MW threshold identified in the available statutory framework.

The Elektriciteitswet 2016 does not establish a gravity-specific statutory generating-capacity limit.

The legal classification should therefore be based on the nature of the activity, its connection status, its purpose and the applicable authorization regime.

Project-specific confirmation should be obtained from the competent Surinamese authorities before construction.
