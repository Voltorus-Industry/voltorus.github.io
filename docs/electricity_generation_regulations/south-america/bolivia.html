Overview

Electricity generation in Bolivia is principally regulated by Law No. 1604 of 21 December 1994 — Ley de Electricidad and its implementing regulations.

The law expressly defines Autoproducción as generation intended exclusively for the producer's own use.

The law therefore expressly recognizes the concept of electricity generation for exclusive self-consumption.

The proposed installation would operate as a completely isolated electrical island, with no connection to the public grid and no electricity import or export.

Article 25 establishes that electricity production for exclusive use of the producer below the regulatory limits does not require a concession or licence, provided that the applicable technical, environmental and cultural-heritage requirements are satisfied.

Administrative Requirements

Article 23 of Law No. 1604 requires a licence for electricity generation where the generating capacity exceeds the minimum limits established by regulation.

Article 25 expressly excludes certain activities from the concession and licensing requirements.

Self-consumption exemption

Article 25 provides that electricity production for supply to third parties or for exclusive use of the producer, when carried out below the limits established by regulation, does not require a concession or licence.

This provision is directly relevant to an isolated self-consumption facility.

The precise applicable capacity limit must, however, be verified against the implementing regulation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for the producer's own consumption.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not export electricity to the public network.
  • Prevent accidental grid interconnection.
  • Determine the exact regulatory capacity threshold.
  • If the threshold is exceeded, obtain the required generation licence before operation.
  • Verify environmental and cultural-heritage requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Bolivia — statutory structure:

No universal MW value is specified directly in Article 25 of Law No. 1604.

Article 23 establishes that generation requires a licence when the power exceeds the minimum limits established by regulation.

Article 25 provides the corresponding exemption for production for exclusive producer use below the regulatory limits.

The precise MW/kW threshold must therefore be determined from the implementing regulation rather than inferred from the wording of the Law alone.

Public Information Notice

Bolivia expressly recognizes electricity autoproduction for exclusive use of the producer. Under Article 23 of Law No. 1604, generation above regulatory minimum limits requires a licence. Article 25 provides that electricity production for exclusive use of the producer below the applicable regulatory limits does not require a concession or licence, subject to technical, environmental and cultural- heritage requirements. The precise capacity threshold must be verified in the implementing regulations.
