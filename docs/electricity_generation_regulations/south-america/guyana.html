Overview

Electricity generation and supply in Guyana are principally regulated by the Electricity Sector Reform Act 1999, Act No. 11 of 1999.

The Act establishes the legal framework for licences concerning electricity supply and generation.

The legislation also contains provisions concerning private suppliers and electricity generation capacity used by a person for supplying electricity to himself and certain persons in the vicinity of the generating source.

A genuinely private isolated installation intended solely for the operator's own consumption therefore requires analysis under the self-supply provisions rather than assuming that it is equivalent to a public electricity supplier.

The Electricity Sector Reform Act must also be read together with the Public Utilities Commission Act, the Guyana Energy Agency Act and environmental legislation.

Administrative Requirements

The Electricity Sector Reform Act establishes a licensing framework for electricity supply.

The Act contains provisions dealing with private suppliers using generation capacity for their own electricity requirements and defines conditions under which electricity may be supplied to specified persons in the vicinity.

  • Determine whether the installation falls within the private generation/self-supply provisions.
  • Verify whether a licence or exemption is required before construction.
  • Verify electrical technical standards.
  • Verify environmental requirements under the Environmental Protection Act.
  • Verify construction and occupational safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation electrically independent from the public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to the public.
  • Use electricity exclusively for the operator's own premises and activities.
  • Avoid electricity distribution to unrelated third parties.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the competent authority concerning the applicable licence regime.
  • Complete environmental and safety assessments before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
No general gravity-specific statutory MW threshold identified.

The Electricity Sector Reform Act does not establish a general capacity limit specifically for gravity-powered generation.

The legal treatment depends on the status of the generator, the purpose of generation, the supply of electricity and the applicable licence.

A project-specific determination should therefore be obtained from the competent Guyanese authorities before construction.
