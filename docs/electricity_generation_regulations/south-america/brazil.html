Overview

Electricity generation in Brazil is principally regulated by Federal Law No. 9,074 of 7 July 1995, together with the electricity-sector legislation administered by ANEEL.

Brazilian legislation expressly recognizes autoprodução de energia elétrica, meaning electricity generation for the producer's own account and risk.

The proposed facility is intended to operate as a completely isolated electrical island and to use electricity exclusively for internal consumption.

No electricity would be imported from or exported to a public electricity network.

The applicable authorization depends upon the generating technology, capacity and applicable provisions of the Federal electricity framework.

Administrative Requirements

Article 4 of Law No. 9,074 establishes the framework for concessions, permissions and authorizations relating to electricity services and installations.

Autoproduction

Article 16-B defines an electricity autoproducer as a consumer holding an authorization/outorga for a generation undertaking to produce electricity for its own account and risk.

Article 7 provides that certain thermoelectric plants exceeding 5,000 kW intended for exclusive autoproducer use are subject to authorization.

Article 7 also provides authorization for certain hydraulic potentials exceeding 5,000 kW and up to 50,000 kW intended for exclusive autoproducer use or independent production.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Install protection against unintended interconnection.
  • Determine the exact generating technology classification under Brazilian law.
  • Determine whether the project falls below or above the applicable 5 MW threshold.
  • Where applicable, communicate the project to the granting authority.
  • Obtain the applicable ANEEL authorization where required.
Maximum Electrical Power
Brazil — principal statutory threshold:

5,000 kW (5 MW) for the categories specifically covered by Articles 7 and 8 of Law No. 9,074.

Article 8 provides that hydroelectric potentials and thermoelectric plants with capacity equal to or below 5,000 kW are exempt from concession, permission or authorization, subject to the statutory communication requirement.

Article 7 establishes authorization requirements above 5,000 kW for specified thermoelectric and hydraulic autoproduction projects.

These thresholds are technology-specific. They should not automatically be applied to a gravity-generation technology unless the competent Brazilian authority classifies the installation under one of those statutory categories.

A gravity-powered project should therefore obtain a formal technology classification before relying on the 5 MW provisions.

Public Information Notice

Brazilian electricity legislation expressly recognizes electricity autoproduction. Law No. 9,074 provides specific authorization and exemption thresholds, including the 5,000 kW threshold for categories specified in Articles 7 and 8. The exact treatment of a gravity-powered generation facility depends on its legal and technical classification. A completely isolated self-consumption project should therefore be classified with ANEEL and the competent authority before construction or operation.
