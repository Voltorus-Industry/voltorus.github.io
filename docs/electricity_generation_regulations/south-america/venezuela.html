Overview

Electricity generation in Venezuela is regulated under the Ley del Servicio Eléctrico / Ley Orgánica del Servicio Eléctrico.

Article 24 provides that electricity generation is open to competition subject to prior authorization by the competent electricity authority.

The same provision expressly defines autogeneración as electricity generation intended exclusively for the use of the natural or legal person carrying it out.

Article 24 provides that autogeneration is exempt from the general regulation, subject to the exceptions established by the law.

Article 43 nevertheless establishes a prior authorization regime for companies carrying out generation, including autogeneration and cogeneration, while allowing the competent authority to exempt installations which, considering their characteristics, do not require authorization.

Venezuela therefore contains an unusually explicit statutory distinction between public electricity generation and private autogeneration.

Administrative Requirements

Article 24 establishes the general authorization framework for electricity generation and specifically addresses autogeneration.

Article 43 provides that companies carrying out generation, including autogeneration and cogeneration, require prior authorization from the competent electricity authority.

Article 43 also provides that the authority may exempt owners of electricity-generation installations when, considering the characteristics of the installations, authorization is not required.

  • Establish that the installation qualifies as autogeneration.
  • Obtain written confirmation concerning the applicable authorization or exemption.
  • Verify technical installation requirements.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify construction and safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Operate exclusively for the operator's own electricity requirements.
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the national electricity system.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Obtain written confirmation of the autogeneration classification.
  • Request confirmation whether the installation requires authorization under Article 43 or qualifies for an exemption.
  • Maintain complete technical and environmental documentation.
Maximum Electrical Power
No general gravity-specific statutory MW threshold identified.

The law does not establish a special maximum generating capacity for gravity-powered electricity generation.

Instead, the legal framework focuses on the activity being performed, including generation, autogeneration and connection to the national electricity system.

Article 43 allows the competent authority to exempt certain generation installations where their characteristics mean that authorization is not required.

Consequently, the appropriate legal approach for a private isolated installation is to obtain a formal determination of its autogeneration status and of whether Article 43 authorization or an exemption applies.
