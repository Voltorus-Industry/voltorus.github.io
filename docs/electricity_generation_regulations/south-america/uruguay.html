Overview

Uruguay's electricity-generation framework is based principally on Law No. 16.832, which established a new legal framework for the national electricity system.

Article 1 of Law No. 16.832 establishes the principle of freedom of generation.

Generation is excluded from the public-service characterization applicable to transmission, transformation and distribution activities when those activities are supplied regularly and permanently to third parties.

Uruguay nevertheless maintains authorization and registration procedures for electricity-generation facilities.

The official government procedure expressly covers generation plants installed at consumer premises for self-consumption, including plants that operate isolated from the electricity network.

Therefore, off-grid operation does not automatically eliminate the requirement to register or obtain the applicable authorization.

Administrative Requirements

Uruguay operates a formal authorization framework for electricity generation.

The official government procedure for generation by a consumer states that registration or authorization is required for electricity-generation plants for self-consumption, whether connected to the Interconnection Network or operating in isolation.

  • Determine whether registration or authorization is required for the proposed plant.
  • Submit the required technical information.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify construction requirements.
  • Verify electrical safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the facility electrically isolated.
  • Do not inject electricity into the network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Register the facility under the applicable self-consumption regime.
  • Maintain technical documentation describing island operation.
  • Obtain all environmental and construction approvals.
Maximum Electrical Power
No general gravity-specific statutory MW ceiling identified.

Law No. 16.832 establishes freedom of generation but does not establish a gravity-specific maximum generating capacity.

The applicable authorization depends on the nature of the generating facility and its participation in the electricity system.

For an isolated self-consumption installation, the official registration/authorization procedure remains relevant regardless of the absence of a public-grid connection.
