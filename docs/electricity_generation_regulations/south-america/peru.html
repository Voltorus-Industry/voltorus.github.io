Overview

Electricity generation in Peru is principally governed by Decreto Ley N.º 25844 — Ley de Concesiones Eléctricas.

The law establishes concession and authorization regimes for electricity activities.

Generation projects can therefore be subject to different legal procedures depending on their technology, installed capacity and characteristics.

The legislation contains a specific simplified regime for generation from Renewable Energy Resources with installed capacity equal to or below 20 MW.

This 20 MW provision is not a general exemption for every electricity-generation technology.

A fully isolated self-consumption project must therefore be classified according to its technology, capacity, resources and connection status.

Administrative Requirements

The Electricity Concessions Law establishes procedures for obtaining electricity concessions and authorizations.

Article 25 establishes requirements for definitive concessions, subject to the specific exception for renewable generation with installed capacity equal to or below 20 MW.

Article 38 establishes requirements concerning authorizations, including technical, environmental, project and financial documentation.

  • Determine whether the project requires a concession or authorization.
  • Determine whether the project qualifies for the specific renewable-energy regime.
  • Prepare the required technical project documents.
  • Demonstrate compliance with environmental requirements.
  • Obtain applicable construction and electrical safety approvals.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Clearly document the facility as isolated.
  • Do not connect the installation to the public electricity system unless specifically authorized.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Document the electricity as internal self-consumption.
  • Determine whether the technology legally qualifies as a Renewable Energy Resource.
  • Verify the applicable concession/authorization before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Specific renewable-generation threshold: 20 MW.

The Electricity Concessions Law provides a specific regime for generation from Renewable Energy Resources with installed capacity equal to or below 20 MW.

This threshold is associated with the statutory renewable-generation regime and should not be treated as a universal maximum generation capacity in Peru.

A gravity-powered installation must first be legally classified according to its energy source and technology before the 20 MW provision can be relied upon.
