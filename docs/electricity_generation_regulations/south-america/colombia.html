Overview

Electricity generation in Colombia is principally governed by Law 143 of 1994, together with Law 1715 of 2014, the electricity-sector regulations issued by the Ministry of Mines and Energy and CREG regulations.

Colombian legislation recognizes autogeneration as electricity production principally intended to meet the producer's own energy requirements.

The proposed installation is designed as a completely isolated electrical island.

It would not be synchronized with the external grid, would not import electricity and would not export electricity.

The CREG has expressly stated that a completely isolated off-grid autogeneration system does not fall under the grid autogeneration regulation because it cannot affect the external electricity network.

Administrative Requirements

Law 143 of 1994 establishes the legal regime for generation, interconnection, transmission, distribution and commercialization of electricity in Colombia.

Law 1715 of 2014 promotes self-generation at small and large scale and distributed generation.

Completely Isolated Generation

CREG Concept 5858 of 2024 expressly considered a proposed isolated autogeneration system without grid connection.

CREG stated that where the autogeneration system is completely isolated, is not synchronized with the external network and supplies circuits independently from that network, the autogeneration regulation does not apply because the system cannot affect the external grid.

This position is directly relevant to a genuinely off-grid installation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the external electricity network.
  • Do not synchronize the installation with the external network.
  • Do not import electricity from the grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Install electrical protection preventing accidental network connection.
  • Maintain technical documentation proving electrical isolation.
  • Comply with RETIE and other applicable electrical safety requirements.
  • Verify environmental and construction permits.
Maximum Electrical Power
Colombia — completely isolated generation:

No general MW ceiling identified for a completely isolated installation solely because it is off-grid.

Colombian regulation distinguishes between autogeneration connected to the electricity system and a completely isolated system.

CREG has expressly stated that a completely isolated autogeneration system is not subject to the grid autogeneration regulation because it cannot affect the external network.

The 1 MW threshold used for classification of small-scale autogeneration is therefore not a general maximum capacity for a completely isolated facility.

The project should nevertheless comply with electrical safety, environmental, construction and other non-grid-specific requirements.

Public Information Notice

Colombia provides a particularly clear regulatory distinction for completely isolated electricity generation. CREG has expressly stated that an autogeneration system that is completely isolated, not synchronized with the external grid and unable to affect that grid is not subject to the grid autogeneration regulation. The project must nevertheless comply with applicable electrical-safety, environmental, construction, land-use and other requirements. The 1 MW threshold used for small-scale autogeneration connected to the regulatory framework should not be treated as a general capacity ceiling for a genuinely off-grid installation.
