Overview

Ecuador regulates electricity generation principally through the Ley Orgánica del Servicio Público de Energía Eléctrica (LOSPEE).

The law distinguishes electricity generation from autogeneration. Electricity generation is subject to authorization through the applicable electricity-sector habilitating title.

Article 40 of the LOSPEE provides that electricity generation may be carried out by public companies, mixed-economy companies and other private or popular and solidarity-economy legal entities duly authorized by the competent authority.

Article 41 specifically regulates autogeneration and provides that autogeneration and its electricity surpluses are subject to the regulation issued by ARCONEL.

Accordingly, an installation intended exclusively for internal consumption must be analyzed under the autogeneration regime rather than automatically being treated as a conventional electricity-generation project.

The fact that a facility is electrically isolated from the public network does not by itself eliminate environmental, construction, safety or electricity-sector requirements.

Administrative Requirements

Article 40 of the LOSPEE provides that electricity generation must be carried out by entities duly habilitated by the competent authority.

Article 41 provides a specific legal framework for autogeneration. Autogeneration and its electricity surpluses are governed by the regulation issued by ARCONEL.

  • Verification of the applicable autogeneration regime.
  • Verification of the applicable electricity-sector authorization or registration.
  • Compliance with technical electricity regulations.
  • Compliance with environmental requirements.
  • Compliance with construction and land-use rules.
  • Compliance with electrical and occupational safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not inject electricity into the public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Document the installation as an autogeneration facility for internal consumption.
  • Verify the applicable ARCONEL regulation before construction.
  • Obtain all environmental and construction approvals applicable to the project.
Maximum Electrical Power
No general gravity-specific MW threshold identified in the LOSPEE.

The LOSPEE does not establish a special electricity generation capacity threshold specifically for gravity-powered generation.

The applicable treatment depends principally on whether the project constitutes generation or autogeneration and on the applicable regulatory framework.

The project capacity should therefore be determined from the engineering design and submitted to the competent authorities for classification and approval.
