Overview

Electricity generation in Honduras is governed principally by the Ley General de la Industria Eléctrica, enacted by Decreto No. 404-2013 and subsequently amended.

Article 1 expressly states that the law regulates the activities of generation, transmission, distribution and commercialization of electricity in the territory of Honduras.

The law defines generation as the production of electricity through the exploitation and transformation of energy from different types of sources.

The Comisión Reguladora de Energía Eléctrica (CREE) is the principal electricity regulatory authority created by the law.

Electricity generation is therefore part of the regulated electricity sector even where a project is designed for private consumption.

No general statutory MW threshold has been identified in Decreto 404-2013 that can safely be presented as a universal exemption from the electricity-sector regulatory framework for an isolated generation facility.

Administrative Requirements
  • Registration within the Public Registry of Electricity-Sector Companies where the project constitutes a regulated electricity-sector enterprise.
  • Compliance with the Ley General de la Industria Eléctrica and applicable regulations.
  • Compliance with technical requirements applicable to generating facilities.
  • Environmental authorization where applicable.
  • Construction and operating authorizations applicable to the project and site.
  • Connection authorization and technical requirements where connection to the national system is proposed.
  • Additional CREE requirements applicable to generators.
Important Off-grid Point

The absence of a physical connection to the national electricity system should not by itself be treated as proof that the installation is outside the electricity-sector legislation. The legal status must be confirmed with the CREE according to the ownership, purpose, capacity and operating configuration of the project.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation physically isolated from the national electricity system.
  • Prohibit electricity export.
  • Prohibit electricity sales to third parties.
  • Document the project as an internal-use facility.
  • Document installed capacity and maximum output.
  • Obtain a written regulatory determination from CREE concerning the project's status.
  • Verify environmental and construction permits before construction.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of an external grid.

No general MW exemption identified in Decreto 404-2013

Article 1 regulates electricity generation without establishing a general generation-capacity threshold that can be used as a universal exemption for isolated self-consumption facilities.

The capacity of a proposed project should therefore be stated precisely and submitted to the competent authority for classification.

Public Information Notice

Honduras regulates electricity generation under the Ley General de la Industria Eléctrica, Decreto No. 404-2013. Article 1 expressly includes generation within the activities regulated by the law. No general MW threshold has been identified in the law that can safely be presented as a universal exemption for an isolated self-consumption facility. The project's capacity, connection status, purpose, ownership and operating configuration should therefore be submitted to the competent Honduran authority for legal classification before construction or operation.
