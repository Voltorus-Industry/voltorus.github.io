Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Belize and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate without a physical connection to the public electricity grid.

No electricity would be imported, exported or sold to third parties.

The current regulatory framework is principally governed by the Electricity Act, Chapter 221, the Public Utilities Commission Act and the Electricity Licensing and Consent Regulations, 2026.

The 2026 Regulations entered into effect on 5 May 2026 and replaced the 2024 licensing regulations.

The current framework specifically recognizes self-generation categories.

A project intended exclusively for self-generation must therefore be classified according to its installed generation capacity and the applicable PUC licence category.

Administrative Requirements

The 2026 Regulations establish a specific licensing regime for electricity generation and self-generation.

The regulations expressly provide that an operation intended for electricity self-generation for less than 75 kW does not require a licence or consent under those regulations.

Above this threshold, the appropriate self-generation licence category must be determined.

  • Determine installed generation capacity.
  • Determine the appropriate PUC licence category.
  • Apply for the applicable licence where required.
  • Obtain consent for a generating facility where required.
  • Submit engineering and technical information.
  • Verify environmental and planning requirements.
  • Comply with electrical safety requirements.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation physically isolated from the utility grid.
  • Use the correct self-generation licence category where applicable.
  • Do not export electricity unless the project has the appropriate distributed-generation authorization.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Document the entire electrical architecture.
  • Obtain PUC confirmation before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Belize — self-generation thresholds under the 2026 PUC framework:

Below 75 kW
No licence or consent under the 2026 Regulations.

Up to 75 kW
Minor Self-Generation and Supply category.

More than 75 kW and up to 250 kW
Intermediate Self-Generation and Supply category.

The 2026 framework therefore provides a clear threshold structure for self-generation.

The PUC's current application documentation identifies the Minor Self-Generation category as generation output capacity up to 75 kW and the Intermediate Self-Generation category as greater than 75 kW and up to 250 kW.

Larger installations enter the major self-generation framework and require a specific licence.

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. Belize's current electricity licensing framework is governed by the Electricity Act and the Electricity Licensing and Consent Regulations, 2026. The 2026 framework expressly recognizes self-generation categories. Self-generation below 75 kW does not require a licence or consent under those regulations. The PUC documentation identifies systems up to 75 kW as Minor Self-Generation and systems above 75 kW and up to 250 kW as Intermediate Self-Generation. Larger projects require the applicable licensing framework. Construction, environmental, electrical and other legal requirements must nevertheless be verified.
