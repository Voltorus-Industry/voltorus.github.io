Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in El Salvador and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for the operator's own household, industrial, commercial, scientific or corporate requirements.

Electricity generation is regulated principally by the Ley General de Electricidad enacted by Legislative Decree No. 843, together with its regulations and amendments.

The law establishes different regulatory treatment according to the primary energy resource used for generation.

Hydraulic and geothermal generation requires a concession. Solar, wind and biomass renewable generation does not require a concession under Article 5. Other generation activities are subject to registration in the Electricity Sector Operators Registry under Article 7.

Administrative Requirements

Article 5 establishes the principal concession requirements for electricity generation.

Hydraulic and Geothermal Generation

Generation from hydraulic and geothermal resources requires a concession under Article 5.

Plants with a total nominal capacity of 5 MW or less are subject to the procedure established for small-scale electricity-generation concessions.

Solar, Wind and Biomass

Article 5 provides that electricity generation from renewable solar, wind and biomass resources does not require a concession.

Other Generation

Article 7 provides that generation activities not covered by Articles 5 and 6, together with transmission, distribution and commercialization, are carried out following registration in the Electricity Sector Operators Registry maintained by SIGET.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain the facility physically isolated from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Document the installation as an internal self-consumption facility.
  • Determine precisely how the gravity technology is legally classified according to its primary energy source.
  • Verify registration requirements with SIGET before construction or operation.
  • Verify environmental and construction requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
El Salvador — statutory threshold:

5 MW for the small-scale procedure applicable to hydraulic and geothermal concessions

Article 5 specifically refers to plants with nominal total capacity of 5 MW or less for the small-scale concession procedure.

The 5 MW value is not a general national maximum generation capacity.

It is a threshold used in the legal treatment of hydraulic and geothermal generation.

Solar, wind and biomass generation are expressly exempt from the concession requirement under Article 5.

For a gravity-powered system, the decisive issue is therefore the legal classification of the technology and its energy source rather than simply its electrical capacity.
