Overview

Electricity generation in Nicaragua is governed principally by Ley No. 272, Ley de la Industria Eléctrica, together with its amendments and implementing regulations.

Article 1 states that the law establishes the legal regime for activities of the electricity industry, including generation, transmission, distribution, commercialization, importation and exportation of electricity.

The Ministry of Energy and Mines states that a Licencia de Generación is required for projects with installed capacity greater than 1 MW.

The generation licence authorizes the construction and operation of the relevant generation project.

The Ministry indicates that the licence may be granted for a maximum period of 30 years.

A project at or below 1 MW should nevertheless be checked against the complete legal framework and applicable permits rather than assuming that every regulatory obligation disappears below the licence threshold.

Administrative Requirements

The Ministry of Energy and Mines specifies a Licencia de Generación for projects with installed capacity greater than 1 MW.

Before the generation licence application, the Ministry states that the provisional licence for project studies must have been satisfactorily completed.

Generation Licence
  • Applicant must be an economic agent eligible under the electricity legislation.
  • Project capacity must be established precisely.
  • Projects above 1 MW require the generation licence.
  • The project must comply with applicable environmental and technical legislation.
  • The applicable provisional study procedure must be completed before the generation licence application.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Precisely establish installed capacity.
  • Keep the facility physically isolated from the national electricity system.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • For projects above 1 MW, obtain the applicable generation licence before construction and operation.
  • Complete the required provisional study procedure.
  • Verify environmental and construction requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Nicaragua — statutory/licensing threshold:

1 MW

The Ministry of Energy and Mines states that a generation licence is required for projects with installed capacity greater than 1 MW.

The 1 MW figure is a licensing threshold and should not be described as the maximum permitted electrical power.

Above 1 MW, the project enters the generation-licensing regime. Below or at 1 MW, other legal, technical, environmental and construction requirements may still apply.
