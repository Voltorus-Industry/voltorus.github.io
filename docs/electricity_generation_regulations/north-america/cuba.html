Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Cuba and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation would operate independently from the Sistema Electroenergético Nacional and would not import electricity from or export electricity to the national grid.

Cuba's principal renewable-energy framework includes the Decreto-Ley No. 345, "Del Desarrollo de las Fuentes Renovables y el Uso Eficiente de la Energía".

The framework is supplemented by regulations of the Ministry of Energy and Mines, including Resolution 235/2021 and Resolution 236/2021.

Resolution 236/2021 establishes technical requirements concerning quality, energy efficiency, electrical safety and tropicalization for equipment used for generation from renewable sources.

Decreto 110/2024 establishes additional rules for the control and efficient use of energy carriers and renewable energy sources.

No general national MW ceiling specifically applicable to an isolated gravity-powered self-consumption electricity-generation facility has been identified in the principal legal instruments reviewed.

Administrative Requirements

Cuba maintains a centralized legal and technical framework for energy use and renewable-energy equipment.

The renewable-energy framework includes technical requirements for renewable-generation equipment and procedures concerning equipment authorization and importation.

  • Verify the legal classification of the generation facility.
  • Verify the requirements of the Ministry of Energy and Mines.
  • Verify applicable technical requirements for renewable-generation equipment.
  • Verify electrical safety requirements.
  • Verify construction and land-use requirements.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify whether the installation must be registered, authorized or inspected.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the national electricity system.
  • Document that the electricity is used solely for internal consumption.
  • Do not sell or export electricity without a specific authorization.
  • Ensure that all generation equipment satisfies applicable technical standards.
  • Verify equipment importation and authorization requirements.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the competent MINEM authorities before construction.
