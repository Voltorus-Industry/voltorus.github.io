Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Barbados and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation would operate as an independent electrical island without connection to the public electricity grid.

No electricity would be imported, exported, sold or supplied to third parties.

The principal legislation is the Electric Light and Power Act, 2013.

Section 3 of the Act principally regulates the supply of electricity to another person.

The First Schedule specifies 5 kW for systems used for domestic purposes only and 100 kW for systems used for other purposes in connection with the statutory exception concerning supply to the public grid.

These figures should not be represented as a general maximum capacity for a genuinely isolated self-consumption generator.

Administrative Requirements

Section 3(1) provides that a person shall not supply electricity to another except in accordance with a licence.

The Act therefore distinguishes the supply of electricity to another person from purely internal electricity use.

  • Confirm that the installation is genuinely isolated from the public grid.
  • Confirm that electricity is not supplied to another person.
  • Verify whether any generation or supply licence applies to the specific project.
  • Verify planning permission under applicable planning legislation.
  • Verify environmental requirements.
  • Verify electrical and fire-safety requirements.
  • Document installed generation capacity.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • No physical connection to the public grid.
  • No electricity export.
  • No electricity sales.
  • No electricity supply to third parties.
  • Use electrical protection preventing accidental grid energisation.
  • Keep engineering documentation showing self-consumption configuration.
  • Obtain written regulatory confirmation before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Barbados — statutory figures identified:

5 kW domestic / 100 kW other purposes

These figures appear in the First Schedule in connection with the statutory exception concerning electricity supplied to the public grid.

Section 3(1) prohibits supplying electricity to another person without a licence.

Section 3(2) provides an exception for certain electricity supplied to the public grid where the cumulative installed capacity does not exceed the relevant First Schedule amount.

The First Schedule specifies:

  • 5 kW for systems used for domestic purposes only.
  • 100 kW for systems used for other purposes.
  • 1 MW as the threshold relevant to the application-publication procedure in section 5(3).

These numbers should not be presented as a general statutory maximum for an isolated private self-consumption installation.
