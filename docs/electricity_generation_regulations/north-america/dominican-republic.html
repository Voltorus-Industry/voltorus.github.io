Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in the Dominican Republic and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the interconnected public electricity system, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public grid.

Electricity would be consumed exclusively by the owner or operator for its own household, industrial, commercial or corporate requirements.

Electricity generation is principally regulated by the Ley General de Electricidad No. 125-01 and its Reglamento de Aplicación, together with subsequent amendments.

The regulatory framework distinguishes conventional electricity-generation projects, autogeneration and isolated systems.

Article 68 of the Reglamento de Aplicación establishes important exceptions to the definitive concession requirement for installations used exclusively for autogeneration and for isolated systems with maximum demand of up to 2 MW.

Administrative Requirements

Article 67 of the Reglamento de Aplicación provides that legal entities wishing to install an electrical work consisting of generation or distribution generally submit an application for a definitive concession.

Article 68 establishes exceptions.

Exclusive Autogeneration

Installations executed exclusively for autogeneration are excluded from the definitive concession requirement.

However, such exclusively autogeneration installations must obtain the corresponding authorization from the environmental authority.

Isolated Systems

Isolated systems with a maximum power demand of up to 2 MW are also expressly excluded from the definitive concession requirement under Article 68.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Operate exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity system.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not inject electricity into the public grid.
  • Clearly classify the facility as an autogeneration/self-consumption installation.
  • Precisely document the maximum electrical demand and installed generation capacity.
  • Obtain the applicable environmental authorization.
  • Verify whether the project qualifies as an isolated system with maximum demand not exceeding 2 MW.
Maximum Electrical Power
Dominican Republic — important statutory threshold:

2 MW maximum demand for the isolated-system concession exception

Article 68 of the Reglamento de Aplicación excludes isolated systems with maximum demand of up to 2 MW from the definitive concession requirement.

This 2 MW figure should not be presented as a general maximum generation capacity permitted in the country.

It is specifically relevant to the exception applicable to isolated systems.

An installation exclusively dedicated to autogeneration benefits from a separate exception to the definitive concession requirement, subject in particular to environmental authorization.

Public Information Notice

In the Dominican Republic, electricity generation is regulated principally by Law No. 125-01 and its implementing regulation. The implementing regulation requires definitive concessions for electrical works in general, but Article 68 establishes exceptions for installations used exclusively for autogeneration and for isolated systems with maximum demand of up to 2 MW. Exclusive autogeneration remains subject to the applicable environmental authorization. The 2 MW value is therefore a concession-exemption threshold for isolated systems, not a general national maximum generating capacity.
