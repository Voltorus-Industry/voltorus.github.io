Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in Antigua and Barbuda and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for the operator's own household, commercial, industrial, scientific or corporate electricity requirements.

The principal electricity framework includes the Public Utilities Act, Cap. 359 and the Renewable Energy Act, 2015.

The Public Utilities Act establishes the Antigua Public Utilities Authority (APUA) and provides it with extensive statutory functions concerning electricity generation, distribution, supply and sale.

The Renewable Energy Act, 2015 establishes a specific framework for renewable generation facilities and provides that the Minister is responsible for issuing renewable generation licences.

No general statutory MW ceiling specifically applicable to an isolated gravity-powered self-consumption installation has been identified in the principal electricity legislation reviewed.

Administrative Requirements

Antigua and Barbuda maintains a statutory electricity framework in which APUA plays a central role.

The Renewable Energy Act, 2015 provides that the Minister is responsible for issuing renewable generation licences and for coordinating the licensing process with relevant authorities.

  • Verification of the applicable generation authorization or licence.
  • Consultation with APUA concerning any proposed electricity-generation installation.
  • Planning and construction approvals where applicable.
  • Environmental impact requirements where applicable.
  • Land-use and construction requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Fire and occupational safety requirements.
  • Technical requirements applicable to renewable generation equipment.
Off-grid Self-consumption

The fact that electricity is consumed solely by the operator does not by itself establish a general statutory exemption from the electricity-generation authorization framework.

The project should therefore obtain written confirmation from the competent authority concerning the authorization applicable to the proposed isolated installation before construction.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical separation from the public electricity grid.
  • Prohibit electricity exports and third-party electricity sales.
  • Document the installation as a private self-consumption facility.
  • Obtain written confirmation from APUA concerning the proposed generation system.
  • Verify whether a renewable generation licence is required.
  • Complete all applicable environmental, planning, construction and electrical safety procedures.
  • Precisely document the installed electrical capacity.
