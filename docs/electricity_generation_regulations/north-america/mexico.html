Overview

Electricity generation in Mexico is currently governed principally by the Ley del Sector Eléctrico (LSE) and its implementing regulations and administrative provisions.

The current framework expressly regulates electricity generation for autoconsumo.

Mexican regulation distinguishes between autoconsumo aislado and autoconsumo interconectado.

The current administrative rules establish a simplified procedure for interconnected self-consumption facilities between 0.7 MW and 20 MW.

For isolated self-consumption, the current administrative provisions require a generation permit for facilities starting at 0.7 MW.

Generation Distributed installations below 0.7 MW are treated under the Generación Distribuida framework and do not require a generation permit.

Consequently, the 0.7 MW threshold is highly relevant when determining the regulatory status of an electricity self-consumption project in Mexico.

Administrative Requirements
Autoconsumo Isolated

The current administrative provisions expressly provide that electricity generation for autoconsumo must be carried out by the holder of a valid generation permit.

For isolated self-consumption facilities with capacity starting at 0.7 MW, the applicant must use the authorized application form and comply with the applicable permit requirements.

Autoconsumo Interconnected

Facilities between 0.7 MW and 20 MW have a simplified procedure for obtaining the generation permit for interconnected self-consumption.

The specific requirements are established by the Commission's administrative agreement and the applicable electricity-sector regulations.

Generation Distributed

The CNE states that electricity-generation facilities below 0.7 MW under the Generación Distribuida framework do not require a generation permit.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Clearly classify the installation as autoconsumo aislado if there is no grid connection.
  • Precisely establish installed electrical capacity.
  • For capacity of 0.7 MW or more, verify and obtain the required generation permit before operation.
  • Prevent physical connection to the national electricity system if isolated operation is intended.
  • Do not export electricity unless the applicable regulatory regime expressly permits it.
  • Maintain technical documentation identifying the generating capacity.
  • Verify environmental, land-use, construction and electrical-safety requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Mexico — key statutory/regulatory threshold:

0.7 MW

At and above 0.7 MW, the applicable generation-permit framework becomes relevant.

For interconnected autoconsumption, a simplified generation-permit procedure exists for facilities from 0.7 MW through 20 MW.

For isolated autoconsumption, facilities starting at 0.7 MW are subject to the generation-permit framework.

Facilities below 0.7 MW can fall under the Generación Distribuida exemption from the generation permit, subject to the conditions of that legal regime.

Therefore, the relevant website classification should state that 0.7 MW is the principal generation permit threshold, rather than presenting 20 MW as a general maximum.

Public Information Notice

Mexico currently distinguishes between isolated and interconnected self-consumption. Under the current electricity framework, generation at or above 0.7 MW is subject to the generation-permit regime. A simplified procedure applies to interconnected self-consumption facilities between 0.7 MW and 20 MW. Generation Distributed facilities below 0.7 MW are exempt from the generation permit, subject to the conditions of the applicable Generación Distribuida framework.
