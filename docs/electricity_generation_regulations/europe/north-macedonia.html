Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of North Macedonia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

North Macedonia's electricity sector is regulated principally by the Energy Law and by the regulatory framework administered by the Energy and Water Services Regulatory Commission of the Republic of North Macedonia.

The electricity-production activity is subject to a licensing framework. The legislation reviewed does not establish a general numerical MW threshold specifically applicable to an isolated gravity-powered electricity production facility.

Consequently, no artificial MW figure from photovoltaic, wind or another technology is transferred to the proposed gravity-powered installation.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity production is regulated as an energy activity and the applicable licensing requirements must be assessed before construction and operation.

The self-consumption configuration does not by itself establish a general MW exemption for a gravity-powered generation facility.

  • Applicable electricity-production licensing requirements must be assessed.
  • Construction and planning requirements may apply.
  • Electrical safety requirements apply.
  • Fire-safety requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Land-use requirements may apply.
  • Structural engineering requirements may apply.
  • Water-law requirements may apply where water resources are involved.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Prevent accidental connection to the public electricity network.
  • Obtain written confirmation from ERC concerning the licensing classification of the proposed gravity-powered installation.
  • Verify construction, environmental and planning requirements independently from the electricity licence.
