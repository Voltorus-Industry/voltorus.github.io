Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of Serbia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Serbian electricity legislation regulates electricity production, transmission, distribution, supply and the licensing of electricity-sector activities.

The Energy Agency of the Republic of Serbia is responsible for issuing licences for electricity production in the cases specified by the Energy Law.

Administrative Requirements

Off-grid operation does not automatically remove Serbian construction, environmental, electrical-safety or other regulatory requirements.

Electricity Production Licence

The Energy Agency of the Republic of Serbia states that a licence for electricity production is issued where the total approved connection power is:

More than 1 MW

The 1 MW threshold is therefore the principal numerical electricity-production threshold identified for the licensing regime.

  • No electricity trading.

  • Independent island operation.
  • Electrical protection against accidental grid energisation.
  • Verification of the applicable Energy Law requirements before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Serbia electricity-production licensing threshold:

1 MW

The Energy Agency of the Republic of Serbia identifies electricity-production facilities with a total approved connection power above 1 MW as requiring a production licence.

For website classification purposes, the relevant threshold is therefore:

1 MW = 1,000 kW

The exact applicability to a completely isolated installation should nevertheless be confirmed because the published licensing criterion is expressed in terms of approved connection power.
