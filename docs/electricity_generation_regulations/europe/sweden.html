Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Sweden and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate as a completely isolated electrical system without connection to the Swedish public electricity network.

No electricity would be imported from or exported to the public electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.

Swedish electricity legislation regulates electricity generation, networks, electricity trading and consumption.

The legal analysis for an isolated generating installation must be distinguished from the rules applicable to electricity-network operations and grid-connected generation.

Administrative Requirements

Swedish law does not establish a general generation licence threshold specifically for a private, genuinely isolated electricity generator merely because of its electrical capacity.

The project must nevertheless comply with applicable construction, environmental, electrical-safety, planning and land-use requirements.

Electricity Generation

Electricity generation must be distinguished from operation of an electricity network.

A private installation that neither operates a public electricity network nor supplies electricity to third parties has a materially different regulatory profile from an electricity-network operator.

Additional permits can nevertheless arise from the physical characteristics and location of the installation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical separation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively internally.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of any public electricity network.
  • Document the installation as a private isolated electrical system.
  • Obtain site-specific confirmation of planning, environmental and electrical-safety requirements.
Maximum Electrical Power
Sweden — generation threshold:

No fixed national kW/MW licensing threshold specific to a genuinely isolated private electricity-generation installation has been identified in the applicable national electricity framework.

The 1,500 kW figure sometimes encountered in Swedish electricity-sector material concerns the classification of small-scale electricity production and network/tariff contexts.

It should not be presented as the maximum permitted capacity of a completely isolated gravity-powered generating station.

Website classification for Sweden:

No specific national generation-capacity threshold identified for the exact isolated self-consumption configuration.

Capacity must instead be assessed against the planning, environmental, electrical-safety and technology-specific rules applicable to the site.
