Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Andorra and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Andorran legislation expressly recognizes isolated electricity-generation installations. The Regulation governing electricity generation defines an "isolated installation" as a generation installation operating autonomously without a physical connection to the electricity distribution or transmission network and supplying one or more uses or activities.

The Andorran electricity-generation framework applies to electricity-generation installations using renewable energy sources, including installations operating autonomously, with a reference regulatory capacity of up to 2,000 kW.

We should nevertheless distinguish the electricity regulatory framework from construction, environmental, planning, electrical-safety and other administrative requirements that may apply to the physical installation.

Administrative Requirements

Andorra has a specific administrative framework for electricity-generation installations.

The Government provides an official procedure for the registration of new electricity-generation installations and for modifications or cancellation of existing installations.

Registration of the Generation Installation

The official Andorran procedure permits the formal registration of new electricity production installations.

The procedure is available to both natural persons and legal persons.

The Andorran Government maintains a national energy register for electricity-generation installations.

We should therefore not describe a qualifying electricity-generation installation as completely free from administrative registration merely because it is off-grid.

Capacity-Based Requirements

For installations with a power greater than 500 kW and less than or equal to 2,000 kW, the Andorran Government requires an administrative authorization.

The promoter must submit a report describing the electricity production, production profiles and environmental impact.

The Government's official procedure also identifies technical and environmental documentation requirements for installations above 20 kW.

These requirements are separate from the question of whether electricity is sold or injected into the public electricity network.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, We recommend that the installation be designed and operated so that:

  • The installation has no physical connection to the public electricity distribution or transmission network.
  • Electricity is generated exclusively for internal consumption.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public electricity network.
  • The installation does not participate in the electricity market.
  • The installation is operated as an isolated electricity system.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection or energisation of the public network.
  • Technical documentation demonstrates the autonomous operating configuration.
  • The actual electricity-generation technology is correctly classified under the applicable Andorran energy legislation.
  • Where the system is intended to qualify as renewable generation, We verify that its primary energy source falls within the applicable definition of renewable energy.
Maximum Electrical Power
Applicable capacity threshold: 2,000 kW (2 MW)

Andorran Law 21/2018 on the promotion of the energy transition and climate change expressly permits the production of electricity from renewable energy sources for installations with a power of less than or equal to 2,000 kW .

The Regulation governing electricity generation applies to renewable electricity-generation installations that are connected to the electricity network or operate autonomously with a power of less than or equal to 2,000 kW.

The Andorran Government's official guidance also expressly states that renewable electricity-generation installations, whether connected to or isolated from the electricity network, may be developed up to 2,000 kW.

Installations with a power greater than 500 kW and less than or equal to 2,000 kW require administrative authorization and additional documentation.

Isolated generation installations with a power of 5 kW or less are expressly exempted from the application of the 2020 electricity-generation regulation.

Technology qualification: The 2,000 kW threshold is established for the Andorran regulatory framework applicable to electricity generation from renewable energy sources. We should therefore verify that the actual primary energy source of a proposed gravity-powered system qualifies as a renewable energy source under Andorran law before applying this renewable-generation threshold to the project.

2,000 kW (2 MW) for an electricity-generation installation falling within the Andorran renewable-generation framework.
