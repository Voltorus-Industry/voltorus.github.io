Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of Moldova and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

The current Moldovan electricity framework is based principally on Law No. 164 of 26 June 2025 on electricity, which entered into force on 19 August 2025.

The law establishes licensing requirements for electricity generation according to installed generation capacity.

Under the current framework, a generation licence is required for a producer operating a power plant with an installed capacity of 5 MW or more, including aggregated generation capacity where applicable.

Administrative Requirements

The current electricity law establishes a generation licence requirement from an installed generation capacity of 5 MW.

The fact that an installation is operated exclusively for internal consumption and is physically isolated from the public network does not eliminate the need to assess the applicable electricity, construction, environmental and safety requirements.

  • Generation licence threshold: 5 MW installed capacity.
  • Electrical installation requirements remain applicable.
  • Construction and building requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Land-use and planning requirements may apply.
  • Fire-safety requirements may apply.
  • Structural engineering requirements may apply.
  • Water-law requirements may apply where the installation uses water resources.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installed generation capacity below 5 MW if the project is intended to remain below the generation-licence threshold.
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Prevent accidental connection to the public electricity network.
  • Obtain confirmation from ANRE concerning the classification of the proposed gravity-powered installation before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Moldova — generation licence threshold:

5 MW installed capacity

Article 16 of Law No. 164 of 26 June 2025 provides that an electricity-generation licence is issued to a producer operating a power plant with an installed capacity of 5 MW or more, or with aggregated installed generation capacity of 5 MW or more.

The same article also addresses a power plant integrated with an energy-storage installation where the combined injection capacity reaches the statutory threshold.

For the comparative database, the relevant figure for electricity generation is therefore 5 MW. The threshold is based on installed generation capacity and is not a technology-specific gravity threshold.
