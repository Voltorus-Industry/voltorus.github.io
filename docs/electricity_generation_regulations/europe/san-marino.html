Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of San Marino and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

San Marino regulates electricity production, network connection, energy supply and renewable electricity installations through its energy legislation and the Autorità di Regolazione per i Servizi Pubblici e l'Energia.

A completely isolated self-consumption installation must be assessed according to its technology, capacity, construction characteristics and whether any physical connection to the public electricity network exists.

Administrative Requirements

Off-grid operation does not by itself remove construction, electrical-safety or environmental requirements.

Renewable Electricity Threshold

San Marino legislation expressly identifies a threshold of 20 kW for specific photovoltaic installations and related energy regimes.

Photovoltaic installations with nominal power above 20 kW are subject to additional regulatory provisions, including specific rules concerning public-network connection.

This 20 kW threshold is specifically documented for photovoltaic installations and should not automatically be transferred to a different technology such as a gravity-powered generator.

Maximum Electrical Power
Documented threshold:

20 kW is the principal numerical threshold identified in San Marino energy legislation for specific photovoltaic installations.

The 20 kW threshold is not a technology-neutral licensing limit for every electricity-generation technology.

For a gravity-powered installation, the competent authority should therefore confirm whether the photovoltaic-specific 20 kW provisions apply to the proposed technology.

Website classification:

20 kW — documented San Marino threshold for the specified photovoltaic regulatory category.

The technology classification must be confirmed before applying this threshold to a gravity-powered facility.
