Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Bosnia and Herzegovina and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Bosnia and Herzegovina does not have a single unified electricity-production licensing regime applicable throughout the entire territory. Electricity production is regulated primarily at the level of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska and Brčko District of Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Consequently, the applicable administrative requirements depend principally on the territorial location of the proposed installation.

In the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and Republika Srpska, electricity production exclusively for the producer's own needs is treated differently from electricity production carried out as a regulated electricity-production activity.

Brčko District has its own electricity legislation and regulatory framework administered principally by the State Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERK).

A completely isolated self-consumption installation must therefore be assessed according to the entity or district in which it is located, while remaining subject to applicable construction, planning, environmental, safety, technical and other site-specific requirements.

Administrative Requirements

We should not treat a completely off-grid installation as automatically exempt from all administrative requirements in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

The applicable requirements depend on whether the facility is located in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska or Brčko District.

Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina

The Law on Electricity of the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, published in the Official Gazette of the Federation of BiH No. 60/23, regulates electricity production, construction of power plants, licensing and related electricity-sector activities.

Article 17 provides that an electricity-sector entity is not required to hold a production licence for:

  • electricity production in power plants with installed capacity up to 1 MW;
  • electricity production exclusively for its own needs without delivery to the distribution network; and
  • electricity production in new power plants for a maximum of six months after obtaining the use permit.

The own-consumption exemption is therefore particularly relevant to a genuinely isolated installation that does not deliver electricity to the distribution network.

The same law separately regulates the energy permit required for construction of electricity-generation facilities.

Under Article 42, an energy permit is generally required before construction of a power plant. The law provides an exemption for plants with installed capacity below 1 MW , except for hydroelectric plants.

Consequently, the 1 MW figure is an important construction and regulatory threshold in the Federation, but it should not be described as a universal production-capacity ceiling for own-consumption generation.

Republika Srpska

Republika Srpska regulates electricity production through its electricity and energy legislation and the Regulatory Commission for Energy of Republika Srpska (RERS).

The applicable framework exempts electricity production carried out exclusively for the producer's own needs from the production licence requirement.

Electricity production in facilities with installed capacity up to 1 MW is also specifically identified as a category not requiring a production licence.

Where the same producer operates several facilities whose combined installed capacity exceeds 1 MW, the 1 MW exemption applicable to multiple small facilities does not apply.

Separate construction, environmental, spatial-planning and technical requirements remain applicable to the facility.

Brčko District

Brčko District has its own Law on Electricity and is regulated in the electricity sector by the State Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERK).

The Brčko District electricity framework provides a specific threshold of 1 MW for electricity-production licensing.

Production facilities with capacity up to 1 MW are exempt from the DERK production licence requirement under the applicable conditions.

The law also provides a specific procedure for electricity production for own needs with installed capacity up to 1 MW .

For facilities exceeding the applicable 1 MW threshold, the production licensing and construction authorisation framework of DERK applies.

Construction, technical, environmental, planning and other applicable requirements must nevertheless be verified separately.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, We recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No electricity is supplied to third parties.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No participation in electricity balancing or aggregation markets is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installed electrical capacity is documented and verified by a qualified electrical engineer.
  • The applicable territorial framework is identified before construction: Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska or Brčko District.
  • The 1 MW regulatory threshold is checked against the applicable electricity-production, construction and permitting provisions.
  • Any required energy, construction, environmental, planning, water or technical authorisations are obtained before construction or operation.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-consumption facility rather than a facility intended for electricity supply or commercial electricity sales.

Bosnia and Herzegovina does not apply a single electricity-production licensing law uniformly to all parts of the country.

The applicable framework depends on whether the installation is located in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska or Brčko District.

Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina

The Federation's Law on Electricity establishes 1 MW as an important installed-capacity threshold.

Electricity-production facilities with installed capacity up to 1 MW are exempt from the ordinary production-licence requirement.

In addition, electricity production exclusively for the producer's own needs without delivery to the distribution network is expressly exempted from the production-licence requirement.

Article 42 separately provides that an energy permit is not required for construction of electricity-generation facilities with installed capacity below 1 MW , except for hydroelectric plants.

For a gravity-powered facility that is not a hydroelectric plant, 1 MW is therefore the key federal installed-capacity threshold for the construction-permit analysis.

Republika Srpska

Republika Srpska uses 1 MW as the principal threshold for electricity production licensing.

Electricity production exclusively for own needs is exempt from the production licence requirement under the applicable legislation.

Electricity production in facilities up to 1 MW is also specifically identified as exempt from the production licence requirement.

Separate construction, environmental and planning approvals may nevertheless apply.

Brčko District

Brčko District establishes a specific 1 MW threshold for electricity-production licensing.

Production facilities up to 1 MW fall within the statutory exemption from the DERK electricity-production licence under the applicable conditions.

The Brčko District framework also provides a specific procedure for production of electricity for own needs with installed capacity up to 1 MW .

Facilities exceeding 1 MW require the applicable DERK production licensing and construction authorisation procedures.

The 1 MW threshold is the principal installed-capacity figure identified in the electricity-production licensing and construction framework of the relevant Bosnian regulatory jurisdictions.

For the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and Republika Srpska, electricity production exclusively for the producer's own needs is subject to specific statutory treatment and is not subject to the ordinary production-licence requirement when the applicable statutory conditions are satisfied.

In Brčko District, the legislation specifically provides for own-needs electricity production up to 1 MW and establishes the corresponding administrative procedure.

For the comparative database, the appropriate figure to record for Bosnia and Herzegovina is therefore 1 MW as the principal installed-capacity threshold, with the important qualification that the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina and Republika Srpska contain separate exemptions for electricity production exclusively for own needs. The precise permitting position must be determined from the law applicable to the specific location of the facility.

Public Information Notice

This information is provided for general public information purposes only. Bosnia and Herzegovina has a territorially divided electricity regulatory framework, and the precise requirements applicable to a gravity-powered electricity-generation facility depend on whether the installation is located in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina, Republika Srpska or Brčko District. The 1 MW figure identified on this page is the principal installed-capacity threshold appearing in the relevant electricity-production licensing and construction frameworks. It must not be interpreted as eliminating other permits or authorisations. In particular, construction, planning, environmental, water, technical, fire-safety, structural, occupational-safety and other requirements may apply independently of the electricity-production licence. For installations intended exclusively for internal self-consumption and physically isolated from the public electricity network, the applicable own-consumption exemption should be assessed under the legislation of the specific territory in which the facility is located. The exact regulatory position should be verified before developing, constructing or operating the installation.
