Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Spain and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate completely independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Spanish electricity legislation distinguishes between grid-connected self-consumption installations and genuinely isolated electricity installations.

Under the Spanish regulatory definition, an isolated installation is an installation having no physical capacity at any time to connect, directly or indirectly, to the electricity transmission or distribution network.

The national regulatory framework for electrical self-consumption does not apply to installations classified as genuinely isolated.

Consequently, the project must be assessed primarily under the rules applicable to isolated electricity generation, together with the applicable autonomous community, construction, environmental, planning, industrial-safety and other site-specific requirements.

Administrative Requirements

A genuinely isolated installation must not be confused with a conventional grid-connected autoconsumption installation.

Real Decreto 244/2019 expressly excludes isolated installations from its scope.

Consequently, the simplified rules applicable to grid-connected autoconsumption installations cannot automatically be used to determine the legal status of a completely isolated facility.

National Electricity Framework

Ley 24/2013 del Sector Eléctrico establishes the Spanish framework governing electricity generation and other electricity-sector activities.

Article 21 establishes the general framework applicable to electricity-production installations.

The precise administrative treatment of an isolated installation must therefore be assessed together with the applicable implementing and regional legislation.

Isolated Installation

Spanish legislation defines an isolated installation by reference to the absence of any physical capacity to connect to the transmission or distribution network.

This is materially different from a grid installation that can be disconnected by a switch.

The project should therefore be physically designed and documented as a genuinely isolated electrical system.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain genuinely isolated and dedicated to internal self-consumption, we recommend that:

  • The installation has no physical connection to the Spanish public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal consumption.
  • The installation operates in permanent island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to the public network.
  • The project is documented as an isolated electricity-generation installation.
  • The autonomous community is identified before construction.
  • Regional electricity, planning, environmental and construction requirements are verified.
Maximum Electrical Power
Spain — applicable classification:

No single national kW threshold has been identified that defines the maximum electrical capacity of a genuinely isolated gravity-powered generation installation solely because it is off-grid and self-consumption-only.

The frequently cited 100 kW threshold in Spanish electricity legislation concerns specific grid-connected autoconsumption and registration provisions.

It must therefore not be entered as the maximum capacity of a genuinely isolated installation.

Spanish legislation expressly distinguishes isolated installations from installations connected to the electricity network.

Important 100 kW Distinction

Certain Spanish autoconsumption provisions use a 100 kW threshold for grid-connected installations.

These provisions cannot be transposed directly to a genuinely isolated installation because isolated installations are outside the scope of the national autoconsumption regulation.

Therefore, the website should not classify 100 kW as the Spanish maximum power for the proposed isolated technology.
