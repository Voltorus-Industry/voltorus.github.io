Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Ireland and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network.

Electricity generation and generating-station authorisations are administered by the Commission for Regulation of Utilities (CRU).

Since 7 September 2022, generating stations with installed capacity greater than 1 MW and not exceeding 10 MW are subject to the specific notification procedure introduced by S.I. Nos. 459 and 460 of 2022.

Generating stations with installed capacity of 1 MW or less are exempt from that authorisation/ notification process.

Administrative Requirements

The CRU states that generating stations with installed capacity of 1 MW or less are exempt from the generation authorisation/notification process applicable to larger stations.

For stations greater than 1 MW and not exceeding 10 MW, a notification procedure applies.

  • Planning permission where applicable.
  • Environmental requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Building and structural requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Water-related authorisation where applicable.
