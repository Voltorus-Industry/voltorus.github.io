Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Switzerland and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation would operate completely independently from the Swiss public electricity network.

No electricity would be imported from or exported to the public network.

Electricity would be used exclusively by the producer for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.

Swiss electricity regulation includes federal rules concerning electricity supply, energy production, self-consumption and electricity networks, together with cantonal and municipal requirements.

The exact administrative treatment depends strongly on the technology and physical characteristics of the installation.

Administrative Requirements

Swiss legislation does not establish a general 30 kW maximum capacity for self-consumption electricity generation.

The 30 kW figure encountered in Swiss energy legislation is associated with specific renewable electricity remuneration provisions and should not be treated as a general maximum generation capacity.

Federal Electricity and Energy Law

The federal StromVG and Energiegesetz establish the principal Swiss electricity and energy framework.

The precise requirements applicable to a generation installation depend on the type of installation and its interaction with electricity networks and other regulated resources.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical separation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not export electricity to the public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of the public electricity network.
  • Document the installation as an isolated private electricity system.
  • Verify cantonal and municipal requirements before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Switzerland — exact regulatory finding:

No general federal maximum kW/MW threshold has been identified that specifically limits a genuinely isolated private electricity-generation installation solely because it is used for self-consumption.

The commonly encountered 30 kW threshold relates to specific renewable-energy remuneration provisions and is not a general electricity-generation capacity ceiling.

Consequently, 30 kW must not be entered into the website as the maximum power of the proposed gravity-powered installation.

Website classification for Switzerland:

No specific federal capacity ceiling identified for the exact isolated self-consumption configuration.

The final permissible project capacity must be assessed against cantonal, municipal, environmental, structural, electrical and technology-specific rules.
