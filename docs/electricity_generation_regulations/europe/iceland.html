Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Iceland and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network.

The principal electricity legislation is the Icelandic Electricity Act No. 65/2003.

Article 4 requires a power development licence for construction and operation of an electric power plant.

Article 4 expressly provides an exemption for an electric power plant with rated capacity under 1 MW.

Administrative Requirements

Article 4 of the Electricity Act requires a power-development licence to construct and operate an electric power plant.

An electric power plant with rated capacity under 1 MW does not require the power-development licence.

If electricity from such an installation is sold or transmitted into an electricity supply system, the generation must nevertheless be reported to Orkustofnun.
