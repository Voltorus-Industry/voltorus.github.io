Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Belarus and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Belarus maintains a regulated electricity sector in which electricity generation, energy supply and electricity-network activities are subject to specific statutory and regulatory requirements.

The current Belarusian Law on Licensing establishes the types of activity subject to licensing and provides an exhaustive framework for determining which activities require a licence.

For the specific configuration considered here, namely a physically isolated generation facility producing electricity exclusively for its own internal consumption, We have not identified a Belarusian statutory provision establishing a specific kW or MW threshold applicable solely to this configuration.

The absence of an identified numerical threshold should not be interpreted as an automatic exemption from all Belarusian administrative requirements. Construction, electrical safety, environmental, industrial-safety, land-use and other requirements may apply according to the characteristics of the installation.

Administrative Requirements

We should distinguish the electricity-market licensing framework from the other administrative requirements applicable to the physical installation.

Electricity Licensing

The Belarusian Law of 14 October 2022 No. 213-Z "On Licensing", as amended, establishes the legal framework governing licensing and determines the list of licensed types of activity.

The law states that only activities included in its prescribed list of licensed activities are subject to licensing.

We have not identified, in the sources reviewed, a provision establishing a specific electricity-generation licence requirement based solely on the existence of a physically isolated generator producing electricity exclusively for its own internal consumption.

The exact legal classification should nevertheless be confirmed for the particular installation before construction or operation.

Self-Consumption Configuration

The proposed configuration should remain strictly limited to internal consumption.

  • Electricity is consumed by the producer.
  • Electricity is not sold to third parties.
  • Electricity is not supplied through the public electricity network.
  • Electricity is not exported to the public electricity network.
  • Electricity is not purchased from the public electricity network.
  • The generating installation remains physically isolated from the public electricity network.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

To maintain the proposed project as a genuine private off-grid self-consumption installation, We recommend that the project be designed and operated so that:

  • The generating installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is exported to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public electricity network.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity is supplied through a public electricity distribution network.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate consumption.
  • The installation operates as an independent electrical island.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental energisation of the public network.
  • Technical documentation clearly identifies the installation as an isolated self-consumption system.
  • The installed electrical capacity is documented and declared accurately.
  • Applicable construction, environmental, electrical and safety requirements are verified before commissioning.
Maximum Electrical Power
Identified statutory capacity threshold: No specific kW/MW threshold identified in the reviewed Belarusian legislation for a completely isolated electricity-generation facility used exclusively for internal self-consumption.

We have reviewed the available Belarusian electricity and licensing framework for the purpose of identifying a specific numerical capacity threshold applicable to an electricity-generation installation that:

  • operates completely off-grid;
  • does not import electricity;
  • does not export electricity;
  • does not sell electricity;
  • does not purchase electricity from the public network; and
  • produces electricity solely for internal consumption.

We have not identified a Belarusian statutory provision establishing a specific maximum installed electrical capacity in kW or MW solely for this configuration.

Belarusian regulatory materials distinguish electricity generation for the electricity system from electricity generated by enterprises for their own needs.

Belarusian energy-sector materials have also identified block-stations of 25 MW and above in the context of wholesale electricity-market participation. This figure should not be interpreted as a maximum or exemption threshold for an isolated self-consumption installation.

Important: We should not publish a numerical kW/MW maximum for Belarus for the specific off-grid configuration unless the competent Belarusian authority confirms such a threshold in the legislation applicable to the proposed technology and installation.

The applicable capacity may nevertheless affect other regulatory regimes, including construction, industrial safety, environmental protection, electrical safety, land use and, where applicable, water-resource regulation.
