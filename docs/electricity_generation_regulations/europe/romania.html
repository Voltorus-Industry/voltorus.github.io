Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Romania and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Romanian electricity activities are regulated principally by Law No. 123/2012 on electricity and natural gas, together with ANRE regulations.

ANRE authorisation requirements distinguish electricity-generation capacities according to installed electrical power.

Under the applicable ANRE framework, an establishment authorisation is required when the total installed electrical capacity of the generation capacity exceeds 1 MW.

Administrative Requirements

ANRE regulates the establishment and commercial operation of electricity-generation capacities.

The current regulatory framework provides that an establishment authorisation is required where the total installed electrical capacity exceeds 1 MW.

The 1 MW figure concerns the establishment authorisation threshold and should not automatically be interpreted as a universal exemption from every electricity-sector obligation.

  • More than 1 MW: ANRE establishment authorisation required.
  • Applicable licensing requirements must be assessed separately for commercial operation.
  • Construction requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Planning requirements may apply.
  • Electrical safety requirements apply.
  • Fire-safety requirements may apply.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep total installed electrical capacity at or below 1 MW if the project is intended to remain below the ANRE establishment-authorisation threshold.
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use generated electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Prevent accidental connection to the public electricity network.
  • Confirm the applicable ANRE licensing and authorisation requirements before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Romania — establishment-authorisation threshold:

1 MW installed electrical capacity

The ANRE regulatory framework provides that an establishment authorisation is required for the creation or repowering of an electricity-generation capacity where the total installed electrical power exceeds 1 MW.

The 1 MW threshold is therefore the relevant numerical figure for the establishment-authorisation analysis.

The threshold does not constitute a technology-specific limit for gravity generation. It is a capacity threshold contained in the Romanian electricity authorisation framework.

For the comparative database, the Romanian value should therefore be recorded as: 1 MW — establishment-authorisation threshold.
