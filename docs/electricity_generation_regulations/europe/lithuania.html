Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Lithuania and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate completely independently from the public electricity network.

Lithuanian electricity-sector activities are governed principally by the Electricity Law of the Republic of Lithuania and regulated by the National Energy Regulatory Council (VERT).

Lithuanian law provides a specific exemption for electricity-generation installations with installed capacity not exceeding 30 kW when electricity is produced solely for the producer's own needs and household/economic requirements without supplying electricity to the electricity networks.

The 30 kW threshold therefore directly corresponds to the intended purely off-grid self-consumption configuration.

Administrative Requirements

VERT expressly states that an electricity-generation permit is not required where a person intends to generate electricity only for its own needs and economic requirements, without supplying electricity to the electricity networks, using generation equipment with installed capacity not exceeding 30 kW.

  • Maximum installed generation capacity under the identified self-consumption exemption: 30 kW.
  • Electricity must not be supplied to the public electricity networks under this exemption.
  • Electricity must be produced for the producer's own needs and economic requirements.
  • Electrical safety requirements remain applicable.
  • Construction and planning requirements may remain applicable.
  • Environmental requirements may apply depending on the site and technology.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep installed electrical generation capacity at or below 30 kW.
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use generated electricity exclusively for internal needs and economic requirements.
  • Prevent accidental connection or energisation of the public electricity network.
Maximum Electrical Power
Lithuania — maximum installed capacity under the specific off-grid self-consumption exemption:

30 kW

VERT expressly identifies installations with installed capacity not exceeding 30 kW that produce electricity solely for their own needs and economic requirements, without supplying electricity to the electricity networks, as installations for which the electricity generation permit is not required.

Therefore, the figure to use for the comparative database for this specific off-grid self-consumption configuration is 30 kW.
