Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Liechtenstein and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate as a completely isolated electrical system, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity network.

Liechtenstein regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution and electricity-market activities through its Electricity Market Act (Elektrizitätsmarktgesetz; EMG) and the legislation governing electrical installations.

The Liechtenstein regulatory framework also requires approval for the construction of electricity-generation installations under the applicable electricity-market legislation.

The legislation reviewed does not provide a general numerical kW/MW exemption threshold specifically for a completely isolated gravity-powered electricity generation installation.

Administrative Requirements

A completely off-grid configuration does not by itself establish an electricity-law exemption.

  • Approval requirements for the electricity generation installation must be assessed.
  • Electrical installation requirements apply.
  • Construction and building requirements may apply.
  • Fire-safety requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Planning and land-use requirements may apply.
  • Structural engineering requirements may apply.
  • Water-law requirements apply where the technology uses water resources.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of the public electricity network.
  • Obtain confirmation from the competent Liechtenstein authority before construction.

No numerical kW/MW exemption threshold specifically applicable to a completely isolated gravity-powered electricity-generation installation has been identified in the legislation reviewed.

The Liechtenstein electricity framework contains approval requirements for electricity-generation installations.

Existing Liechtenstein government material confirms that electricity-generation installations are regulated under the electricity-market and electrical-installation framework.

Therefore, no numerical maximum capacity should be entered for an unlicensed gravity-powered installation in Liechtenstein unless the competent authority confirms that the proposed installation falls within a specific statutory exemption.
