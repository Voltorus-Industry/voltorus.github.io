Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Principality of Monaco and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate as a completely isolated electrical system, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity network.

Monaco regulates electricity production and distribution through its national legislation and the concession framework governing the public electricity distribution service.

Monaco also has specific regulatory provisions for photovoltaic electricity generation and self-consumption.

The legislation reviewed does not establish a general numerical kW or MW exemption threshold specifically applicable to a gravity-powered electricity generation installation.

Consequently, the photovoltaic thresholds found in Monaco's legislation must not be transferred to a gravity-powered installation.

Administrative Requirements

Monaco has specific provisions concerning photovoltaic electricity production and self-consumption.

Those provisions do not establish a general authorisation-free capacity threshold for every electricity-generation technology.

  • Construction and urban-planning requirements must be assessed.
  • Electrical safety requirements apply.
  • Fire-safety requirements may apply.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Building-owner approval may be required.
  • Technical inspection requirements may apply.
  • The competent Monegasque authorities should classify a gravity-powered installation before construction.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use generated electricity exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of the public electricity network.
  • Verify the construction and urban-planning requirements before installation.
  • Obtain a written classification from the competent Monegasque authority for the proposed gravity-powered technology.
Maximum Electrical Power
Monaco — gravity-powered technology:

No specific numerical kW/MW threshold applicable to a completely isolated gravity-powered electricity generation installation has been identified in the legislation reviewed.

Monaco's current environmental legislation contains a photovoltaic support regime.

The current photovoltaic regime applies to installations with an installed unitary capacity of at least 3 kWc for the purpose of obtaining the photovoltaic production aid.

The 3 kWc figure is therefore a minimum eligibility threshold for a photovoltaic support mechanism.

It is not a maximum capacity for electricity production and it is not a statutory threshold applicable to gravity-powered electricity generation.

The proposed gravity-powered installation should therefore be submitted to the competent Monegasque authority for classification before a numerical generation threshold is entered into the comparative database.
