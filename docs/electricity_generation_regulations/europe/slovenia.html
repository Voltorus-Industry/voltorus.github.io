Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Republic of Slovenia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Slovenian energy legislation regulates electricity generation and establishes an energy-permit regime for certain electricity-generation facilities.

The principal national threshold identified for an electricity-generation energy permit is a nominal electrical power greater than 1 MW for facilities connected to the public electricity network.

Administrative Requirements

Slovenian legislation requires an energy permit for specified electricity-generation facilities.

Energy Permit Threshold

The documented threshold is:

Electricity-generation facilities with nominal electrical power greater than 1 MW that are connected to the public electricity network.

Consequently, the threshold separating the identified energy-permit category is 1 MW.

Because the proposed installation is completely isolated, the applicability of the energy permit must be checked against the current Slovenian legislation and the precise project configuration.
