Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Italy and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island with no connection to the public electricity network.

Italian electricity-production authorisation depends strongly on the type of energy source, the technology, the installed capacity and the applicable regional administrative procedure.

Italian legislation contains simplified procedures for electricity-production installations using renewable energy sources.

A single nationwide capacity threshold specifically applicable to an isolated gravity-powered installation has not been identified in the current sources reviewed.

Administrative Requirements

The Italian authorisation procedure depends on the legal classification of the production installation.

  • Authorisation for electricity production where applicable.
  • Simplified authorisation procedures for qualifying renewable-energy installations.
  • Regional and municipal planning requirements.
  • Environmental assessment where applicable.
  • Electrical safety requirements.
  • Structural and fire-safety requirements.
  • Water-law requirements where applicable.
Maximum Electrical Power
Italy — gravity-powered technology:

No single nationwide numerical maximum has been identified that can legally be assigned specifically to an isolated gravity-powered electricity-generation installation.

Italian legislation contains capacity thresholds for particular authorisation procedures and particular renewable technologies.

For example, current administrative procedures distinguish installations above and below specified capacity levels.

Those technology-specific thresholds must not be transferred to a gravity-powered installation unless the Italian authorities classify the gravity system within the corresponding statutory technology category.
