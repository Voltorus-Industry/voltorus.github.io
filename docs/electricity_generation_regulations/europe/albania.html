Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Albania and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate completely independently from the Albanian public electricity grid and is not intended to import electricity from or export electricity to the public electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively to satisfy internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements within the installation or under the producer's control.

The Albanian electricity-sector framework regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution, supply and participation in the electricity market. The Energy Regulator Authority (ERE) is responsible for the regulation and licensing of activities within the electricity sector.

Albania's current primary electricity-sector legislation includes Law No. 59/2026 on the Electricity Sector . ERE's current legislation page also identifies Law No. 24/2023 on the promotion of the use of energy from renewable sources.

For a completely isolated installation used only for internal self-consumption, We should distinguish electricity-market licensing requirements from construction, environmental, land-use, electrical safety, water-use and other authorisations that may apply to the physical installation.

Administrative Requirements

We should not interpret the absence of a public-grid connection as an exemption from all Albanian administrative requirements.

The current Albanian electricity framework provides for electricity-generation licensing and other regulated electricity-sector activities. Whether a particular completely isolated self-consumption installation falls within a licensing requirement must be determined from the applicable provisions and the actual characteristics of the project.

Electricity-Sector Assessment
  • Electricity generation for the electricity market is subject to the Albanian electricity regulatory framework.
  • Electricity transmission and distribution are regulated activities.
  • Electricity supply and electricity trading are regulated activities.
  • Participation in the electricity market creates additional regulatory obligations.
  • A completely isolated self-consumption installation must be assessed separately from a grid-connected electricity generation facility.
  • We should obtain confirmation from the competent Albanian authority before treating a large installation as exempt from electricity-generation licensing.
Other Potential Administrative Requirements
  • Construction and building authorisations, where applicable.
  • Land-use and planning requirements.
  • Structural engineering requirements.
  • Electrical installation and safety requirements.
  • Fire-safety requirements.
  • Occupational health and safety requirements.
  • Environmental authorisations or environmental assessment, where applicable.
  • Water-use authorisations if the technology uses water resources.
  • Other permits or approvals depending on the location, technology and physical characteristics of the installation.
Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

To maintain the project as a genuine private off-grid self-consumption installation, We recommend that the project be designed and operated so that:

  • The installation remains physically isolated from the Albanian public electricity network.
  • No electrical connection is established with the public transmission or distribution network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No electricity-market participation is undertaken.
  • No electricity trading activity is undertaken.
  • No electricity supply activity is undertaken for third parties.
  • No electricity distribution network is operated for unrelated third parties.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.
  • The installation operates permanently in island mode.
  • The electrical design prevents unintended energisation or export towards the public grid.
  • Technical documentation demonstrates the independent and isolated nature of the facility.
  • We maintain documentation showing that the facility does not participate in the Albanian electricity market.
Maximum Electrical Power
Identified regulatory capacity: 5 MW

We have identified an Albanian ERE generation-licence category covering electricity generation facilities with a general installed capacity under 5 MW.

ERE's published generation-licence documentation expressly describes a licence for electricity generation from power plants with a general capacity under 5 MW.

This 5 MW threshold should, however, be understood as a regulatory licensing category and not automatically as a general maximum electrical capacity applicable to every completely isolated self-consumption facility.

The fact that the proposed facility uses a gravity-powered low-carbon technology does not by itself establish a separate Albanian statutory capacity limit specifically applicable to this technology.

For a completely off-grid installation, We should therefore determine the applicable licensing treatment according to the actual operating configuration, including:

  • whether the installation is physically connected to the public electricity network;
  • whether electricity is exported to the public network;
  • whether electricity is supplied or sold to third parties;
  • whether the installation participates in the electricity market;
  • the installed electrical generation capacity;
  • the technology and characteristics of the generating equipment; and
  • the applicable construction, environmental, land-use, water and safety requirements.
Important: The identified 5 MW figure is the capacity associated with the published ERE generation-licence category under 5 MW. It should not be represented as a universal statutory maximum for every off-grid self-consumption installation without confirmation under the current Law No. 59/2026 and its implementing regulations.
