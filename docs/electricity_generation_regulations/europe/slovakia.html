Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Slovak Republic and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Slovak electricity activities are regulated principally by Act No. 251/2012 Coll. on Energy.

The Slovak Energy Act expressly establishes a 1 MW threshold for the authorization requirement applicable to certain electricity-production and electricity-storage activities.

Administrative Requirements

The Slovak Energy Act establishes an authorization regime for specified electricity-sector activities.

Electricity Production Authorization

Under Act No. 251/2012 Coll., authorization is not required for:

Production or storage and supply of electricity by electricity-generation or electricity-storage facilities with a total installed capacity of up to and including 1 MW.

This is a direct statutory capacity threshold and is therefore highly relevant to the proposed installation.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain a total installed electrical capacity not exceeding 1 MW where relying on the statutory authorization exemption.
  • No physical connection to the Slovak public electricity network.
  • No electricity import.
  • No electricity export.
  • No electricity sale to third parties.
  • No electricity trading.
  • Internal self-consumption only.
  • Independent island operation.
  • Electrical protection against accidental grid energisation.
