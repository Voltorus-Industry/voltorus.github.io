Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Kosovo and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the transmission and distribution systems.

Kosovo's electricity licensing regime is administered by the Energy Regulatory Office (ERO).

Article 28 of Law No. 03/L-185 on the Energy Regulator identifies activities that do not require an energy licence.

The licensing rules expressly exempt electricity generation for self-consumption where neither the generation facility nor the electricity consumers are connected to the transmission or distribution system.

The same framework also identifies a general no-licence capacity threshold of 5 MW for electricity generation.

Administrative Requirements

Article 28 identifies electricity-generation activities that do not require an energy licence.

  • Electricity generation at an electricity site with total capacity not exceeding 5 MW.
  • Electricity generation for self-consumption where neither the generation facility nor the consumers are connected to the transmission or distribution system.
  • Building and planning requirements remain applicable where relevant.
  • Environmental requirements may apply.
  • Electrical and occupational safety requirements remain applicable.
Maximum Electrical Power
Kosovo — general electricity-generation no-licence threshold:

5 MW

Article 28 identifies electricity generation at an electricity site with total capacity not exceeding 5 MW as an activity not requiring an energy licence.

The same Article separately exempts electricity generation for self-consumption when neither the generation facility nor its consumers are connected to the transmission or distribution system.

For the comparative database, the explicit numerical threshold is therefore 5 MW , while the completely isolated self-consumption configuration benefits from the separate statutory exemption.
