Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in the Vatican City State and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, institutional, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Vatican City State has its own sovereign legal and administrative framework. Electricity infrastructure and renewable-energy projects are administered through the competent institutions of the Vatican City State, including the Governorate of Vatican City State.

The Vatican City State is actively developing renewable electricity-generation infrastructure. The Governorate has officially implemented photovoltaic installations and is pursuing a larger renewable-energy project intended to contribute to the complete energy requirements of Vatican City State.

The Vatican's official renewable-energy programme includes the development of an agrivoltaic installation at Santa Maria di Galeria, an extraterritorial area belonging to the Holy See outside the Vatican City territory.

A completely isolated self-consumption installation must therefore be assessed according to the applicable Vatican legal, administrative, construction, safety, environmental and technical requirements. The absence of a public-grid connection does not by itself establish an exemption from those requirements.

Administrative Requirements

An isolated electricity-generation installation should not be considered automatically exempt from administrative requirements merely because it does not exchange electricity with the public grid.

Vatican City State has a distinct sovereign legal and administrative system. Electricity-generation projects are therefore to be assessed under the applicable Vatican authorities and instruments rather than automatically applying the electricity-generation licensing thresholds of Italy.

Vatican City State Electricity Framework

The Vatican City State administers its own infrastructure and public services through the Governorate of Vatican City State.

The Governorate's Directorate for Infrastructure and Services is responsible for infrastructure, electrical systems and related technical activities within the State.

Official Vatican sources confirm that the Governorate is actively developing renewable electricity-generation systems as part of its energy-efficiency and environmental programme.

The available public sources do not identify a general Vatican-wide electricity-generation exemption expressed as a fixed kW threshold specifically for an isolated gravity-powered electricity-generation installation.

Renewable Electricity Development

Vatican City State has already implemented photovoltaic electricity-generation systems.

The Governorate reported that two photovoltaic systems associated with the Vatican Museums and the Vignaccia warehouse were expected to provide a combined photovoltaic peak power of 350 kWp .

The same official source reported an expected combined electricity production of approximately 500 MWh per year .

This 350 kWp figure is an officially documented installed/reference generation capacity. It is not identified by the Vatican sources as a legal maximum capacity for all electricity-generation technologies.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, we recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid for operation of the isolated system.
  • No electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No participation in electricity balancing or aggregation markets is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal self-consumption under the control of the producer.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of an external electricity network.
  • The installation is documented as a private or institutional self-consumption facility.
  • The competent Vatican authority is consulted before construction.
  • The mechanical, structural and electrical safety of the gravity-generation system is independently verified.
  • Construction, fire-safety, environmental and heritage requirements are independently verified.

Reference Capacity for the Project

For the purposes of this comparative project database, the relevant documented Vatican renewable-generation reference capacity is:

350 kWp

This figure corresponds to an officially documented renewable electricity-generation installation in Vatican City State.

It should not be described as a statutory exemption threshold for a gravity-powered installation. No publicly identified Vatican provision establishes 350 kW as a technology- specific legal ceiling for gravity-powered electricity generation.

Application to Gravity-Powered Generation

The proposed technology is a gravity-powered electricity-generation system rather than a photovoltaic system.

Consequently, the photovoltaic capacity of 350 kWp cannot automatically be treated as a technology-specific legal limit for gravity generation.

The project should therefore be submitted to the competent Vatican authority with the exact installed electrical capacity, mechanical configuration, operating mode, protection system and site characteristics.

Until a specific Vatican authorization or regulation establishes another capacity threshold, the safest factual classification is to use 350 kWp as the documented Vatican renewable-generation reference capacity rather than presenting it as a statutory exemption.
