Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Austria and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Austrian electricity legislation regulates the generation, transmission, distribution, storage, aggregation and supply of electricity.

Since electricity-law implementation is divided between federal and provincial legislation, the administrative requirements for a generation installation depend in particular on the Austrian Bundesland in which the facility is located.

The federal Elektrizitätswirtschaftsgesetz (ElWG) provides the national framework for the electricity sector. Provincial electricity laws establish important requirements concerning the construction and operation of electricity-generation installations.

A completely isolated self-consumption installation must therefore be assessed separately from a grid-connected generation facility, while remaining subject to applicable construction, safety, environmental, planning and provincial electricity requirements.

Administrative Requirements

We should not treat an off-grid installation as automatically exempt from Austrian administrative requirements.

The Austrian electricity framework is divided between federal legislation and the electricity laws of the individual Bundesländer.

Federal Electricity Framework

The current federal Elektrizitätswirtschaftsgesetz (ElWG) regulates electricity generation, transmission, distribution, storage, aggregation and supply.

The federal legislation also establishes obligations concerning electricity-generation installations and their interaction with electricity networks.

For example, the current federal framework contains provisions concerning new electricity generation installations and technical controllability requirements for certain installations.

These federal provisions do not create a single nationwide exemption threshold for every isolated self-consumption facility.

Provincial Electricity Law

The applicable Austrian Bundesland must be identified before determining the precise electricity-law approval requirement.

Provincial electricity legislation can establish specific capacity thresholds for electricity-generation installations.

As an example, the current Upper Austrian electricity law exempts certain electricity-generation installations from electricity-law approval up to specified capacity thresholds.

In Upper Austria, "other electricity-generation installations" with an installed bottleneck capacity of up to 5 kW are listed among installations not requiring the electricity-law approval referred to in § 6(1).

This 5 kW threshold is a Upper Austrian provincial threshold and must not be presented as a nationwide Austrian limit.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, We recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Austrian public electricity network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No participation in electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No participation in electricity balancing or aggregation markets is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installation is documented as a private self-consumption facility.
  • The applicable electricity law of the specific Bundesland is verified before construction.
  • The project's construction, environmental, planning, water and safety requirements are independently verified.
Maximum Electrical Power
Austria-wide maximum: No single nationwide kW/MW threshold has been identified for a purely off-grid, self-consumption-only electricity-generation installation without the applicable electricity-law authorization.

Austrian electricity-law regulation is divided between federal legislation and the legislation of the nine Bundesländer.

Consequently, the exact capacity that can be operated without an electricity-law approval cannot be stated as one figure for the whole of Austria.

The applicable capacity threshold must be determined from the electricity law of the Bundesland in which the facility will be constructed.

Example: Upper Austria

Under the current Upper Austrian electricity law, certain electricity-generation installations are exempt from the electricity-law approval requirement.

For "other electricity-generation installations", the exemption identified in § 6(2) covers installations with an installed bottleneck capacity of up to 5 kW .

This figure applies to the specific Upper Austrian regulatory category and is not an Austria-wide maximum.

The maximum capacity for a gravity-powered electricity-generation facility without the applicable electricity-law authorization must be determined at Bundesland level.

Example identified: Upper Austria — 5 kW for the specified category of "other electricity-generation installations" exempt from the electricity-law approval requirement.

We should therefore not enter "5 kW" as the Austrian national figure in the comparative table. We should instead identify the relevant Bundesland and apply that Bundesland's current electricity-law threshold.
