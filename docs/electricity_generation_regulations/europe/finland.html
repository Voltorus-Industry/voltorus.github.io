Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Finland and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The intended installation is designed to operate independently from the public electricity network, without importing electricity from or exporting electricity to the public electricity grid.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate electricity requirements under the control of the producer.

Finnish electricity generation is principally regulated by the Electricity Market Act (Sähkömarkkinalaki 588/2013) together with regulations and technical requirements adopted under Finnish electricity legislation.

The Finnish Energy Authority (Energiavirasto) is the principal national authority responsible for electricity-market regulation and supervision.

Finnish legislation distinguishes small-scale electricity production from larger electricity-generation installations.

The statutory small-scale electricity-production category covers generating installations with electrical capacity below 2 MVA .

Finnish energy-sector guidance confirms that installations below 2 MVA are treated as small-scale electricity production, while larger installations are substantially subject to the rules applicable to industrial-scale electricity production.

A separate category of microgeneration is generally considered to cover installations of up to 100 kVA , particularly where the installation is connected to a low-voltage network at the consumption site.

For the comparative classification requested here, the principal Finnish capacity threshold is therefore 2 MVA for small-scale electricity production. The completely isolated configuration must nevertheless be assessed separately because the statutory 2 MVA classification is principally a production-size classification rather than a general licence-free limit for every possible private electricity installation.

Administrative Requirements

We should not treat a completely off-grid installation as automatically exempt from all Finnish administrative requirements.

Electricity-production classification must be considered separately from construction, planning, environmental, electrical-safety, fire-safety and other technical requirements.

Small-Scale Electricity Production

Finnish legislation defines small-scale electricity production by reference to the capacity of the generating installation.

The statutory small-scale electricity production category covers installations below 2 MVA .

Finnish energy-sector guidance confirms that electricity-production installations below 2 MVA are considered small-scale production.

Accordingly, 2 MVA is the principal Finnish capacity threshold to use for the small-scale production classification.

Microgeneration

Finnish electricity-sector guidance generally treats electricity-production installations up to 100 kVA as microgeneration.

Microgeneration normally refers to a production installation connected at the consumption site whose primary purpose is to supply the consumption site.

This classification is particularly relevant to small household and commercial installations.

The proposed installation is different in that it is intended to be completely isolated from the public electricity network.

Completely Off-Grid Generation

The proposed installation has no physical connection to the Finnish transmission or distribution network.

Electricity would be generated and consumed entirely within the private electrical installation controlled by the producer.

No electricity would be injected into the public electricity network.

No electricity would be purchased from the public electricity network.

The final legal classification should nevertheless be confirmed against the Electricity Market Act and applicable construction, environmental and technical legislation before construction.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk

For a project intended to remain a genuinely isolated self-consumption installation, We recommend that:

  • The electricity-generation installation has no physical connection to the Finnish transmission or distribution network.
  • No electricity is imported from the public grid.
  • No electricity is exported to the public grid.
  • No electricity is sold to third parties.
  • No electricity is purchased from the public grid.
  • No electricity trading is undertaken.
  • No electricity aggregation or balancing-market participation is undertaken.
  • Electricity is used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate self-consumption.
  • The installation operates independently in island mode.
  • Electrical protection prevents accidental connection to or energisation of the public grid.
  • The installed electrical capacity is documented and verified by a qualified electrical engineer.
  • The 2 MVA small-scale production threshold is verified against the current Finnish framework.
  • The 100 kVA microgeneration classification is considered where relevant.
  • Construction, environmental, planning, water, fire-safety and electrical requirements are independently verified.

Small-Scale Production

The Finnish small-scale production category extends to installations below 2 MVA .

This category includes different types of electricity production, including solar, wind, small hydro, small CHP and biogas installations.

A gravity-powered electricity-generation installation would be assessed according to its actual electrical generating capacity and technical configuration rather than according to the energy source alone.

Microgeneration Reference Point

Finnish energy-sector guidance generally uses 100 kVA as the threshold for microgeneration.

Microgeneration is generally associated with an installation at the electricity-consumption site whose primary purpose is to supply that consumption site.

The proposed completely isolated installation should therefore not automatically be classified as grid-connected microgeneration.

Principal production-size threshold: 2 MVA

Finland classifies electricity-production installations below 2 MVA as small-scale electricity production.

A secondary reference point is 100 kVA for the generally recognised microgeneration category.

The 2 MVA figure should be presented as the small-scale production threshold and not as a universal construction or operating licence ceiling for every isolated private installation.

For the comparative database, the appropriate primary Finnish figure is therefore 2 MVA as the statutory small-scale electricity-production threshold. The project remains subject to verification of construction, environmental, electrical-safety, taxation and other requirements applicable to the actual installation.
