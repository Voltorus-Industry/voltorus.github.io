Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity generation facility located in Ukraine and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network.

No electricity would be imported from or exported to the public electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial or corporate requirements.

Ukrainian electricity-generation licensing rules establish a specific installed-capacity threshold for electricity-generation activities.

The licensing analysis is particularly relevant to the intended self-consumption configuration because the project does not involve electricity sales to third parties.

Administrative Requirements

Ukrainian legislation establishes a licensing regime for electricity-generation activities.

The applicable licensing conditions distinguish generation according to installed generating capacity and the intended commercial treatment of the electricity.

Electricity Generation Licence

Under the applicable licensing conditions, electricity-generation activity is subject to licensing where the installed capacity of the electricity-generating equipment reaches the specified licensing threshold.

The Ukrainian regulator identifies the relevant threshold as 5 MW of installed generating capacity.

Producers below this threshold that do not undertake the specified regulated sales activity can operate without a generation licence under the applicable conditions.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Keep the installation physically isolated from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public grid.
  • Do not export electricity to the public grid.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not participate in electricity trading.
  • Keep installed generating capacity below the applicable 5 MW licensing threshold where the licence-free regime is intended.
  • Verify that no special licensing condition applicable to the chosen technology is triggered.
  • Verify all construction, environmental and safety requirements before construction.
Maximum Electrical Power
Ukraine — maximum installed capacity for the licence-free generation regime:

5 MW

The Ukrainian energy regulator states that electricity-generation activity is subject to licensing when the installed capacity of the electricity-generating equipment is 5 MW or more, subject to the applicable licensing conditions.

Where the installed capacity does not exceed 5 MW and the producer does not undertake the specified regulated sale of alternative electricity to the guaranteed buyer under the green-tariff mechanism, the generation activity is not subject to the generation licence requirement described by the regulator.

Website classification for Ukraine:

5 MW installed generating capacity

This is the key national licensing threshold applicable to the proposed self-consumption configuration, subject to verification of the technology and final operating model.

A project designed specifically to remain within the licence-free regime should therefore be dimensioned below the 5 MW threshold rather than at or above it.
