Overview

We are considering a private electricity-generation facility located in Palau and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The facility is intended to operate independently from the central electricity grid operated by the Palau Public Utilities Corporation (PPUC).

No electricity would be imported from or exported to the public electricity network.

The principal legal framework includes Title 37 of the Palau National Code and the Palau Energy Act.

The Palau Energy Act establishes the Energy Administration and a regulatory framework for the national energy sector.

PPUC is responsible for electricity operations of the Republic, including generation, transmission and distribution.

The 2024 renewable-energy net-metering regulations expressly concern facilities connected to the PPUC network. They state that facilities above 500 kW AC and utility-scale independent power producers are subject to a different process. This 500 kW value is therefore not a general statutory maximum for an isolated private self-consumption facility.

  • Verify environmental requirements.

Measures to Reduce Regulatory Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the PPUC central grid.
  • Do not participate in net metering.
  • Do not export electricity to PPUC.
  • Do not purchase electricity from PPUC.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use the electricity exclusively for internal self-consumption.
  • Install protection against accidental connection to the PPUC network.
  • Document the generation capacity and electrical architecture.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the competent Palauan authorities before construction.

The 2024 renewable-energy net-metering regulations provide a 500 kW AC threshold for the particular net-metering framework.

The same regulations state that utility-scale independent power producers and facilities above 500 kW AC must use another process with PPUC.

Because the proposed project is completely isolated from the PPUC network, the 500 kW AC net-metering threshold should not be treated as a general statutory maximum for the project.

The permissible capacity must instead be determined from the applicable Palau laws, regulations, environmental requirements, electrical standards and site-specific approvals.

Public Information Notice

Palau has a statutory energy framework centred on Title 37 of the Palau National Code and the Palau Energy Act. PPUC operates the national electricity system and the Palau Energy Administration performs energy regulatory functions. The 500 kW AC threshold appearing in the 2024 net-metering regulations relates to renewable facilities using the regulated grid-connected net-metering framework. It should not be interpreted as a general maximum capacity for a completely isolated private self-consumption facility. The project must nevertheless be reviewed against all applicable electricity, environmental, construction and safety requirements.
