Overview

Electricity generation in Vanuatu is principally governed by the Electricity Supply Act [Cap. 65] together with the Utilities Regulatory Authority Act No. 11 of 2007, as amended.

The Electricity Supply Act provides the statutory framework for electricity generation and transmission and establishes the basis for electricity concessions and supply arrangements.

The Utilities Regulatory Authority is responsible for regulation of the electricity sector and for promoting safe, reliable and affordable regulated services.

The proposed installation is assumed to operate as a completely isolated electrical system with no physical connection to a public electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively by the operator for internal household, industrial, scientific, research-station or corporate requirements.

The absence of grid export or electricity sales does not by itself establish that the installation is outside the electricity regulatory framework. The applicable authorization and technical requirements must therefore be confirmed with the Utilities Regulatory Authority and the competent governmental authority.

Administrative Requirements

The Utilities Regulatory Authority Act establishes the Utilities Regulatory Authority as an independent regulatory body for regulated utility services, including electricity.

The Electricity Supply Act establishes the legal framework for electricity generation and transmission and provides for concession arrangements concerning electricity supply.

Electricity Generation

Electricity generation is regulated under the Electricity Supply Act and the regulatory framework administered by the Utilities Regulatory Authority.

The precise legal requirements for an isolated self-consumption generator depend on whether the proposed activity falls within the regulated electricity supply and generation framework.

A written determination from the URA should be obtained for a private off-grid generation facility before construction or commissioning.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Prevent accidental energisation of an external network.
  • Do not import electricity from a public network.
  • Do not export electricity to a public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Clearly document the installation as an isolated self-consumption generation facility.
  • Obtain written confirmation from the URA concerning the licensing and authorization status.
  • Verify all electrical safety standards before commissioning.
  • Document nominal, maximum and operating electrical capacity.
  • Complete any environmental, construction and land-use approvals applicable to the site.
Maximum Electrical Power
Vanuatu — statutory capacity threshold identified:

No specific MW threshold for gravity-powered generation identified in the legislation reviewed.

The electricity legislation reviewed establishes a regulatory and concession framework but does not identify a specific gravity-generation capacity threshold.

Consequently, the project's maximum electrical generating capacity should be established from the engineering design and assessed under the applicable electricity regulatory framework.

The absence of a specific MW threshold should not be interpreted as an exemption from electrical, environmental, construction or regulatory requirements.

Public Information Notice

Electricity generation in Vanuatu is governed principally by the Electricity Supply Act and the Utilities Regulatory Authority framework. The Electricity Supply Act provides the legal foundation for generation and transmission and for electricity-supply concession arrangements. Electrical safety is also subject to applicable standards and rules. No specific MW threshold dedicated to gravity-powered generation has been identified in the legislation reviewed. A private, isolated self-consumption facility should therefore be submitted to the competent Vanuatu authorities, particularly the Utilities Regulatory Authority, for confirmation of the applicable authorization and technical requirements.
