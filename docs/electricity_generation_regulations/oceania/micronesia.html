Overview

We are considering a gravity-powered low-carbon electricity-generation facility located in the Federated States of Micronesia and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate as an independent electrical island without connection to a public electricity network.

No electricity would be imported from or exported to an external public electricity network.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, scientific, research, commercial or corporate requirements under the control of the operator.

The Federated States of Micronesia is a federal constitutional system in which electricity regulation is substantially implemented at the State level.

The national government has adopted national energy policy, while important electricity utility and generation legislation is found in the individual States of Pohnpei, Kosrae, Chuuk and Yap.

Consequently, the exact legal treatment of an off-grid generating installation must be determined according to the State in which the installation is located.

Administrative Requirements

Electricity regulation in the Federated States of Micronesia must be analyzed at both national and State levels.

The FSM Energy Policy provides national policy objectives concerning reliable, affordable and environmentally sound energy and encourages increased use of renewable energy.

Pohnpei

Pohnpei enacted legislation in 2012 to reform power generation and distribution.

The legislation amended provisions of the Pohnpei Code concerning power generation and distribution and provided for power purchase agreements between Pohnpei Utilities Corporation and large-scale generators.

A project located in Pohnpei must therefore be assessed against the applicable Pohnpei legislation and utility requirements.

Kosrae

Kosrae State Law 5-38 established the Kosrae Utilities Authority.

The Authority is vested with responsibilities to provide public electric power and to operate and manage the State's electric power system.

A private off-grid generating installation in Kosrae therefore requires assessment under the Kosrae State legal framework, including utility, construction and safety requirements.

Chuuk and Yap

Chuuk has the Chuuk Public Utility Corporation Act of 1996 establishing the public utility corporation and defining its powers and functions.

Yap has a State public service corporation framework governing public electricity services.

The State-specific legal framework must be verified before construction of a private generating facility.

Measures to Reduce Regulatory Risk
  • Identify the specific FSM State before applying the electricity legislation.
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from public electricity networks.
  • Do not import electricity.
  • Do not export electricity.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Do not participate in electricity trading.
  • Verify the State utility legislation.
  • Verify utility connection and supply restrictions.
  • Verify construction and land-use approvals.
  • Verify environmental impact assessment requirements.
  • Verify electrical safety and fire-safety requirements.
  • Install protection preventing accidental connection to an external electrical system.
Maximum Electrical Power
Federated States of Micronesia — national statutory capacity threshold:

No single national MW threshold identified.

The available legal framework is not based on a single national electricity-generation licensing threshold applicable to every generating facility throughout the Federation.

Electricity legislation and utility arrangements are substantially State-specific.

Therefore, a single national figure such as a particular MW threshold should not be presented as the maximum permissible capacity for an isolated generating facility in the Federated States of Micronesia.

Public Information Notice

The Federated States of Micronesia does not operate under a single national electricity-generation licensing framework applicable in identical form throughout the Federation. Important electricity regulation is implemented at the State level, including Pohnpei, Kosrae, Chuuk and Yap. The FSM Energy Policy provides national energy-policy objectives, while State legislation establishes important utility and electricity frameworks. No single national MW capacity threshold for isolated self-consumption generation has been identified. The precise legal treatment therefore depends on the State, location, technology, electrical configuration, environmental impact and applicable State utility law.
