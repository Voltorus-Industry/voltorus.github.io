Overview

We are considering a private electricity-generation facility located in Nauru and operated exclusively for internal self-consumption.

The installation is intended to operate independently from the public electricity network, with no intended import of electricity from or export of electricity to the public network.

Electricity would be used exclusively for internal household, industrial, scientific, commercial, corporate or other private requirements under the control of the operator.

The principal electricity legislation identified for Nauru is the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 (Act No. 13 of 2011).

Section 8 of the Act defines the electricity functions of the Nauru Utilities Corporation, including the generation, acquisition, exchange, transport, distribution, marketing and supply of electricity.

The Act reviewed does not establish a general electricity-generation licence regime for private self-consumption installations comparable to the licensing systems identified in several other Pacific jurisdictions. This should not be interpreted as a blanket exemption from other applicable laws, safety requirements, land requirements or approvals.

Administrative Requirements

Section 8 of the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011 assigns electricity-generation and electricity-supply functions to the Nauru Utilities Corporation.

Section 26 provides that Cabinet may prescribe safety standards for the exercise of the Corporation's powers and functions.

The Act also permits regulations to be made under section 27.

  • Verification of the applicable Nauru Utilities Corporation requirements.
  • Verification of electrical safety requirements.
  • Verification of construction and land requirements.
  • Verification of environmental requirements.
  • Verification of fire-safety requirements.
  • Verification of occupational safety requirements.
  • Verification of any Cabinet regulations applicable to the installation.
  • Verification of any requirements imposed by other Nauruan legislation.
Measures to Reduce Regulatory Risk
  • Maintain complete physical isolation from the public electricity network.
  • Do not import electricity from the public network.
  • Do not export electricity to the public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use the installation exclusively for internal consumption.
  • Install electrical protection preventing accidental energisation of an external network.
  • Document the nominal electrical capacity.
  • Verify requirements directly with the competent Nauruan authorities before construction.
  • Verify all applicable electrical, structural, environmental and safety requirements.

Public Information Notice

Nauru's electricity framework is principally based on the Nauru Utilities Corporation Act 2011. The Act assigns extensive electricity functions to the Nauru Utilities Corporation and provides for safety standards and regulations. No general private electricity generation capacity threshold specifically applicable to an isolated self-consumption installation was identified in the Act reviewed. The absence of such a threshold must not be interpreted as confirmation that every project is automatically exempt from other approvals. The exact project, site, technology, electrical installation and applicable legislation must be verified before construction or operation.
