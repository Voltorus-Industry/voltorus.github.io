Overview

Electricity generation in Tuvalu is principally governed by the Tuvalu Electricity Corporation Act and the applicable Electricity Regulations.

The Tuvalu Electricity Corporation (TEC) was established and incorporated under the legislation governing the electricity sector and is responsible for the operation and supply of electricity in Tuvalu.

The legislation gives TEC an important statutory role in electricity generation and supply and provides for exclusive electricity-supply rights within declared electricity supply areas.

The proposed installation is assumed to operate as an independent electrical island for internal self-consumption, without import from or export to an external public network.

Because the legislation gives TEC a central and potentially exclusive role in electricity supply, the legal treatment of an independent private generation installation should be confirmed with the competent Tuvalu authorities before construction.

Administrative Requirements

The Tuvalu Electricity Corporation Act establishes the statutory framework for the Tuvalu Electricity Corporation.

TEC is responsible for the electricity supply system and has statutory powers and functions concerning electricity generation and supply.

The legislation provides TEC with the sole and exclusive right to supply electricity for sale within an electricity supply area declared by the Minister.

Private Self-Generation

The legislation reviewed does not establish a general nationwide MW exemption specifically for private off-grid generation.

The principal legal question for a private isolated installation is therefore whether the proposed activity constitutes electricity generation reserved to TEC or whether it falls outside the statutory electricity-supply monopoly because the electricity is used solely by the operator.

Written confirmation should be obtained from the relevant Tuvalu authority before construction.

Measures to Reduce Licensing Risk
  • Maintain complete electrical isolation from TEC's public electricity network.
  • Do not export electricity to a public network.
  • Do not sell electricity to third parties.
  • Use electricity solely for the operator's internal requirements.
  • Clearly document the installation as an isolated private self-consumption system.
  • Prevent accidental interconnection with the public electricity network.
  • Obtain written legal and administrative confirmation from Tuvalu authorities before construction.
  • Document the nominal and maximum electrical generating capacity.
Maximum Electrical Power

No specific MW threshold for gravity-powered generation identified.

The legislation reviewed does not establish a specific electrical-generation capacity threshold applicable to a gravity-powered installation.

The principal legal issue is instead the statutory role and supply rights of the Tuvalu Electricity Corporation.

The project's electrical capacity should therefore be established according to engineering design and submitted to the competent authorities for a determination of the applicable legal framework.
